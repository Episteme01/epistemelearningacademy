# Academic framework

## Structure and identifiers

`ELA-PS-<CLASS>-<SUBJECT>-T<term>-W<week>-D<day>`

| Part | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Class | TOD, N1, N2, REC |
| Subject | NUM, LIT, SCI, SOC |
| Term | T1 to T3 |
| Week | W01 to W10 |
| Day | D01 to D05 |

Programme ID: `ELA-PS-TOD-NUM`. Week ID: `ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T1-W01`. IDs never change once published; retired lessons are set to `archived`.

## Weekly rhythm

Each week has one idea. Typical day types: Day 1 introduce or explore, Day 2 explore or practise, Day 3 practise, Day 4 apply (story, song, outdoor or role play), Day 5 review and assess. Weeks are typed `new-learning`, `consolidation` or `revision-assessment`; Week 10 of every term is revision and assessment.

## Lesson sections

Every lesson contains: title, class, subject, term, week, day, duration, topic, subtopic, objectives, success criteria, previous knowledge, vocabulary, materials, starter, step-by-step presentation (teacher and children actions side by side), practical activity with safety, differentiation (support and extension), assessment questions with expected responses, expected outcomes, conclusion, home connection, teacher reflection, and a `learnerFacing` block for presentation tools.

## Number and language ranges (ELA design targets)

These are ELA planning targets, to be reviewed against the Nigerian and EYFS documents (see `STANDARDS_ALIGNMENT.md`). See also `preschool/progression-matrix.md`.

Configurable in `preschool/config/age-bands.json`. Defaults are unvalidated assumptions.
