# Teacher implementation guide

## Before the week

1. Read the week file (`week-NN.md`) and the five lessons. 2. Collect resources (Day 1 materials first). 3. Check safety lines. 4. Plan where the practical station will go.

## Each day

Starter (2 to 3 minutes) then 3 to 4 short steps with active tasks, then the practical activity and a short conclusion. Lessons are 20 minutes by default for Toddler; the `durationMin` field may differ for older classes.

## Routines

Use the same greeting, clean-up signal and "sit-down" song every day so children know what to expect.

## Using the lesson text

- "Teacher:" is what you say or do. "Children:" is what you expect them to do.
- Wait at least five seconds after a question.
- Say the key word clearly each time. Accept actions, sounds and the home language as answers.
- Expected responses show what a secure answer looks like. Do not require the exact wording.

## Misconceptions to watch (Toddler Numeracy)

- Saying number words quickly without touching each object (counting is not yet one-to-one).
- Thinking the biggest-looking pile has the most when objects are spread out.
- Mixing "heavy" with "big".

## Behaviour and engagement

Keep groups small, give every child a turn, change activity when attention drops, and use calm, positive language. Never shame or threaten a child.

## Reinforcement

Use the home connection, a song at tidy-up, and routines such as counting steps to the playground.
