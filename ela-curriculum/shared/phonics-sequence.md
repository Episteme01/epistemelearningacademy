# ELA phonics sequence (Toddler to Reception)

**Status:** draft, ELA design. It follows the widely used systematic synthetic phonics order (small sets of sounds, taught in a fixed sequence, with blending before segmenting). It is ELA's own planning sequence, not a copy of any commercial scheme, and has not been checked against the NERDC documents (see `SOURCE_REGISTER.md`). Terminology is used the same way everywhere: **sound** (phoneme), **letter** (grapheme), **blend**, **segment**, **digraph**.

## Principles
1. Listening before letters. Children first tune in to sounds around them, then rhyme, then syllables, then the first sound of a word, and only then link sounds to letters.
2. Sounds are said "pure" (for example /s/ not "suh"). Teachers practise this before teaching.
3. Letters are met in small sets. New letters are added only when the previous set is secure. Previous letters are revised every week.
4. Blending (reading) is taught before segmenting (spelling), then they are taught together.
5. Children read decodable words made only of sounds they have been taught, plus a few "tricky" words taught as whole words.
6. Handwriting follows gross motor and fine motor readiness. Speed is never a measure of literacy.

## Revision schedule
Every Friday lesson and every Week 10 revisits all sounds taught so far. New sounds are never added in a revision week.

## Blending and segmenting progression
1. Oral blending: teacher says /s/ /a/ /t/, children say "sat" (Nursery 1).
2. Reading CVC words by sounding and blending (Nursery 1 Term 3 onward).
3. Oral segmenting: children say a word and push a counter for each sound (Nursery 2).
4. Writing CVC words from sounds (Nursery 2).
5. Words with digraphs, then two-syllable words and simple sentences (Reception).

## Decodable vocabulary (examples for early sets)
Set 1: sat, pat, tap, nap, pin, tin, sip, pit, tip, nip, ant, at, it, in, is. Set 2 adds: mat, map, dog, dig, mop, cat, kit, sock. Tricky words (taught whole): I, the, to, no, go, my.

## Reading and writing milestones
| By the end of | Reading | Writing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toddler | Enjoys books, names pictures, joins rhymes, hears the first sound of a few words | Makes marks and large patterns |
| Nursery 1 | Knows Sets 1 to 3 sounds, blends oral and simple written CVC words | Forms the letters of own name, writes some letters |
| Nursery 2 | Reads CVC words and simple captions, knows Sets 4 and 5 and four digraphs | Writes CVC words and labels |
| Reception | Reads short decodable sentences and simple books, recognises common tricky words | Writes simple sentences from sounds |
