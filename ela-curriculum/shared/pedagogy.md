# Pedagogy

Blend play, guided play, short direct teaching, practical demonstrations, stories, songs, role play, manipulatives, outdoor learning, individual and small-group work.

Principles: start with real objects; keep teacher talk short; use local, low-cost resources; repeat key ideas on different days; give every child a turn; model language, then wait; let children show with actions; avoid worksheets and long sitting; end lessons calmly.
