# Safety guidelines

- Objects used with toddlers are larger than a child's fist; no small parts, beads, coins, bottle tops, whole nuts or sweets.
- Real food only when fresh, washed, allergy-checked and offered by the teacher.
- Water play is always supervised, with shallow containers; clean up spills at once.
- Children sit for handling activities; walk, never run, indoors.
- Check floors, walkways and outdoor areas before every practical activity.
- No chemicals, electrical experiments, flames or unsupervised activities.
- Wash hands after outdoor collecting, sand, soil, real food and toileting.
- Follow ELA's safeguarding policy and the national minimum standard for early child-care centres (title in `SOURCE_REGISTER.md`; to be reviewed).
