# Glossary (British English)

- **Cardinality:** knowing that the last number said tells how many.
- **One-to-one correspondence:** saying one number word for each object touched once.
- **Guided play:** play set up and supported by an adult towards a learning aim.
- **Observation-led assessment:** judging learning by watching and listening during normal activities.
- **Scope and sequence:** the order in which ideas are taught across the year.
- **Scheme of work:** a weekly plan for a term.
- **Success criteria:** what the teacher looks for to say a child has learned the idea.
- **Phonics:** teaching the link between sounds and letters (details in Literacy programmes).
- **Differentiation:** adjusting an activity so every child can take part and be stretched.
- **Learner-facing content:** content that is safe to show on a board or slide.
- **Practise (verb) / practice (noun):** British spelling is used throughout.
