## Limitations (read before relying on alignment claims)

1. The NERDC curriculum documents themselves could not be opened in the authoring environment. Titles were confirmed in the catalogue only.
2. The EYFS framework document on GOV.UK could not be opened. The seven areas listed in `STANDARDS_ALIGNMENT.md` follow the ELA brief.
3. Therefore the curriculum states **"mapped by subject area and developmental theme"**, not "aligned to numbered statements". ELA claims no government approval, accreditation or certification.
4. A named ELA reviewer should open each source above, tick the check column in `STANDARDS_ALIGNMENT.md` and record the date before any programme moves from `draft` to `approved`.
