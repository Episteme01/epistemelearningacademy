# Toddler Numeracy — Term 3, Week 2: Time

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T3-W02 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Morning and night; Afternoon; What comes first and next in our day

## Learning objectives
- Children will tell morning from night using pictures and routines.
- Children will say what happens first and next in the school day with support.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, first, next, sleep

**Content:** Telling morning, afternoon and night apart using familiar routines and pictures, and saying what happens first and next.

**Teaching activities:** Link each time word to a clear daily event; use picture cards in order; refer to the routine each day using the time words.

**Learner activities:** Sort day and night pictures, order the school day with picture cards, act out routines and sing a day song.

**Resources:** Picture cards: waking up, eating breakfast, school, lunch, nap, dinner, bedtime; A sun and moon picture; A soft toy; A drum

**Assessment:** Check whether children sort day and night pictures and name one thing that happens in the morning.

## Expected outcomes
- Sorts day and night pictures correctly.
- Names one activity that happens in the morning.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: sequencing and time words; Social Habits: routines and hygiene; Science: day and night

## Day 1 (Monday): Day and night

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T3-W02-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Morning and night

### Learning objectives
- Children will tell day pictures from night pictures using the sun and the moon.

### Success criteria
- Points to the sun for day and the moon for night
- Says morning or night with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children know the daily routine at school and can use first, next and words such as morning and sleep in simple talk.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, first, next, sleep

**Instructional materials:** A large sun picture and a large moon picture; Picture cards of day activities (playing outside, breakfast) and night activities (sleeping, stars); A mat

### Starter
Show the sun and say "When the sun is up, it is day." Show the moon and say "When the moon is up, it is night." Ask "What do we do at night?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Place the sun picture and the moon picture on the mat. Hold up one card and say "Breakfast. Is it day or night?" Children: Children point to the sun for day.

Step 2. Teacher: Hold up a card of a child sleeping and ask again. Children: Children point to the moon and say "night".

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child choose a card and place it under the sun or the moon. Children: Child places the card and says "day" or "night".

Step 4. Teacher: Act out waking up in the morning and going to sleep at night. Children: Children stretch for the morning and curl up for night.

### Practical activity: Sun and moon sort
1. Lay the sun and moon on the floor.
2. Children sort picture cards under the sun or the moon.
3. Check each pile together.

**Safety:** Use large cards with rounded corners; children walk to place cards and do not run.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two cards at a time, one clear day and one clear night.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to name something they do when the sun is up and something they do when the moon is up.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Is breakfast in the day or the night?
  **Expected response:** Day, in the morning.
- **Ask:** What do we do at night?
  **Expected response:** Sleep.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts day and night pictures
- Says morning or night with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye to the sun and moon. Say "Good morning, sun. Goodnight, moon."

### Home connection
Talk with your child about what happens in the morning and what happens at night.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "tell day pictures from night pictures using the sun and the moon" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- DAY (sun)
- NIGHT (moon)

**Picture suggestions:**
- The sun and children playing
- The moon and a sleeping child

**Song or rhyme:** Sun is up, wake up, wake up. Moon is up, sleep, sleep, sleep.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Morning, afternoon, night

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T3-W02-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Afternoon

### Learning objectives
- Children will place pictures in three groups: morning, afternoon and night.

### Success criteria
- Places a picture in the correct time group
- Says one of the three time words

**Previous knowledge:** Children know the daily routine at school and can use first, next and words such as morning and sleep in simple talk.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, first, next, sleep

**Instructional materials:** Three large paper circles labelled with a rising sun, a bright sun and a moon; Picture cards for breakfast, lunch and bedtime, plus playing and nap pictures; A mat

### Starter
Show three circles: a rising sun, a high sun and a moon. Say "Morning, afternoon and night. These are three parts of a day."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Hold up the breakfast card and say "Morning." Place it under the rising sun. Children: Children say "morning" and watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Hold up the lunch card and say "Afternoon." Place it under the bright sun. Children: Children say "afternoon".

Step 3. Teacher: Hold up the bedtime card and say "Night." Place it under the moon. Children: Children say "night".

Step 4. Teacher: Give each child a card and ask them to place it in a group. Children: Child chooses a group and places the card.

### Practical activity: Three part day
1. Lay out the three circles.
2. Each child sorts picture cards into the three groups.
3. Tell a friend what they did.

**Safety:** Use large cards; keep the sorting area clear of obstacles.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use morning and night only until the child is secure.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell what comes between morning and night.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Is lunch in the morning or the afternoon?
  **Expected response:** Afternoon.
- **Ask:** When do we sleep?
  **Expected response:** Night.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts routines into morning, afternoon and night
- Says time words

### Conclusion
Sing the day song and put the cards away.

### Home connection
Name the part of the day when you eat meals at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "place pictures in three groups: morning, afternoon and night" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Morning
- Afternoon
- Night

**Picture suggestions:**
- Three parts of the day

## Day 3 (Wednesday): First and next in our day

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T3-W02-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** What comes first and next in our day

### Learning objectives
- Children will order three picture cards of the school day and say first and next.

### Success criteria
- Puts two or three cards in order
- Says first or next with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children know the daily routine at school and can use first, next and words such as morning and sleep in simple talk.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, first, next, sleep

**Instructional materials:** Picture cards: arrive at school, play, wash hands, snack, home time; A string line on the floor; A drum

### Starter
Say "What do we do first at school? What do we do next?" Show the picture of arriving at school and say "First!"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Put the "arrive" card at the start of the string line. Say "First." Children: Children say "first".

Step 2. Teacher: Place the "play" card next and say "Next, we play." Then "wash hands" and "snack". Children: Children say "next" and point to each card.

Step 3. Teacher: Mix the cards and ask a child to put them in the right order. Children: Child places the cards from first to last.

Step 4. Teacher: Act out the routine: walk in, play, wash hands, eat, wave goodbye. Children: Children act each part in order.

### Practical activity: Our school day line
1. Lay a string line on the floor.
2. Place the picture cards in order.
3. Walk along the line saying first, next, last.

**Safety:** Fix the string with tape; children walk one at a time with an adult beside them.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use only three cards and place the first card for the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell the order without the cards.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do first at school?
  **Expected response:** Arrive or say good morning.
- **Ask:** What comes next after play?
  **Expected response:** Wash hands or snack.

### Expected outcomes
- Orders three school day pictures
- Uses first and next

### Conclusion
Walk the line one last time and wave goodbye at the end.

### Home connection
Talk about what you do first, next and last when getting ready for school.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "order three picture cards of the school day and say first and next" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- First, next, last

**Picture suggestions:**
- Picture cards of the school day

## Day 4 (Thursday): My day story

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T3-W02-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** What comes first and next in our day

### Learning objectives
- Children will listen to a story about a day and say what happens in the morning, afternoon and night.

### Success criteria
- Says one thing that happens in each part of the day
- Acts out the story

**Previous knowledge:** Children know the daily routine at school and can use first, next and words such as morning and sleep in simple talk.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, first, next, sleep

**Instructional materials:** A soft toy named Kemi; Picture cards of the story; A blanket

### Starter
Hold up the soft toy and say "This is Kemi. Let us hear about Kemi’s day. Listen for morning, afternoon and night."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Tell the first part and act it: wash face, eat breakfast. Place the sun card. Children: Children copy the actions and say "morning".

Step 2. Teacher: Tell the second part and act playing. Place the bright sun card. Children: Children play and say "afternoon".

Step 3. Teacher: Tell the last part and act brushing teeth and sleeping. Place the moon card. Children: Children pretend to brush teeth and say "night".

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "What did Kemi do first?" and "What did Kemi do at night?" Children: Children answer with a word or action.

### Practical activity: Act Kemi’s day
1. Give each child a toy or doll.
2. Act the day in order.
3. Put the toy to bed at the end.

**Safety:** Use soft toys; place a blanket on the floor; children move gently.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Act only the morning part and say the words together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell the story in their own words using the cards.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did Kemi do in the morning?
  **Expected response:** Washed and ate breakfast.
- **Ask:** When did Kemi go to bed?
  **Expected response:** At night.

### Expected outcomes
- Names events in morning, afternoon and night
- Acts out a daily routine

### Conclusion
Say "Goodnight, Kemi" together and tuck the toy under the blanket.

### Home connection
Tell your child a story about their own day at bedtime.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "listen to a story about a day and say what happens in the morning, afternoon and night" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Kemi: morning, afternoon, night

**Picture suggestions:**
- Kemi waking up
- Kemi at school
- Kemi going to bed

**Short story:** In the morning, Kemi woke up. She washed her face and ate her breakfast. In the afternoon, Kemi went to school and played with her friends. At night, Kemi had her supper. She brushed her teeth and said goodnight. Kemi closed her eyes. Goodnight, Kemi.

## Day 5 (Friday): Time of day stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T3-W02-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Morning and night

### Learning objectives
- Children will show they know day and night and the order of the day at three stations.

### Success criteria
- Sorts day and night pictures
- Puts routine cards in order with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children know the daily routine at school and can use first, next and words such as morning and sleep in simple talk.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, first, next, sleep

**Instructional materials:** Station 1: sun and moon sort; Station 2: three part day; Station 3: first and next line; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the day song and show the three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Rotate groups every four minutes and observe one station closely. Children: Children play at each station.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "Is this day or night?" and "What comes next?" Children: Child answers and places cards.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Station circuit
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. Teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Keep cards large; supervise stations; keep walkways clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to explain the order of the day.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: sorts day and night; names morning events; orders routine pictures.

- **Ask:** Is it day or night when we sleep?
  **Expected response:** Night.
- **Ask:** What happens in the morning?
  **Expected response:** Waking up, washing or breakfast.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 2
- Understands parts of the day with support

### Conclusion
Sing the day song and tidy up together.

### Home connection
Use morning, afternoon and night words in talk at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "show they know day and night and the order of the day at three stations" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Morning, afternoon, night

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of stations
