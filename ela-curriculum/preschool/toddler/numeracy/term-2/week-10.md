# Toddler Numeracy — Term 2, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T2-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising numerals and counting; Revising comparing and patterns; Revising shapes

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, long, short, heavy, light, pattern, square, triangle

**Content:** Revising Term 2 learning through play stations and gathering evidence for the Term 2 record.

**Teaching activities:** Keep stations relaxed; observe quietly; give help only when asked; praise effort and kindness.

**Learner activities:** Revisit familiar games, play with a friend and show what they know in many ways.

**Resources:** Materials from Weeks 1 to 9 of Term 2; Class record sheets; A camera or phone; Stickers

**Assessment:** Use the term record to judge each strand: comparing quantity, counting to five, numerals, measures, patterns and shapes. Use two or more occasions before recording Secure.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar tasks with growing independence.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 2.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and rhymes from the term; Social Habits: working with a friend and tidying up; Physical Development: movement games

## Day 1 (Monday): Counting and numeral games

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T2-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising numerals and counting

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit counting to five and numerals 1 to 5 through games.

### Success criteria
- Counts up to five objects by touching
- Matches numerals 1 to 5 to groups with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met counting to five, numerals 1 to 5, long and short, heavy and light, patterns, squares and triangles.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, long, short, heavy, light, pattern, square, triangle

**Instructional materials:** Blocks and trays; Numeral cards 1 to 5; A drum; Class record sheet

### Starter
Beat the drum five times and say "One, two, three, four, five." Say "Let us play our counting games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play "Count the blocks" with a tray of up to five blocks. Children: Children touch and count the blocks.

Step 2. Teacher: Play "Show me the numeral" with cards 1 to 5. Children: Children point to the numeral called.

Step 3. Teacher: Play "Fill the tray": show a numeral and ask children to count out that many blocks. Children: Child counts blocks to match the numeral.

Step 4. Teacher: Sing the rhyme to five with finger actions. Children: Children sing and show fingers.

### Practical activity: Counting stations
1. Set up counting and numeral stations.
2. Children play with a friend.
3. Teacher records at one station.

**Safety:** Use large blocks only; supervise stations; keep cards on the mat.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Count only to three and use numerals 1 to 3.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to count to five and write or trace the numeral.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many blocks?
  **Expected response:** The correct number up to five.
- **Ask:** Which numeral goes with this group?
  **Expected response:** The correct numeral.

### Expected outcomes
- Counts to five with support
- Matches numerals to groups

### Conclusion
Sing the counting rhyme and tidy up together.

### Home connection
Count to five at home with real objects.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit counting to five and numerals 1 to 5 through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Comparing games

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T2-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising comparing and patterns

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit more and same, long and short, and heavy and light through games.

### Success criteria
- Chooses the group with more
- Chooses the longer or heavier object

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met counting to five, numerals 1 to 5, long and short, heavy and light, patterns, squares and triangles.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, long, short, heavy, light, pattern, square, triangle

**Instructional materials:** Block piles; Ropes and sticks; Heavy and light baskets; Class record sheet

### Starter
Say "Today we play games about more, long and heavy. Show me what you know!" Show a big and a small pile of blocks.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show two piles of blocks and ask "Which has more?" Children: Children point to the pile with more.

Step 2. Teacher: Show two ropes and ask "Which is longer?" Line up the ends. Children: Children point to the longer rope.

Step 3. Teacher: Show the two baskets and ask "Which is heavy?" Children: Children lift and point to the heavy basket.

Step 4. Teacher: Let children play with a friend at each game. Children: Pairs play and take turns to ask each other.

### Practical activity: Comparing stations
1. Set up three comparing stations.
2. Children play with a friend.
3. Teacher records at one station.

**Safety:** Use soft ropes and light objects; keep weights moderate; supervise stations.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use only one comparison game at a time.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to ask a friend questions such as "Which is longer?"

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which pile has more?
  **Expected response:** The larger pile.
- **Ask:** Which rope is long?
  **Expected response:** The longer rope.

### Expected outcomes
- Compares quantity, length and weight
- Uses comparison words

### Conclusion
Sing the long and short song and tidy up together.

### Home connection
Compare things at home: which is heavier, longer, has more.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit more and same, long and short, and heavy and light through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- More, long, heavy

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of comparing games

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Pattern and shape games

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T2-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Revising shapes

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit patterns and the shapes circle, square and triangle through games.

### Success criteria
- Continues a two-part pattern
- Names or points to circle, square and triangle

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met counting to five, numerals 1 to 5, long and short, heavy and light, patterns, squares and triangles.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, long, short, heavy, light, pattern, square, triangle

**Instructional materials:** Coloured blocks; Cardboard shapes; A drum; Class record sheet

### Starter
Beat a clap and stamp pattern and say "What comes next?" Show the three shapes and say "Let us play pattern and shape games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the clap and stamp pattern and ask children to continue it. Children: Children clap and stamp in the pattern.

Step 2. Teacher: Make a red and blue block pattern and ask for the next block. Children: Child places the next block.

Step 3. Teacher: Show the shapes and ask "Find the triangle" and "Find the square." Children: Children find and name the shapes.

Step 4. Teacher: Make a shape pattern: circle, square, circle, square. Children: Children continue the shape pattern with support.

### Practical activity: Pattern and shape stations
1. Set up a pattern station and a shape station.
2. Children visit each with a friend.
3. Teacher records.

**Safety:** Use large cardboard shapes; supervise stations; keep the floor clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer only one pattern and one shape at a time.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make their own shape pattern.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What comes next in this pattern?
  **Expected response:** The next item in the pattern.
- **Ask:** Which shape is this?
  **Expected response:** Circle, square or triangle.

### Expected outcomes
- Continues patterns
- Names shapes with support

### Conclusion
Sing the shape songs and tidy up together.

### Home connection
Make shape patterns with cut-out paper at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit patterns and the shapes circle, square and triangle through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Pattern, shape

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of patterns and shapes

## Day 4 (Thursday): Market counting game

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T2-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Revising numerals and counting

### Learning objectives
- Children will use counting to five, more and same, and shapes in a pretend market.

### Success criteria
- Counts out up to five items
- Uses more or same in play

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met counting to five, numerals 1 to 5, long and short, heavy and light, patterns, squares and triangles.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, long, short, heavy, light, pattern, square, triangle

**Instructional materials:** A play market stall; Toy fruits and vegetables; Baskets; Number cards 1 to 5

### Starter
Set up the market and say "The market is open! Who wants to buy three oranges?" Show a number card.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Act as a trader. Ask a child to buy the number of items on a card. Children: Child counts out the items and puts them in a basket.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "Who has more? Who has the same?" after two children buy. Children: Children compare baskets and say more or same.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "Find a round fruit" and "Find a square box." Children: Children find shapes in the market.

Step 4. Teacher: Swap roles so each child is the trader and the customer. Children: Children take turns to sell and buy.

### Practical activity: Number market
1. Give each customer a number card.
2. They count out that number of items.
3. The trader checks by counting.

**Safety:** Use toy items only; keep baskets on the table; supervise role play.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use number cards 1 to 3 and ask for one item at a time.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to buy five items and say how many they have.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many oranges did you buy?
  **Expected response:** The number on the card.
- **Ask:** Who has more?
  **Expected response:** The basket with more.

### Expected outcomes
- Counts items in play
- Uses number and comparison words

### Conclusion
Close the market and say "Thank you, goodbye."

### Home connection
Play shopping at home and count out items to a family member.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "use counting to five, more and same, and shapes in a pretend market" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Market: 1 to 5

**Picture suggestions:**
- A market stall with number cards

## Day 5 (Friday): Show and celebrate again

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T2-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** assessment · **Subtopic:** Revising shapes

### Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 2 learning in familiar games and celebrate their work.

### Success criteria
- Takes part confidently in at least three familiar tasks
- Shows or says one thing they have learned

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met counting to five, numerals 1 to 5, long and short, heavy and light, patterns, squares and triangles.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, long, short, heavy, light, pattern, square, triangle

**Instructional materials:** Station materials from Weeks 1 to 9; Class record sheets; A camera or phone; Stickers or small certificates

### Starter
Sing a favourite song from the term. Say "Today we show what we know and celebrate."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six quick stations: more and same, counting, numerals, length and weight, patterns, shapes. Spend three minutes at each. Children: Children visit stations in pairs.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask a simple question and wait. Children: Child answers or shows the response.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs of each child at work and record levels. Children: Children continue playing.

Step 4. Teacher: Give each child a sticker or certificate and say one thing they did well. Children: Children receive their award and clap for friends.

### Practical activity: Celebration stations
1. Set up six stations.
2. Children visit each with a friend.
3. Teacher records evidence and takes photos.

**Safety:** Remove small items; supervise all stations; never compare children aloud.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Visit only three stations, with an adult partner at each.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to be a station helper.

### Assessment
Observe each child at the six stations and complete the Term 2 record. Record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for each strand: comparing quantity, counting to five, numerals 1 to 5, length and weight, patterns, shapes.

- **Ask:** Show me the number 4.
  **Expected response:** Child points to the numeral 4.
- **Ask:** What comes next in this pattern?
  **Expected response:** The next item in the pattern.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 2 observation record
- Celebrates their own learning

### Conclusion
Clap for the whole class and sing the closing song. Send the term note home.

### Home connection
Parents receive a Term 2 note with one strength, one next step and one home idea.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "show Term 2 learning in familiar games and celebrate their work" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Well done!

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of the celebration
