# Toddler Numeracy — Term 1, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T1-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising size, sorting and same and different; Revising counting to three; Revising circles and position

## Learning objectives
- Children will show secure learning of Term 1 ideas through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record.

**Key vocabulary:** big, small, same, sort, one, two, three, circle, in, on, under

**Content:** Revising and showing Term 1 learning through play and gathering evidence for the term record.

**Teaching activities:** Run relaxed play stations; observe and note without testing; offer help only when a child is stuck; celebrate effort.

**Learner activities:** Play familiar games, choose stations, show what they know to a friend and tidy up.

**Resources:** Materials from Weeks 1 to 9; Class record sheets; A camera or phone for evidence; A small certificate or sticker for each child

**Assessment:** Use the term record to judge each strand: size, same and different, sorting, quantity, counting to three, shape and position. Use two or more occasions before recording Secure.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 1.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and rhymes from the term; Social Habits: working with a friend and tidying up; Physical Development: movement games

## Day 1 (Monday): Size, same and different games

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T1-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising size, sorting and same and different

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit big and small, same and different and sorting through games.

### Success criteria
- Chooses the big or small object on request
- Matches an identical pair or sorts a group

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met big and small, same and different, sorting, one and lots, counting to three, circles and in, on and under.

**Key vocabulary:** big, small, same, sort, one, two, three, circle, in, on, under

**Instructional materials:** Big and small balls, cups and sandals; Colour baskets; Matching pairs; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing "Big, small" and "Same, same, same". Say "Today we play games we know. Show me what you can do!"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play "Show me big" and "Show me small" with a mixed basket of objects. Children: Children choose and show the correct object.

Step 2. Teacher: Play "Find my twin" with matching pairs, with children carrying objects to the right place. Children: Children find matches and say "same".

Step 3. Teacher: Open the colour baskets and ask children to sort objects into groups. Children: Children sort by colour and size.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask each child to show you one thing they can do well. Children: Child shows a skill and says a word if they can.

### Practical activity: Revision basket games
1. Set up three baskets: size, same, sort.
2. Children visit each basket with a friend.
3. Teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Use large objects; keep the room tidy and walkways clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer one task at a time with an adult partner.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to sort objects in a way of their own and explain it.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Show me the big one.
  **Expected response:** Child points to the larger object.
- **Ask:** Which two are the same?
  **Expected response:** Child points to a matching pair.

### Expected outcomes
- Revisits size, same and different and sorting
- Shows confident familiar skills

### Conclusion
Sing "Big, small" and tidy up together.

### Home connection
Play "big and small" with kitchen items this weekend.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit big and small, same and different and sorting through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Counting and one games

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T1-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising counting to three

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit one, lots, rhymes and counting to three through games.

### Success criteria
- Takes one on request
- Counts up to three objects by touching

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met big and small, same and different, sorting, one and lots, counting to three, circles and in, on and under.

**Key vocabulary:** big, small, same, sort, one, two, three, circle, in, on, under

**Instructional materials:** Blocks, cups and toy goats; A drum; Trays; Class record sheet

### Starter
Beat the drum three times and say "One, two, three." Say "Let us play the counting games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play "Give me one" and "Give me three" with blocks. Children: Children give one or three blocks.

Step 2. Teacher: Repeat the rhyme "One, clap! Two, clap! Three, clap!" Children: Children join in with claps.

Step 3. Teacher: Count cups by touching and sliding while the group says the numbers. Children: Children touch and count cups.

Step 4. Teacher: Play the goat story and count each goat in. Children: Children say the numbers one, two, three.

### Practical activity: Counting stations
1. Visit stations for blocks, cups and goats.
2. Count together at each station.
3. Teacher records at one station.

**Safety:** Use only large objects; supervise at every station.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Count only to two with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to count four objects with help and say four.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many cups?
  **Expected response:** The correct number up to three.
- **Ask:** Can you give me one block?
  **Expected response:** Child gives one block.

### Expected outcomes
- Counts to three
- Takes one on request

### Conclusion
Sing the clapping rhyme and tidy up together.

### Home connection
Count to three at mealtimes with real objects.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit one, lots, rhymes and counting to three through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- 1, 2, 3

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Shape and position games

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T1-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Revising circles and position

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit circles and in, on and under through games.

### Success criteria
- Points to circles
- Follows in, on and under instructions

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met big and small, same and different, sorting, one and lots, counting to three, circles and in, on and under.

**Key vocabulary:** big, small, same, sort, one, two, three, circle, in, on, under

**Instructional materials:** Round and not round objects; A box, a cloth and soft toys; A tunnel; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the circle song and "Where is Teddy?" Say "Let us play shape and hide and find games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Spread round and not round objects on the mat. Ask "Show me the round one." Children: Children pick the round objects.

Step 2. Teacher: Roll a ball and a block and ask "Which rolls?" Children: Children point to the ball.

Step 3. Teacher: Hide Teddy in, on and under and ask "Where is Teddy?" Children: Children find Teddy and say where.

Step 4. Teacher: Play follow the leader through the tunnel, on the bench and under the table. Children: Children follow safely.

### Practical activity: Shape and position circuit
1. Set up a round things basket and a position circuit.
2. Children visit each with a friend.
3. Teacher records.

**Safety:** Supervise the circuit closely; crawling only under tables.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer one task at a time with an adult.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to give a friend a position instruction.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Show me a circle.
  **Expected response:** Child points to a round object.
- **Ask:** Put Teddy under the cloth.
  **Expected response:** Child places Teddy under the cloth.

### Expected outcomes
- Finds circles and follows position instructions
- Shows confident familiar skills

### Conclusion
Sing "Where is Teddy?" and say goodbye to Teddy.

### Home connection
Look for round things and play hide and find at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit circles and in, on and under through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Our maths party

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T1-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Revising counting to three

### Learning objectives
- Children will use Term 1 maths ideas in a pretend party.

### Success criteria
- Counts or gives one, two or three items
- Uses big, small, same or round in play

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met big and small, same and different, sorting, one and lots, counting to three, circles and in, on and under.

**Key vocabulary:** big, small, same, sort, one, two, three, circle, in, on, under

**Instructional materials:** Plastic cups, plates and toy fruits; Soft toys as guests; A cloth for a party table

### Starter
Say "Teddy is having a party! He needs help with the party table." Put out the cloth and the toys.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Ask children to set the table: "Put one plate for each guest." and "Put one cup on each plate." Children: Children place one plate and one cup for each guest.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask them to choose fruit: "Give Teddy three oranges." and "Give the small bear a small cup." Children: Children count and choose big or small items.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "Where is the cake? In the box! On the table!" and "Which one is round?" Children: Children use position and shape words.

Step 4. Teacher: Sing a party song and clap one, two, three. Children: Children clap and sing together.

### Practical activity: Maths party table
1. Give each child a role such as server or guest.
2. Children set the table using plates, cups and fruits.
3. Everyone says what they have done.

**Safety:** Use toy or unbreakable items; keep real food out unless it is a planned snack with allergy checks.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Give one simple job such as "put one cup on the table".
- **Extension:** Ask the child to set the table for three guests without help.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many cups on the table?
  **Expected response:** The correct number up to three.
- **Ask:** Which plate is big?
  **Expected response:** The larger plate.

### Expected outcomes
- Uses number and shape words in play
- Takes a role in a group game

### Conclusion
Say thank you to the guests and tidy the party table together.

### Home connection
Help to lay the table at home: one plate for each person.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "use Term 1 maths ideas in a pretend party" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Party table

**Picture suggestions:**
- A table with plates, cups and fruit

## Day 5 (Friday): Show and celebrate

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-NUM-T1-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** assessment · **Subtopic:** Revising circles and position

### Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 1 learning in familiar games and celebrate their work.

### Success criteria
- Takes part confidently in at least three familiar tasks
- Shows or says one thing they have learned

**Previous knowledge:** Children have met big and small, same and different, sorting, one and lots, counting to three, circles and in, on and under.

**Key vocabulary:** big, small, same, sort, one, two, three, circle, in, on, under

**Instructional materials:** Station materials from Weeks 1 to 9; Class record sheets; A camera or phone; Stickers or small certificates

### Starter
Sing a favourite song from the term. Say "Today we show what we know and celebrate."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six quick stations: size, same, sort, count, circle, position. Spend three minutes at each. Children: Children visit stations in pairs.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask a simple question and wait. Children: Child answers or shows the response.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs of each child at work and record levels. Children: Children continue playing.

Step 4. Teacher: Give each child a sticker or certificate and say one thing they did well. Children: Children receive their award and clap for friends.

### Practical activity: Celebration stations
1. Set up six stations.
2. Children visit each with a friend.
3. Teacher records evidence and takes photos.

**Safety:** Remove small items; supervise all stations; ensure no child feels compared.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Visit only three stations, with an adult partner at each.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to be a station helper.

### Assessment
Observe each child at the six stations and complete the Term 1 record. Record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for each strand: size, same and different, sorting, quantity, counting to three, shape and position.

- **Ask:** Show me the big one.
  **Expected response:** Child points to the larger object.
- **Ask:** How many blocks?
  **Expected response:** The correct number up to three.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 1 observation record
- Celebrates their own learning

### Conclusion
Clap for the whole class and sing the closing song. Send the term note home.

### Home connection
Parents receive a Term 1 note with one strength, one next step and one home idea.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
