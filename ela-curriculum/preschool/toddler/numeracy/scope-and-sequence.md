# Toddler Numeracy — annual scope and sequence

Three terms of ten teaching weeks. Five planned lessons a week (150 in the year). Dates are configured by ELA's academic calendar, not written here.

## Term 1: Term 1 builds the habits of looking closely: size, same and different, sorting, one and lots, rhymes to three, counting to three and the circle

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Big and small | Big and small objects in the classroom; Big and small in my family and compound; Finding the big one and the small one | Children will point to the big or the small one when asked. Children will say or sign big and small with support. | big, small, little, bigger, show me | Chooses the big or small object on request in most turns. Uses the words big and small with prompting. |
| 2 | Same and different | Matching identical pairs; Same colour, same kind; Finding the one that is different | Children will match two identical objects. Children will point to the one that is different in a group of three. | same, different, match, pair, find | Matches a pair of identical objects independently. Points to the odd one out with a prompt. |
| 3 | Sorting | Sorting into two colour groups; Sorting three colours; Saying the colour of a group | Children will put objects of the same colour together. Children will name or point to a group by its colour. | sort, red, yellow, green, basket, together | Sorts into two colour groups with few errors. Names at least one colour in a group. |
| 4 | Sorting | Sorting big and small; Sorting by kind (food, clothes, toys); Telling why: "because they are fruits" | Children will sort objects by size into big and small groups. Children will sort objects by kind and give a one-word reason with support. | sort, group, kind, food, toys, clothes | Sorts by size or by kind with growing accuracy. Offers a word or gesture as a reason. |
| 5 | Quantity | One; Lots and a few; None: all gone | Children will take or show one when asked. Children will tell one thing from lots of things. | one, lots, few, none, all gone | Gives exactly one object on request. Uses the word one or lots. |
| 6 | Counting | One, two, three rhymes with actions; Fingers and toes counting; Number rhyme and movement | Children will join in a counting rhyme to three with actions. Children will show one, two and three fingers with help. | one, two, three, count, fingers | Joins in saying one, two, three in a rhyme. Shows the number of fingers with support. |
| 7 | Counting | Touch and count one at a time; How many?; Counting steps, claps and jumps | Children will touch each object once while saying the number words. Children will say or show how many when there are up to three. | count, how many, one, two, three, touch | Counts up to three objects with one touch for each. Answers how many with the last number word, with support. |
| 8 | Shape | Circles all around us; Rolling and not rolling; Drawing a circle in the air and in sand | Children will find round things in the classroom and outdoors. Children will name the circle with support. | circle, round, roll, ball, wheel | Points to round objects when asked. Says circle or round. |
| 9 | Position | In and on; Under; Hide and find games | Children will follow instructions using in, on and under. Children will say where a hidden object is using one position word. | in, on, under, where, hide, find | Places an object in, on or under on request. Uses a position word to say where. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising size, sorting and same and different; Revising counting to three; Revising circles and position | Children will show secure learning of Term 1 ideas through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | big, small, same, sort, one, two, three, circle, in, on, under | Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. |

## Term 2: Term 2 widens number to five and links quantity to numerals 1 to 5

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Comparing quantity | More; The same amount; No more, all gone | Children will point to the group that has more. Children will say more when they want another. | more, same, all gone, again, please | Chooses the group with more when the difference is clear. Uses the word more purposefully. |
| 2 | Counting | Five little rhymes with fingers; Count down and count up; Jumping to five | Children will join in counting rhymes to five. Children will show up to five fingers with help. | four, five, count, up, down, fingers | Says the numbers one to five in order with the group. Shows five fingers with support. |
| 3 | Counting | Touch and count four and five; How many now?; Counting while moving | Children will count up to five objects with one touch for each. Children will say how many using the last number word. | count, how many, four, five, last | Counts up to three objects reliably and up to five with support. Gives how many with support. |
| 4 | Numerals | Finding numerals in our world; Matching numerals to groups; Feeling and tracing numerals | Children will recognise the numerals 1, 2 and 3 with support. Children will match a numeral card to a group of one, two or three objects. | number, numeral, one, two, three, match | Matches a numeral to the right group of up to three. Points to a named numeral from three. |
| 5 | Numerals | Meeting 4 and 5; Putting numerals in order; Numeral and quantity games | Children will recognise the numerals 4 and 5 with support. Children will place numeral cards 1 to 5 in order with help. | four, five, in order, next, number line | Matches numerals 1 to 5 to groups with support. Orders 1 to 3 independently and 1 to 5 with help. |
| 6 | Measuring | Long and short objects; Comparing two lengths side by side; Long and short in stories and songs | Children will say which of two things is long or short. Children will line up two objects at one end to compare them. | long, short, longer, shorter, line up | Chooses the longer or shorter of two objects. Begins to line up objects to compare. |
| 7 | Measuring | Holding and comparing weight; Heavy and light baskets; Guess, feel and check | Children will say which of two objects feels heavy and which feels light. Children will predict which is heavy and check by holding. | heavy, light, hold, feel, guess | Identifies the heavier object when the difference is clear. Makes a prediction and checks it. |
| 8 | Pattern | Sound and clap patterns; Colour patterns AB; What comes next? | Children will copy a clap or movement pattern. Children will continue a two-colour pattern with support. | pattern, next, again, clap, stamp | Copies a two-part clap pattern. Adds the next object to an AB pattern with support. |
| 9 | Shape | Squares in our world; Triangles in our world; Circle, square or triangle? | Children will find squares and triangles around them. Children will tell a circle from a square with support. | square, triangle, circle, corner, side | Names or points to a square and a circle. Points to a triangle when asked. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising numerals and counting; Revising comparing and patterns; Revising shapes | Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record. | count, numeral, long, short, heavy, light, pattern, square, triangle | Completes familiar tasks with growing independence. Each child has an observation record for Term 2. |

## Term 3: Term 3 deepens and applies learning: capacity, the daily routine, shape hunts, position, sharing one-to-one, one more, counting with reasons, a simple class graph and a role-play market

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Measuring | Full and empty containers; Pouring water and sand; Nearly full, half full | Children will say whether a container is full or empty. Children will pour to fill a container with support. | full, empty, pour, fill, water, half | Identifies full and empty containers. Fills a cup and says full. |
| 2 | Time | Morning and night; Afternoon; What comes first and next in our day | Children will tell morning from night using pictures and routines. Children will say what happens first and next in the school day with support. | morning, afternoon, night, first, next, sleep | Sorts day and night pictures correctly. Names one activity that happens in the morning. |
| 3 | Shape | Circle, square, triangle revision; Shape hunt indoors and outdoors; Building with shapes | Children will find and name a circle, a square and a triangle in the environment. Children will build a simple picture using shapes with support. | circle, square, triangle, shape, build, find | Names or points to the three shapes with growing accuracy. Makes a shape picture. |
| 4 | Position | Up and down; In front and behind; Position games and obstacle courses | Children will follow instructions using up, down, in front and behind. Children will describe where they are using one position word. | up, down, in front, behind, next to | Moves to a position on request. Says where an object is using one word. |
| 5 | Counting | Sharing one each; Giving out to friends; Enough or not enough | Children will give one object to each friend. Children will show when there is not enough with support. | one each, share, enough, friend, give | Hands out one object to each of up to four friends. Notices when someone has none. |
| 6 | Counting | Adding one more; One more game with fingers; One more in stories and songs | Children will add one more object to a small group. Children will say or show how many there are now, with support. | one more, now, how many, add, altogether | Adds one more to a group of one to three. Shows how many now with fingers. |
| 7 | Counting | Counting without missing any; The last number tells how many; Checking our counting | Children will count up to five objects without skipping any. Children will check a count by counting again. | count, again, check, how many, last | Counts to three reliably and to five with growing accuracy. Says the last number as the answer to how many. |
| 8 | Data | Choosing and standing in lines; Which line has more?; Counting a line | Children will sort themselves or objects into groups by a simple choice. Children will say which group has more. | more, fewer, line, group, choose | Joins a group according to a choice. Points to the group with more. |
| 9 | Applying number | Setting up our market; Buying and selling up to five; Counting change in sharing games | Children will count out up to three or five items in a play market. Children will use number, more and one each words in role play. | buy, sell, market, how many, please, thank you | Counts out items on request in play. Uses number words naturally in role play. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising number, measures and shapes; Revising position and time; Celebration and end-of-year record | Children will show Year learning through games and stations. Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note. | count, one more, full, empty, morning, night, circle, square, triangle | Takes part confidently in familiar maths games. Each child has an end-of-year observation record. |
