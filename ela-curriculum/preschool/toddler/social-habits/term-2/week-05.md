# Toddler Social Habits — Term 2, Week 5: Responsibility

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W05 · **Week type:** consolidation · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Helping my teacher; Helping at home; Our class helpers

## Learning objectives
- Children will do small helping jobs.
- Children will say how they helped.

**Key vocabulary:** help, job, carry, clean, tidy, helper

**Content:** Helping my teacher, helping at home, and our class helpers.

**Teaching activities:** Give every child a small real job; make jobs safe and short; praise the effort; rotate helpers daily; use a helpers chart; link jobs at school to jobs at home; never compare children.

**Learner activities:** Do small helping jobs and say "I helped".

**Resources:** A helpers chart; Small brooms and cloths; Baskets; A watering can; Pictures of helping at home

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child does a small job with support and says "I helped".

## Expected outcomes
- Does a small job with support.
- Says "I helped".

**Cross-curricular connections:** Numeracy: counting helpers; Science: caring for plants; Literacy: job words

## Day 1 (Monday): Helping my teacher

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W05-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Helping my teacher

### Learning objectives
- Children will do a small helping job for the teacher.

### Success criteria
- Does a small job
- Says I helped

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to listen, say sorry and help a friend.

**Key vocabulary:** help, job, carry, clean, tidy, helper

**Instructional materials:** A helpers chart; Baskets; A bell; A small broom

### Starter
Show the helpers chart and say "Today our helpers are Ada and Tunde."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the jobs: line leader, door helper, tidy helper and plant helper. Children: Children look at the jobs.

Step 2. Teacher: Choose helpers and give each a job. Children: Child does their job.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Thank you, helper." Children: Child says you are welcome.

Step 4. Teacher: Change helpers so everyone has a turn. Children: Another child takes the job.

### Practical activity: Class helpers
1. Choose jobs.
2. Do the jobs.
3. Say thank you.

**Safety:** Give only safe jobs and supervise closely. Use child-size tools and never leave children alone with them.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Do the job together with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the next helper.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is your job?
  **Expected response:** The job name.
- **Ask:** Did you help?
  **Expected response:** Yes, I helped.

### Expected outcomes
- Does a small helping job
- Says I helped

### Conclusion
Say thank you to the helpers.

### Home connection
Give your child a small job to do at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "do a small helping job for the teacher" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- HELP
- JOB

**Picture suggestions:**
- A helpers chart

**Song or rhyme:** I am a helper, I help every day.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Helping at home

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W05-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Helping at home

### Learning objectives
- Children will name jobs they can do at home.

### Success criteria
- Names one job at home
- Acts out a helping job

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to listen, say sorry and help a friend.

**Key vocabulary:** help, job, carry, clean, tidy, helper

**Instructional materials:** Pictures of home jobs: sweeping, washing plates, setting the table, putting away shoes; Toy brooms and plates

### Starter
Show a picture of a child putting away shoes and say "This child helps at home."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture and name the job. Children: Children look and repeat the job.

Step 2. Teacher: Act out each job with toys. Children: Children act the jobs.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "What do you do at home?" Children: Child says or points to a job.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Helping makes our home happy." Children: Children smile and nod.

### Practical activity: Home jobs
1. Look at the pictures.
2. Act out the jobs.
3. Say what you do at home.

**Safety:** Use plastic dishes and child-size tools only. Never encourage children to touch hot or sharp things at home.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Act together and say the job.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to name two jobs.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do you do at home?
  **Expected response:** A small job.
- **Ask:** Who do you help?
  **Expected response:** Family.

### Expected outcomes
- Names a job at home
- Acts out a helping job

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "I help."

### Home connection
Let your child do one small job and say thank you.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "name jobs they can do at home" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- SWEEP
- HOME

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Plant helpers

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W05-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Our class helpers

### Learning objectives
- Children will help to care for a class plant.

### Success criteria
- Waters a plant with support
- Says plant helper

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to listen, say sorry and help a friend.

**Key vocabulary:** help, job, carry, clean, tidy, helper

**Instructional materials:** A class plant; A small watering can; A tray

### Starter
Show the class plant and say "Our plant needs a helper. Who will water it?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to pour a little water slowly. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each helper water the plant. Children: Child waters the plant.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Thank you, helper." Children: Children clap and cheer.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Plants need us." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Water the plant
1. Take a small can.
2. Water the plant slowly.
3. Put the can back.

**Safety:** Use a tray to catch drips and wipe spills at once. Wash hands after touching soil and supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the can together and pour slowly.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say how often the plant needs water.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What does the plant need?
  **Expected response:** Water.
- **Ask:** Who helps the plant?
  **Expected response:** The helper.

### Expected outcomes
- Waters a plant with support
- Says plant helper

### Conclusion
Wash hands and say "We helped."

### Home connection
Let your child water a plant at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 4 (Thursday): Tidy helpers

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W05-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Helping my teacher

### Learning objectives
- Children will work as helpers to tidy the classroom.

### Success criteria

- Says tidy helper

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to listen, say sorry and help a friend.

**Key vocabulary:** help, job, carry, clean, tidy, helper

**Instructional materials:** Baskets; Cloths; Small brooms; A tidy song

### Starter
Say "Our room is untidy. Tidy helpers, please help."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Give each child a tidy job. Children: Children take jobs.

Step 2. Teacher: Sing the tidy song while working. Children: Children tidy and sing.

Step 3. Teacher: Check the room together. Children: Children look at the tidy room.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Thank you, tidy helpers." Children: Children say well done.

### Practical activity: Tidy team
1. Choose a job.
2. Tidy together.
3. Check the room.

**Safety:** Use child-size tools and supervise closely. Keep walkways clear and never use cleaning chemicals with children.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Tidy beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead the tidy song.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did we do?
  **Expected response:** We tidied.
- **Ask:** Is the room tidy?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes

- Says tidy helper

### Conclusion
Look at the tidy room and say "Thank you."

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 5 helpers check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W05-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Our class helpers

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child helps at school.

### Success criteria
- Does a small job
- Says I helped

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to listen, say sorry and help a friend.

**Key vocabulary:** help, job, carry, clean, tidy, helper

**Instructional materials:** Helpers chart; Baskets; A plant; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the helper song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: plant, tidy and home jobs. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child helps. Children: Child helps with a prompt.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Helper stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors dry. Use child-size tools only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to teach a friend a job.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: does a small job; names a home job; waters a plant; says I helped.

- **Ask:** What is your job?
  **Expected response:** A job name.
- **Ask:** Did you help?
  **Expected response:** Yes, I helped.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 5
- Helps with small jobs

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say thank you.

### Home connection
Keep giving small jobs at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child helps at school" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Helpers

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of stations
