# Toddler Social Habits — Term 2, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising family and friends; Revising manners and honesty; Revising helping and respect

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record.

**Key vocabulary:** family, friend, sorry, truth, help, respect

**Content:** Revising family and friends, manners and honesty, and helping and respect, with end-of-term records.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly during play; record what children can do; share records with parents in plain language with a strength, a next step and a home idea.

**Learner activities:** Show learning from the term through play stations and a celebration.

**Resources:** Family cards; Puppets; A play kitchen; Helper hats; Baskets; Stickers; Class record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s Term 2 social learning.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar social tasks with growing independence.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 2.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs; Numeracy: counting; Creative Development: role play

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising family and friends

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising family and friends

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit family, friends and listening through games.

### Success criteria
- Names family members
- Plays with a friend

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about family, friends, listening, sorry, helping, honesty, caring, eating manners and respect.

**Key vocabulary:** family, friend, sorry, truth, help, respect

**Instructional materials:** Family cards; A play house; A ball; A friendship song

### Starter
Sing the family song and say "Today we play our favourite family and friend games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the family card game. Children: Child names family members.

Step 2. Teacher: Play family in the play house. Children: Child plays a family role.

Step 3. Teacher: Play ball with a friend. Children: Child rolls the ball to a friend.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Family and friends games
1. Set up three areas.
2. Children play each for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes behaviour.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and keep floors clear. Be sensitive to every family situation.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play with the child and one game.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is in your family?
  **Expected response:** A name.
- **Ask:** Who is your friend?
  **Expected response:** A child’s name.

### Expected outcomes
- Names family and friends
- Plays with a friend

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "I love my family."

### Home connection
Keep talking about family and friends.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit family, friends and listening through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- FAMILY
- FRIEND

**Picture suggestions:**
- Family cards

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Social stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising family and friends

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 2 social learning at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows manners, honesty and respect

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about family, friends, listening, sorry, helping, honesty, caring, eating manners and respect.

**Key vocabulary:** family, friend, sorry, truth, help, respect

**Instructional materials:** Six play stations; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Say "Today we play at six stations and show what we know."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: family, friends, listening, sorry, helping and respect. Children: Children rotate every three minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Social stations
1. Set up six stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep floors clear and use large safe toys.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names family; plays with friends; listens and follows instructions; says sorry; helps; shows respect.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a game.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 2 observation record
- Shows familiar social skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite game at home during the holiday.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 2 social learning at play stations" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 5 (Friday): Term celebration and records

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising manners and honesty

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate Term 2 with songs, and teachers will complete each child’s Term 2 record.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows something they enjoyed

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about family, friends, listening, sorry, helping, honesty, caring, eating manners and respect.

**Key vocabulary:** family, friend, sorry, truth, help, respect

**Instructional materials:** Favourite songs; Stickers; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate our term. Let us sing our favourite songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite songs and rhymes with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Give each child a sticker and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

Step 3. Teacher: Complete each child’s record and next step. Children: Children play quietly.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing and clap.
3. Give out stickers.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Keep the floor clear and supervise all play.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and sing together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s Term 2 record from the term’s observations. Note a strength, a next step and a home idea for parents.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite thing?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** What do you like to do?
  **Expected response:** A game or song.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed Term 2 record
- Enjoys the celebration

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you next term."

### Home connection
Share the Term 2 record and keep practising over the holiday.
