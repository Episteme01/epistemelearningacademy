# Toddler Social Habits — Term 2, Week 8: Good manners

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W08 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Washing hands before eating; Sitting and eating nicely; Not wasting food

## Learning objectives
- Children will wash hands before eating and sit to eat.
- Children will say please and thank you at mealtimes.

**Key vocabulary:** eat, plate, cup, wash, sit, thank you

**Content:** Washing hands before eating, sitting and eating nicely, and not wasting food.

**Teaching activities:** Check allergies and dietary needs before any food activity; use real snack time where possible; never force a child to eat; respect families’ ways of eating (with hands, spoons or forks) and food customs; keep a second adult nearby for choking safety; avoid whole nuts, hard sweets and other choking hazards.

**Learner activities:** Wash hands before eating, sit to eat and say please and thank you at mealtimes.

**Resources:** Plastic plates and cups; A play kitchen or toy food; Washing basin, soap and towels; A snack-time song; Pictures of eating nicely

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child washes hands before eating, sits to eat and says thank you.

## Expected outcomes
- Washes hands before eating with support.
- Sits to eat and says thank you.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Science: food and health; Literacy: mealtime words; Numeracy: one each

## Day 1 (Monday): Washing hands before eating

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W08-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Washing hands before eating

### Learning objectives
- Children will wash their hands before eating.

### Success criteria
- Washes hands with soap
- Says clean hands before food

**Previous knowledge:** Children can care for belongings and use please and thank you.

**Key vocabulary:** eat, plate, cup, wash, sit, thank you

**Instructional materials:** A basin and soap; Towels; A washing song

### Starter
Show a plate of toy food and say "Before we eat, we wash our hands."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to wash hands with soap and rub. Children: Children watch and copy.

Step 2. Teacher: Sing the hand washing song. Children: Children sing and rub.

Step 3. Teacher: Dry hands on a clean towel. Children: Child dries hands.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Clean hands before food." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Clean hands for food
1. Wash hands with soap.
2. Rinse and dry.
3. Sit at the table.

**Safety:** Use mild soap and shallow water and wipe spills. Supervise every child at the basin.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Wash hands together with a hand over the child’s hand.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to remind a friend to wash.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do before we eat?
  **Expected response:** Wash our hands.
- **Ask:** What do we use?
  **Expected response:** Soap and water.

### Expected outcomes
- Washes hands before eating
- Says clean hands

### Conclusion
Show clean hands and say "Ready to eat."

### Home connection
Wash hands together before every meal.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "wash their hands before eating" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Sitting and eating nicely

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W08-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Sitting and eating nicely

### Learning objectives
- Children will sit at a table and eat nicely.

### Success criteria
- Sits at the table to eat
- Chews with the mouth closed with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children can care for belongings and use please and thank you.

**Key vocabulary:** eat, plate, cup, wash, sit, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Plastic plates and cups; Toy food or a small safe snack; A mealtime song

### Starter
Set a small table and say "We sit down to eat. We do not run with food."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to sit with feet down and pull your chair in. Children: Children sit at the table.

Step 2. Teacher: Show eating slowly with a closed mouth. Children: Children copy with toy food.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Please may I have some?" and "Thank you." Children: Child asks and thanks.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We eat slowly and stay seated." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Table manners
1. Sit at the table.
2. Pretend to eat slowly.
3. Say please and thank you.

**Safety:** Use toy food for practice or only soft safe foods after checking allergies. A second adult must watch for choking at all times.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Eat beside the child and copy together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the host who serves friends.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where do we eat?
  **Expected response:** At the table.
- **Ask:** What do we say?
  **Expected response:** Please and thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Sits at the table to eat
- Uses please and thank you at the table

### Conclusion
Wipe the table and say "Thank you."

### Home connection
Eat together at a table and talk about the day.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "sit at a table and eat nicely" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- SIT
- EAT
- PLEASE

**Picture suggestions:**

**Song or rhyme:** Sit and eat, sit and eat, slowly, slowly.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Not wasting food

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W08-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Not wasting food

### Learning objectives
- Children will take only what they can eat and say that food is precious.

### Success criteria
- Takes a small amount
- Says food is good

**Previous knowledge:** Children can care for belongings and use please and thank you.

**Key vocabulary:** eat, plate, cup, wash, sit, thank you

**Instructional materials:** A plate and a small bowl of toy food; Pictures of farmers and food; A bin

### Starter
Show a plate with too much toy food and say "That is too much. We take a little."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to take a small amount and ask for more. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Show pictures of a farmer growing food and say "Someone worked hard." Children: Children look and listen.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children serve themselves toy food on a plate. Children: Child takes a little.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We do not waste food." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: A little at a time
1. Take a little toy food.
2. Eat it slowly.
3. Ask for more if hungry.

**Safety:** Use toy food only for serving practice. Never force a child to finish food and be sensitive to families’ circumstances.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Serve a little together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say who grew the food.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How much do we take?
  **Expected response:** A little.
- **Ask:** Who grew the food?
  **Expected response:** A farmer.

### Expected outcomes
- Takes a small amount
- Says that food is precious

### Conclusion
Say "Thank you for our food."

### Home connection
Let your child serve small portions and thank the cook.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "take only what they can eat and say that food is precious" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Snack time practice

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W08-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Sitting and eating nicely

### Learning objectives
- Children will use all their eating manners at a real or pretend snack time.

### Success criteria
- Washes hands and sits to eat
- Says please and thank you

**Previous knowledge:** Children can care for belongings and use please and thank you.

**Key vocabulary:** eat, plate, cup, wash, sit, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Cups, plates, safe soft snack or toy food; Soap and towels; A snack song

### Starter
Say "It is snack time. First wash, then sit, then please and thank you."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Remind children of the three steps. Children: Children repeat wash, sit and ask.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children wash hands and go to the table. Children: Children wash and sit.

Step 3. Teacher: Serve the snack and say please and thank you. Children: Child asks and thanks.

Step 4. Teacher: Clear the plates and say "Well done." Children: Child puts a plate away.

### Practical activity: Snack time
1. Wash hands.
2. Sit down nicely.
3. Say please and thank you.

**Safety:** Check every child’s allergies and avoid choking hazards. Keep a second adult nearby and seat children while they eat.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Sit beside the child and prompt each step.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the snack helper.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do first?
  **Expected response:** Wash our hands.
- **Ask:** What do we say?
  **Expected response:** Please and thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Uses eating manners
- Says please and thank you

### Conclusion
Wipe hands and say "Thank you for the food."

### Home connection

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 8 mealtime check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W08-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Washing hands before eating

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child behaves at mealtimes.

### Success criteria
- Washes hands before eating
- Sits and says thank you

**Previous knowledge:** Children can care for belongings and use please and thank you.

**Key vocabulary:** eat, plate, cup, wash, sit, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Toy food; Basin and soap; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the snack song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: hand washing, table manners and serving. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child behaves. Children: Child follows with a prompt.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Mealtime stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Use toy food only and supervise each station. Keep floors dry and wipe spills.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to teach a friend table manners.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: washes hands before eating; sits to eat; uses please and thank you; takes a small amount.

- **Ask:** What do we do before eating?
  **Expected response:** Wash hands.
- **Ask:** Where do we sit?
  **Expected response:** At the table.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 8
- Shows mealtime manners with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep good mealtime habits at home.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
