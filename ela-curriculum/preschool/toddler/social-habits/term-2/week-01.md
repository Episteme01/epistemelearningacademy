# Toddler Social Habits — Term 2, Week 1: Family

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W01 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Who is in my family; What my family does for me; Showing love to my family

## Learning objectives
- Children will name people in their family.
- Children will say one way they show love to their family.

**Key vocabulary:** mother, father, baby, grandma, family, love

**Content:** Who is in my family, what my family does for me, and showing love to my family.

**Teaching activities:** Welcome every kind of family: children may live with parents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, guardians or siblings; never ask a child to explain a family situation; use family photographs only if the family agrees; use home-language words for family members and repeat them in English.

**Learner activities:** Name people in their family and show or say love to a family member.

**Resources:** Family photographs brought from home (optional); A doll family or picture cards; A heart cut-out; A family song; Crayons

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child names at least two family members and shows love in a simple way.

## Expected outcomes
- Names at least two family members.
- Shows or says love to a family member.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: family words; Numeracy: counting family members; Creative Development: drawing

## Day 1 (Monday): Who is in my family?

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W01-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Who is in my family

### Learning objectives
- Children will name people who are in their family.

### Success criteria
- Names one or two family members
- Points to a family picture card

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness and safety in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** mother, father, baby, grandma, family, love

**Instructional materials:** Picture cards: mother, father, baby, grandma, grandpa, brother, sister; A doll family; A family song

### Starter
Hold up the doll family and say "This is a family. Every family is special."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture card and say the family word. Children: Children look and repeat the word.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "Who is in your family?" and wait. Children: Child names or points to a person.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children share a home-language word for a family member. Children: Child says the word if they wish.

Step 4. Teacher: Sing the family song with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

### Practical activity: Family cards
1. Look at the family cards.
2. Name each person.
3. Choose a card for your family.

**Safety:** Never ask a child to explain their family situation and accept every kind of family. Use photographs only with parents’ agreement.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Name the family members together with the cards.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say a family member’s name and what they do.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is in your family?
  **Expected response:** Mother, father, sister or other person.
- **Ask:** Who looks after you?
  **Expected response:** A family member.

### Expected outcomes
- Names at least one family member
- Points to a family picture card

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "I love my family."

### Home connection
Talk about family members with your child and name each one.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "name people who are in their family" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- FAMILY
- MOTHER
- FATHER

**Picture suggestions:**
- A family picture
- Doll family

**Song or rhyme:** My family, my family, I love my family.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): What my family does for me

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W01-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** What my family does for me

### Learning objectives
- Children will say one thing their family does for them.

### Success criteria
- Says or shows one thing a family member does
- Says thank you to the family with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness and safety in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** mother, father, baby, grandma, family, love

**Instructional materials:** Pictures: cooking, bathing, reading, playing, hugging; A puppet

### Starter
Show a picture of someone cooking and say "Someone cooks for me. My family helps me."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture and say what the family member does. Children: Children look and say the action.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "Who cooks for you?" and listen. Children: Child points to a person or says a word.

Step 3. Teacher: Act out looking after a baby doll with kindness. Children: Child rocks or feeds the doll gently.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We say thank you to our family." Children: Children say thank you together.

### Practical activity: Family helpers
1. Look at the pictures.
2. Say what the family does.
3. Say thank you.

**Safety:** Keep pictures gentle and accept every child’s answer. Be sensitive to children whose home circumstances are difficult.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Show a picture and say the action together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say two things their family does.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who cooks for you?
  **Expected response:** A family member.
- **Ask:** What do we say to them?
  **Expected response:** Thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Says one thing a family member does
- Says thank you to the family

### Conclusion
Hug the doll and say "I am thankful."

### Home connection
Thank each family member for something small each day.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say one thing their family does for them" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- COOK
- LOVE
- THANK YOU

**Picture suggestions:**
- A mother cooking
- A father reading

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Showing love

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W01-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Showing love to my family

### Learning objectives
- Children will show love to family members in simple ways.

### Success criteria
- Makes a heart or gives a smile
- Says "I love you" or waves with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness and safety in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** mother, father, baby, grandma, family, love

**Instructional materials:** A paper heart for each child; Crayons; Stickers

### Starter
Show a paper heart and say "A heart means love. We can show love to our family."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to colour a heart for a family member. Children: Children colour a heart.

Step 2. Teacher: Help each child write or mark their name on the heart. Children: Child makes a mark on the heart.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Who is the heart for?" Children: Child names or points to a person.

Step 4. Teacher: Practise saying "I love you" or giving a smile and wave. Children: Child says or shows love.

### Practical activity: Heart for my family
1. Colour a heart.
2. Add your name.
3. Say who it is for.

**Safety:** Use non-toxic crayons and supervise closely. Never force a child to say I love you; accept a smile or a wave.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Colour together and say the family name.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to colour two hearts.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is your heart for?
  **Expected response:** A family member.
- **Ask:** How do you show love?
  **Expected response:** Hug, smile or kind words.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes a heart for a family member
- Shows love in a simple way

### Conclusion
Sing the family song and wave.

### Home connection
Give the heart to a family member and say thank you.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "show love to family members in simple ways" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- HEART
- LOVE

**Picture suggestions:**
- A paper heart

## Day 4 (Thursday): Family helping game

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W01-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** What my family does for me

### Learning objectives
- Children will act out how family members help each other.

### Success criteria
- Acts out a family job
- Says help with a prompt

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness and safety in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** mother, father, baby, grandma, family, love

**Instructional materials:** Doll house or play kitchen; Dolls; Toy cups and plates

### Starter
Set up a play house and say "Let us play family and help each other."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to feed a doll and put it to bed. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children choose a role such as mother, father or child. Children: Child chooses a role.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "How can we help?" and let children act. Children: Child acts out a helping job.

Step 4. Teacher: Say thank you for the help. Children: Children say thank you to friends.

### Practical activity: Play family
1. Set up the play house.
2. Choose roles.
3. Act out helping.

**Safety:** Keep toys large and clean and supervise sharing. Respect every child’s idea of family.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child and suggest a job.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the helper who thanks the others.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What job did you do?
  **Expected response:** Feed, cook or tidy.
- **Ask:** Who helps in a family?
  **Expected response:** Everyone helps.

### Expected outcomes
- Acts out a family job
- Says help or thank you in play

### Conclusion
Tidy the play house and say "We help."

### Home connection
Let your child help with a small job and say thank you.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "act out how family members help each other" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- HELP
- FAMILY

**Picture suggestions:**
- A play house

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 1 family check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W01-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Showing love to my family

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child talks about family and shows love.

### Success criteria
- Names two family members
- Shows love in a simple way

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness and safety in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** mother, father, baby, grandma, family, love

**Instructional materials:** Family cards; Hearts; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the family song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: family cards, hearts and play house. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "Who is in your family?" and watch. Children: Child answers with a word or action.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Family stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and be sensitive to every family situation. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to tell a friend about their family.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names family members; says what the family does; shows love; helps in play.

- **Ask:** Who is in your family?
  **Expected response:** A person’s name.
- **Ask:** How do you show love?
  **Expected response:** Hug or kind words.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 1
- Talks about family with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep talking about family and showing love at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child talks about family and shows love" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Family

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of stations
