# Toddler Social Habits — Term 2, Week 7: Responsibility

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W07 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** My bag and my things; Caring for toys and books; Putting things back

## Learning objectives
- Children will care for toys, books and their own belongings.
- Children will put things back in the right place.

**Key vocabulary:** bag, book, toy, gentle, back, place

**Content:** My bag and my things, caring for toys and books, and putting things back.

**Teaching activities:** Give every child a labelled hook and bag; show how to hold a book gently; use pictures on shelves so children can put things back; praise care not just tidiness; replace broken items without shame.

**Learner activities:** Care for toys, books and belongings and put things back in the right place.

**Resources:** Bags and hooks with names; Picture books; Toy baskets with picture labels; A shelf; A caring song

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child puts things back and handles a book gently.

## Expected outcomes
- Puts a toy back where it belongs.
- Handles a book gently.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: book handling; Numeracy: matching and sorting; Science: caring for the environment

## Day 1 (Monday): My bag and my things

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W07-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** My bag and my things

### Learning objectives
- Children will find and care for their own bag and belongings.

### Success criteria
- Finds their own bag or hook
- Puts their things away with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children can tell the truth, help and tidy up.

**Key vocabulary:** bag, book, toy, gentle, back, place

**Instructional materials:** Labelled hooks and bags; A water bottle; A lunch box; A song

### Starter
Show a bag on a hook and say "This is Ada’s bag. It goes on her hook."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to hang a bag on its hook. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Help each child find their name and hook. Children: Child finds their name.

Step 3. Teacher: Show how to put a bottle and lunch box in the bag. Children: Child puts things in the bag.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "I look after my things." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: My hook and my bag
1. Find your name.
2. Hang your bag.
3. Put your things inside.

**Safety:** Keep bags low and hooks at child height. Check no small objects are in reach of younger children.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Find the hook together and hang the bag.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to help a friend find their hook.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where does your bag go?
  **Expected response:** On my hook.
- **Ask:** Whose bag is this?
  **Expected response:** A child’s name.

### Expected outcomes
- Finds their own bag or hook
- Puts things away with support

### Conclusion
Look at the hooks and say "Everyone has a place."

### Home connection
Give your child a place at home for their bag and shoes.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "find and care for their own bag and belongings" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Caring for toys and books

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W07-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Caring for toys and books

### Learning objectives
- Children will handle toys and books gently.

### Success criteria
- Turns a page gently
- Puts a toy down gently

**Previous knowledge:** Children can tell the truth, help and tidy up.

**Key vocabulary:** bag, book, toy, gentle, back, place

**Instructional materials:** Picture books; Soft toys; A puppet who is rough and then gentle

### Starter
Show a puppet throwing a book and say "Oh no. Books are for looking, not throwing."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to hold and turn the pages gently. Children: Children watch and copy.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children look at a book with a friend. Children: Child looks at the book gently.

Step 3. Teacher: Show how to place toys down gently. Children: Child puts a toy down gently.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "I am gentle with books and toys." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Gentle with books
1. Hold a book.
2. Turn the pages gently.
3. Put it back on the shelf.

**Safety:** Use sturdy board books and supervise closely so books are not chewed. Replace torn items without comment.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Turn the pages together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to show a friend how to hold a book.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How do we hold a book?
  **Expected response:** Gently.
- **Ask:** Where does it go?
  **Expected response:** On the shelf.

### Expected outcomes
- Handles books gently
- Puts toys down gently

### Conclusion
Put the books on the shelf and say thank you.

### Home connection
Read a book together and let your child turn the pages.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "handle toys and books gently" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Putting things back

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W07-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Putting things back

### Learning objectives
- Children will put toys back where they belong using picture labels.

### Success criteria
- Matches a toy to its label
- Puts the toy back

**Previous knowledge:** Children can tell the truth, help and tidy up.

**Key vocabulary:** bag, book, toy, gentle, back, place

**Instructional materials:** Baskets with picture labels; Toys; A tidy song

### Starter
Show a basket with a picture of cars and say "Cars go here. Every toy has a home."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to match a toy to its picture label. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child pick a toy and find its basket. Children: Child puts the toy in the basket.

Step 3. Teacher: Sing the tidy song while working. Children: Children tidy and sing.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Every toy is home." Children: Children say home.

### Practical activity: Toy homes
1. Look at the labels.
2. Match each toy.
3. Put it in its basket.

**Safety:** Keep walkways clear and use large toys only. Supervise the tidy-up.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Match the toy together and name the label.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to check a basket for the right toys.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where does this toy go?
  **Expected response:** In its basket.
- **Ask:** Is every toy home?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Matches toys to labels
- Puts toys back

### Conclusion
Look at the tidy shelves and clap.

### Home connection
Make picture labels for toy boxes at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "put toys back where they belong using picture labels" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Looking after our classroom

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W07-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Putting things back

### Learning objectives
- Children will share responsibility for the classroom and its things.

### Success criteria
- Tidies and cares with a friend
- Reminds a friend gently

**Previous knowledge:** Children can tell the truth, help and tidy up.

**Key vocabulary:** bag, book, toy, gentle, back, place

**Instructional materials:** The classroom toys and books; A helpers chart; Stickers

### Starter
Say "This is our class. We look after our things together."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Walk around and notice things to put back. Children: Children walk and look.

Step 2. Teacher: Give each child a job such as books, blocks or cups. Children: Child does a job.

Step 3. Teacher: Remind children to be gentle. Children: Child reminds a friend gently.

Step 4. Teacher: Give a sticker to every helper. Children: Child receives a sticker and says thank you.

### Practical activity: Class care
1. Look around the room.
2. Choose a job.
3. Put things back together.

**Safety:** Supervise closely and use only safe items. Keep stickers away from mouths.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Work beside the child and name the jobs.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the job allocator.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did you look after?
  **Expected response:** Books or toys.
- **Ask:** Who looked after it?
  **Expected response:** We did.

### Expected outcomes
- Cares for the classroom with a friend
- Reminds a friend gently

### Conclusion
Say "We look after our class."

### Home connection
Show your child how to look after the family’s things.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 7 caring check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W07-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Caring for toys and books

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child cares for belongings.

### Success criteria
- Handles a book gently
- Puts things back

**Previous knowledge:** Children can tell the truth, help and tidy up.

**Key vocabulary:** bag, book, toy, gentle, back, place

**Instructional materials:** Books; Toys; Baskets; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the caring song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: bags, books and toys. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child cares. Children: Child cares with a prompt.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Caring stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep walkways clear. Use sturdy items only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show a friend how to care for a book.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: finds own bag; handles books gently; puts toys back; helps keep the class tidy.

- **Ask:** How do we hold a book?
  **Expected response:** Gently.
- **Ask:** Where do toys go?
  **Expected response:** In their baskets.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 7
- Cares for belongings with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep showing how to care for things at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child cares for belongings" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
