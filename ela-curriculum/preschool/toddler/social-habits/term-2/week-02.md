# Toddler Social Habits — Term 2, Week 2: Friends

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W02 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Playing with friends; Being a good friend; Making friends

## Learning objectives
- Children will play beside and with friends.
- Children will say what a good friend does.

**Key vocabulary:** friend, play, together, kind, share, help

**Content:** Playing with friends, being a good friend, and making friends.

**Teaching activities:** Notice and name friendly acts; let children choose play partners; help shy children join with a simple script such as "Can I play?"; avoid forcing friendships; use pairs and small groups; reassure children when a friend is absent.

**Learner activities:** Play with a friend for a short time and say what a good friend does.

**Resources:** Matching toys; A friendship song; Puppets; Pictures of friends playing; A friendship chart

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child plays with a friend for a short time and says one thing a good friend does.

## Expected outcomes
- Plays with a friend for a short time.
- Says one thing a good friend does.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: friend words; Numeracy: pairs; Creative Development: group songs

## Day 1 (Monday): Playing with friends

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W02-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Playing with friends

### Learning objectives
- Children will play with a friend for a short time.

### Success criteria
- Plays with a friend
- Says friend with a prompt

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name family members and show love in simple ways.

**Key vocabulary:** friend, play, together, kind, share, help

**Instructional materials:** Large building blocks; A ball; A friendship song

### Starter
Sing "Friends, friends, I have friends. We play together, hand in hand."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how two children can play with one ball. Children: Children watch and wave.

Step 2. Teacher: Pair children and give them blocks. Children: Child plays beside a friend.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Look, you are playing together." Children: Children smile at each other.

Step 4. Teacher: Change partners and play again. Children: Child plays with a new friend.

### Practical activity: Play with a friend
1. Choose a friend.
2. Build or play together.
3. Change partners.

**Safety:** Use large toys with no small parts and supervise pairs closely. Step in gently if a child is left out.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play with the child and a friend.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose a friend and say their name.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is your friend?
  **Expected response:** A child’s name.
- **Ask:** What do you play?
  **Expected response:** A game or toy.

### Expected outcomes
- Plays with a friend for a short time
- Says friend with a prompt

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye to friends.

### Home connection
Arrange short play with a friend and notice when they play well.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "play with a friend for a short time" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Being a good friend

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W02-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Being a good friend

### Learning objectives
- Children will say what a good friend does.

### Success criteria
- Says one thing a good friend does
- Does a friendly act

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name family members and show love in simple ways.

**Key vocabulary:** friend, play, together, kind, share, help

**Instructional materials:** Pictures of good friends sharing, helping, playing and comforting; A puppet

### Starter
Show a puppet who shares and say "A good friend shares and plays nicely."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture and say what the friend does. Children: Children look and say the action.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "What does a good friend do?" Children: Child says or points to a picture.

Step 3. Teacher: Act out sharing, helping and comforting with a friend. Children: Child acts with a friend.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "I am a good friend." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Friend actions
1. Look at the pictures.
2. Act each one.
3. Say I am a good friend.

**Safety:** Supervise acting closely so play stays gentle. Offer a wave or high five instead of touch if a child prefers.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Act together and say the word.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to give an example of a good friend.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What does a good friend do?
  **Expected response:** Shares, helps or plays.
- **Ask:** Are you a good friend?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Says what a good friend does
- Does a friendly act

### Conclusion
Sing the friendship song and wave.

### Home connection
Praise your child when they are a good friend.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say what a good friend does" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Making friends

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W02-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Making friends

### Learning objectives
- Children will practise asking to join a game.

### Success criteria
- Says "Can I play?" with a prompt
- Invites a friend to play

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name family members and show love in simple ways.

**Key vocabulary:** friend, play, together, kind, share, help

**Instructional materials:** A puppet; A play area; Pictures of children playing together

### Starter
Use the puppet to say "Can I play with you?" and let the friend say yes.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to ask "Can I play?" and wait for the answer. Children: Children watch and listen.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "If a friend says yes, play. If not, try another friend." Children: Children nod and smile.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children practise asking a friend. Children: Child asks a friend.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Come and play" to a child who is alone. Children: Child invites a friend.

### Practical activity: Can I play?
1. Practise asking a friend.
2. Say yes and play.
3. Invite a child who is alone.

**Safety:** Supervise the practice and keep it gentle so no child feels rejected. Pair shy children with a kind partner.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Ask with the child and wait together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find a friend who is alone and invite them.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do you say to join?
  **Expected response:** Can I play?
- **Ask:** What do you do for a lonely friend?
  **Expected response:** Invite them to play.

### Expected outcomes
- Asks to join a game
- Invites a friend to play

### Conclusion
Say "Everyone can play" and wave.

### Home connection
Practise asking to join a game at the park.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "practise asking to join a game" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Friends play together

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W02-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Playing with friends

### Learning objectives
- Children will play a cooperative game with friends.

### Success criteria
- Plays a group game
- Says friend or friends

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name family members and show love in simple ways.

**Key vocabulary:** friend, play, together, kind, share, help

**Instructional materials:** A parachute or large cloth; A soft ball; A friendship song

### Starter
Show the cloth and say "We hold the cloth together and make it go up and down."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to hold the cloth edge and lift it together. Children: Children hold the cloth.

Step 2. Teacher: Lift up and down while singing. Children: Children lift together.

Step 3. Teacher: Roll the ball on the cloth. Children: Children watch the ball and help.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We played together." Children: Children say we did it.

### Practical activity: Cloth game
1. Hold the cloth edge.
2. Lift together.
3. Roll the ball.

**Safety:** Use a soft light cloth and a very soft ball and stand away from furniture. Keep the group small.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the cloth beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to count up and down.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did we do?
  **Expected response:** We played together.
- **Ask:** Who played?
  **Expected response:** All friends.

### Expected outcomes
- Plays a group game with friends
- Says we did it

### Conclusion
Fold the cloth and say "Friends."

### Home connection
Play a group game with the family.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "play a cooperative game with friends" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 2 friends check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T2-W02-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Being a good friend

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child plays with and behaves toward friends.

### Success criteria
- Plays with a friend
- Says one thing a good friend does

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name family members and show love in simple ways.

**Key vocabulary:** friend, play, together, kind, share, help

**Instructional materials:** Toys; Puppets; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the friendship song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: play, good friend and join a game. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child plays and speaks. Children: Child plays with a friend.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Friendship stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station closely and keep play gentle. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to welcome a quiet friend.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: plays with a friend; says what a good friend does; asks to join a game; includes others.

- **Ask:** Who is your friend?
  **Expected response:** A child’s name.
- **Ask:** What does a good friend do?
  **Expected response:** Shares and helps.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 2
- Plays with friends with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep encouraging friendly play.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child plays with and behaves toward friends" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
