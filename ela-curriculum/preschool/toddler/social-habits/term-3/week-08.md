# Toddler Social Habits — Term 3, Week 8: Community

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W08 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Our clothes and food; Songs and dances; Festivals and special days

## Learning objectives
- Children will enjoy and name familiar cultural clothes, foods, songs and dances.
- Children will say that families celebrate in different ways.

**Key vocabulary:** dance, song, food, clothes, party, celebrate

**Content:** Our clothes and food, songs and dances, and festivals and special days.

**Teaching activities:** Celebrate the cultures of every family in the class (Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, and others, and different faiths); invite parents to share clothes, foods, songs and stories; never rank cultures or religions; use real objects and songs; keep any food tasting allergy-safe and halal or dietary needs respected.

**Learner activities:** Join in songs and dances and name a food or clothing from home.

**Resources:** Traditional clothes and fabric; Pictures of local foods; Drums and shakers; Recorded or live songs; Pictures of festivals

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child joins a song or dance and names a food or clothing from home.

## Expected outcomes
- Joins in a song or dance.
- Names a food or clothing from home.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and stories; Creative Development: music and dance; Understanding the World: people and communities

## Day 1 (Monday): Our clothes and food

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W08-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Our clothes and food

### Learning objectives
- Children will name familiar clothes and foods from their homes.

### Success criteria
- Names a clothing item or a food from home
- Touches different fabrics

**Previous knowledge:** Children know about family, respect and community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** dance, song, food, clothes, party, celebrate

**Instructional materials:** Pieces of traditional fabric; A cap and a head-tie; Pictures of local foods such as rice, yam and beans

### Starter
Show a piece of colourful fabric and say "This is a cloth from our homes. Our families wear lovely clothes."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show fabrics and clothes and name each one. Children: Children touch and repeat the names.

Step 2. Teacher: Show pictures of foods and say "We eat many good foods." Children: Children name foods they know.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children share a food from home. Children: Child says or points to a food.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We are proud of our clothes and food." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Clothes and food
1. Touch the fabrics.
2. Look at the food pictures.
3. Say a food you like.

**Safety:** Check all objects are clean and safe and never rank one family’s culture above another. Use pictures only for foods unless parents agree to tasting.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Touch a fabric and name it together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what their family wears on a special day.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What food do you like?
  **Expected response:** A food name.
- **Ask:** What do you wear on a special day?
  **Expected response:** Special clothes.

### Expected outcomes
- Names clothes and foods from home
- Touches different fabrics

### Conclusion
Say "We love our culture" and wave.

### Home connection
Talk to your child about traditional clothes and foods your family enjoys.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "name familiar clothes and foods from their homes" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Songs and dances

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W08-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Songs and dances

### Learning objectives
- Children will join in songs and dances from different homes.

### Success criteria
- Joins a song with actions
- Moves to a drum beat

**Previous knowledge:** Children know about family, respect and community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** dance, song, food, clothes, party, celebrate

**Instructional materials:** A drum; Shakers; A recording or live singer; Scarves

### Starter
Beat the drum gently and say "Listen. Can you hear the beat? Let us dance."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the drum slowly and let children clap the beat. Children: Children clap with the beat.

Step 2. Teacher: Teach a simple song from the community. Children: Children join with actions.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children dance with scarves. Children: Child dances to the beat.

Step 4. Teacher: Invite a child to share a song from home. Children: Child sings or hums if they wish.

### Practical activity: Drum and dance
1. Clap the beat.
2. Sing the song.
3. Dance with a scarf.

**Safety:** Keep the drum volume gentle and the floor clear. Offer a quiet space to a child sensitive to noise.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Dance beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a song.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we play?
  **Expected response:** A drum.
- **Ask:** Do you like to dance?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Joins a song with actions
- Moves to a drum beat

### Conclusion
Sit quietly and say "Thank you."

### Home connection
Sing a family song and dance together.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "join in songs and dances from different homes" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Festivals and special days

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W08-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Festivals and special days

### Learning objectives
- Children will talk about special days that families celebrate.

### Success criteria
- Names a special day
- Says how it feels

**Previous knowledge:** Children know about family, respect and community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** dance, song, food, clothes, party, celebrate

**Instructional materials:** Pictures of festivals, birthdays and weddings; Decorations; A puppet

### Starter
Show a birthday picture and say "A birthday is a special day. Families celebrate in many ways."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show pictures of different celebrations and say what people do. Children: Children look and say party or dance.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "What do you do on a special day?" Children: Child says or points.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Every family celebrates in its own way." Children: Children listen and nod.

Step 4. Teacher: Make a simple decoration for the class. Children: Child makes a decoration.

### Practical activity: Special day decorations
1. Look at the pictures.
2. Choose a colour.
3. Make a decoration.

**Safety:** Use non-toxic washable materials and avoid assuming which festivals families celebrate. Never ask a child to join a celebration their family does not observe.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Make the decoration together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what makes a day special.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is a special day?
  **Expected response:** A birthday or festival.
- **Ask:** What do you do?
  **Expected response:** Party, eat or dance.

### Expected outcomes
- Names a special day
- Says how it feels

### Conclusion
Hang the decorations and say well done.

### Home connection
Talk about the special days your family celebrates.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "talk about special days that families celebrate" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Our cultural party

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W08-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Songs and dances

### Learning objectives
- Children will join a small class party with songs, dances and decorations.

### Success criteria
- Joins the party
- Greets friends happily

**Previous knowledge:** Children know about family, respect and community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** dance, song, food, clothes, party, celebrate

**Instructional materials:** Decorations from yesterday; A drum; Scarves; Fabrics

### Starter
Say "Today we have a party for our class. We will sing and dance together."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Wear fabrics or scarves and greet friends. Children: Children greet each other.

Step 2. Teacher: Sing and dance to the drum. Children: Children dance and sing.

Step 3. Teacher: Share a song or dance from a child’s home. Children: Child shows a song or dance.

Step 4. Teacher: Say thank you and goodbye. Children: Children wave and say thank you.

### Practical activity: Class party
1. Wear scarves.
2. Sing and dance.
3. Say thank you.

**Safety:** Keep the volume gentle and the floor clear. Do not require any child to wear or perform anything that their family does not agree to.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Dance together and stay beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the drum leader.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did we do?
  **Expected response:** We sang and danced.
- **Ask:** Did you enjoy it?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Joins the party
- Greets friends happily

### Conclusion
Put away the scarves and say thank you.

### Home connection
Enjoy a family song and dance at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 8 culture check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W08-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Our clothes and food

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child joins in cultural activities.

### Success criteria
- Joins a song or dance
- Names a food or clothing from home

**Previous knowledge:** Children know about family, respect and community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** dance, song, food, clothes, party, celebrate

**Instructional materials:** Fabrics; Drum; Pictures; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing a favourite song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: clothes and food, songs and special days. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child joins in. Children: Child joins with a prompt.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Culture stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep the volume gentle. Use pictures and clean fabrics only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to teach a friend a song.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names clothes or foods from home; joins a song; moves to a beat; talks about a special day.

- **Ask:** What do you like to sing?
  **Expected response:** A song name.
- **Ask:** What do you eat?
  **Expected response:** A food name.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 8
- Joins cultural activities with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep sharing family culture at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child joins in cultural activities" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
