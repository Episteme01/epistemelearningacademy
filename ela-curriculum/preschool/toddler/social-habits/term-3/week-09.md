# Toddler Social Habits — Term 3, Week 9: Independence

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W09 · **Week type:** consolidation · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Putting on shoes and bag; Washing and drying hands; Packing away and helping

## Learning objectives
- Children will do simple self-help tasks with less help.
- Children will say "I can do it".

**Key vocabulary:** shoes, bag, myself, dress, help, proud

**Content:** Putting on shoes and bag, washing and drying hands, and packing away and helping.

**Teaching activities:** Allow time; wait before helping; give just enough help; praise effort ("You did it yourself"); use real shoes, bags and jackets; set up a dress-up area; avoid taking over when a child struggles; link to routines at arrival and home time.

**Learner activities:** Do simple self-help tasks with less help and say "I can do it".

**Resources:** Shoes with Velcro and with laces for practice; Bags and jackets; A basin and soap; Baskets; A dressing board

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child does one self-help task with little support and says "I can do it".

## Expected outcomes
- Does one self-help task with little support.
- Says "I can do it".

**Cross-curricular connections:** Physical Development: fine motor skills; Literacy: I can do it; Numeracy: pairs of shoes

## Day 1 (Monday): Shoes and bag

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W09-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Putting on shoes and bag

### Learning objectives
- Children will put on shoes and a bag with less help.

### Success criteria
- Puts on shoes with Velcro with little help
- Puts a bag on their back

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt many habits and helped at home and school.

**Key vocabulary:** shoes, bag, myself, dress, help, proud

**Instructional materials:** Shoes with Velcro; Bags; A mat

### Starter
Show a pair of shoes and say "We can put on our shoes by ourselves."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to put the right shoe on the right foot and close the Velcro. Children: Children watch and copy.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child try with their own shoes. Children: Child puts on shoes.

Step 3. Teacher: Show how to put a bag on both shoulders. Children: Child puts on a bag.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "I can do it." Children: Children repeat I can do it.

### Practical activity: Shoes and bag
1. Sit on the mat.
2. Put on your shoes.
3. Put on your bag.

**Safety:** Wait before helping and keep floors clear of tripping hazards. Help gently and never rush children.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Help with the first step and let the child do the rest.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to help a friend with Velcro.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do you put on?
  **Expected response:** Shoes and bag.
- **Ask:** Can you do it?
  **Expected response:** Yes, I can do it.

### Expected outcomes
- Puts on shoes with little help
- Puts on a bag

### Conclusion
Stand up and say "I did it."

### Home connection
Give your child time to put on shoes and a bag by themselves.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "put on shoes and a bag with less help" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Washing and drying by myself

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W09-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Washing and drying hands

### Learning objectives
- Children will wash and dry their hands with little help.

### Success criteria
- Washes hands with little help
- Dries hands on a towel

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt many habits and helped at home and school.

**Key vocabulary:** shoes, bag, myself, dress, help, proud

**Instructional materials:** A basin and soap; Towels; A step stool

### Starter
Show clean hands and say "We can wash our hands by ourselves."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Remind children of the steps: wet, soap, rub, rinse and dry. Children: Children say the steps.

Step 2. Teacher: Watch each child wash with a song. Children: Child washes with the song.

Step 3. Teacher: Help only where needed. Children: Child dries hands.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "I did it myself." Children: Children say I did it.

### Practical activity: Wash by myself
1. Wet your hands.
2. Use soap and rub.
3. Rinse and dry.

**Safety:** Supervise closely and wipe spills at once. Use mild soap and a safe step stool.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Wash together and name each step.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say the steps to a friend.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we use?
  **Expected response:** Soap and water.
- **Ask:** Can you do it?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Washes hands with little help
- Dries hands

### Conclusion
Show clean hands and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Let your child wash hands independently while you watch.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 4 (Thursday): Dressing up

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W09-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Putting on shoes and bag

### Learning objectives
- Children will practise putting on and taking off clothing in play.

### Success criteria
- Puts on a jacket or hat
- Takes it off

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt many habits and helped at home and school.

**Key vocabulary:** shoes, bag, myself, dress, help, proud

**Instructional materials:** Dress-up clothes with big buttons or Velcro; A mirror; A basket

### Starter
Show a box of dress-up clothes and say "Let us put on our clothes by ourselves."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to put on a jacket and do up Velcro. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child choose and put on a piece of clothing. Children: Child dresses up.

Step 3. Teacher: Look in the mirror and say "I did it." Children: Child looks in the mirror.

Step 4. Teacher: Take off the clothing and put it away. Children: Child puts the clothing in the basket.

### Practical activity: Dress-up play
1. Choose a piece of clothing.
2. Put it on.
3. Take it off and put it away.

**Safety:** Use clean clothes only and avoid anything with cords or ties around necks. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the sleeve and let the child push their arm through.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to help a friend with a button or Velcro.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did you put on?
  **Expected response:** A jacket or hat.
- **Ask:** Can you take it off?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Puts on and takes off clothing
- Says I did it

### Conclusion
Tidy the dress-up box and say well done.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 9 independence check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W09-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Packing away and helping

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child manages self-help tasks.

### Success criteria
- Does a self-help task with little support
- Says I can do it

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt many habits and helped at home and school.

**Key vocabulary:** shoes, bag, myself, dress, help, proud

**Instructional materials:** Shoes; Bags; Basin and soap; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the I can do it song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: shoes and bag, washing and packing. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child manages. Children: Child tries with little help.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Independence stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station closely and wipe spills at once. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show a friend how to do a task.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: puts on shoes; washes and dries hands; packs a bag; says I can do it.

- **Ask:** What can you do by yourself?
  **Expected response:** Wash hands or put on shoes.
- **Ask:** How do you feel?
  **Expected response:** Proud.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 9
- Does self-help tasks with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep letting your child do things for themselves.

- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child manages self-help tasks" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
