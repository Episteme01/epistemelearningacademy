# Toddler Social Habits — Term 3, Week 5: Safety

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W05 · **Week type:** consolidation · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Holding hands near roads; Stop, look and listen; Safe walking

## Learning objectives
- Children will hold an adult’s hand near roads.
- Children will stop at a pretend kerb and look and listen.

**Key vocabulary:** road, car, stop, look, listen, hand

**Content:** Holding hands near roads, stop, look and listen, and safe walking.

**Teaching activities:** Always practise in a safe, closed play space with chalk roads and toy cars; never practise on a real road with children as the lesson; stress holding an adult’s hand; use short rules; link to what families do on the way to school; invite parents to hold hands at the gate.

**Learner activities:** Hold an adult’s hand near roads and stop, look and listen at a pretend kerb.

**Resources:** Chalk to draw roads; Toy cars; A stop sign; Pictures of roads and crossings; A road safety song

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child holds a hand to walk and stops at a pretend kerb on request.

## Expected outcomes
- Holds an adult’s hand to walk.
- Stops at a pretend kerb on request.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: safety words; Physical Development: stopping and starting; Numeracy: counting cars

## Day 1 (Monday): Holding hands near roads

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W05-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Holding hands near roads

### Learning objectives
- Children will hold an adult’s hand when walking near roads.

### Success criteria
- Holds an adult’s hand
- Says hold hands

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt safety at school and know community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** road, car, stop, look, listen, hand

**Instructional materials:** Chalk road on the ground; A picture of a road; A song

### Starter
Draw a chalk road and say "Roads are for cars. Children hold a grown-up’s hand."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to hold an adult’s hand firmly. Children: Children hold a partner’s hand.

Step 2. Teacher: Walk beside the chalk road holding hands. Children: Children walk with a partner.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Always hold hands near roads." Children: Children repeat hold hands.

Step 4. Teacher: Stop and start with hands held. Children: Children stop and wait.

### Practical activity: Hold hands on the road
1. Hold a partner’s hand.
2. Walk by the chalk road.
3. Stop and wait.

**Safety:** Practise only in a closed safe area, never on a real road. An adult must hold every child’s hand.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and walk together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell a friend to hold hands.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we hold near roads?
  **Expected response:** A grown-up’s hand.
- **Ask:** Are roads safe to run on?
  **Expected response:** No.

### Expected outcomes
- Holds an adult’s hand
- Says hold hands

### Conclusion
Hold hands and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Hold your child’s hand firmly near roads and say "Hold hands."

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Stop, look and listen

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W05-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Stop, look and listen

### Learning objectives
- Children will stop, look and listen at a pretend kerb.

### Success criteria
- Stops at a line
- Looks and listens with an adult

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt safety at school and know community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** road, car, stop, look, listen, hand

**Instructional materials:** A line of tape as a kerb; A toy car sound; A stop sign

### Starter
Put a tape line on the floor and say "This is the kerb. We stop at the kerb."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to stop at the kerb, look and listen. Children: Children watch and copy.

Step 2. Teacher: Play a car sound and say "A car is coming. Wait." Children: Children wait at the line.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "When it is safe, we walk with a grown-up." Children: Children hold hands.

Step 4. Teacher: Practise again with each child. Children: Child stops and looks.

### Practical activity: Stop at the kerb
1. Stand at the line.
2. Look and listen.
3. Hold hands and walk.

**Safety:** Use a floor line only and never use a real road. Supervise every child and hold hands for each crossing.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Stand at the line and look together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the traffic helper.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do at the kerb?
  **Expected response:** Stop, look and listen.
- **Ask:** Who do we walk with?
  **Expected response:** A grown-up.

### Expected outcomes
- Stops at a pretend kerb
- Looks and listens with an adult

### Conclusion
Stop, look and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Practise stop, look and listen when you walk with your child.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Safe walking

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W05-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Safe walking

### Learning objectives
- Children will practise walking safely in a line with an adult.

### Success criteria
- Walks beside an adult
- Stays on the pavement

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt safety at school and know community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** road, car, stop, look, listen, hand

**Instructional materials:** A chalk pavement and road; Toy cars; A hat or bag to carry

### Starter
Draw a chalk pavement and road and say "We walk on the pavement, not the road."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the pavement and the road. Children: Children look and point.

Step 2. Teacher: Walk along the pavement with a partner. Children: Children walk in pairs.

Step 3. Teacher: Stop at the corner and look both ways. Children: Children stop and look.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Safe walking." Children: Children repeat safe walking.

### Practical activity: Pavement walk
1. Walk on the pavement.
2. Hold hands.
3. Stop at the corner.

**Safety:** Use chalk lines in a closed area only and keep an adult beside each pair. Never practise near real traffic.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Walk with the child holding hands.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a pair along the pavement.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where do we walk?
  **Expected response:** On the pavement.
- **Ask:** What do we do at the corner?
  **Expected response:** Stop and look.

### Expected outcomes
- Walks beside an adult
- Stays on the pavement

### Conclusion
Walk back together and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Walk on the pavement together and talk about safety.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "practise walking safely in a line with an adult" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Traffic play

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W05-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Stop, look and listen

### Learning objectives
- Children will play a traffic game that uses stop and go.

### Success criteria
- Stops and goes on signal
- Takes a turn as driver

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt safety at school and know community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** road, car, stop, look, listen, hand

**Instructional materials:** Paper plates as steering wheels; A stop sign and a go sign; Chalk roads

### Starter
Give out paper plate steering wheels and say "Today we are drivers. Stop when I show stop."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the stop and go signs and say what each means. Children: Children look and repeat.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children drive around the chalk road. Children: Children drive and stop.

Step 3. Teacher: Change the signs quickly and slowly. Children: Children stop and go.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child hold the sign. Children: Child shows the sign.

### Practical activity: Drivers on the road
1. Drive on the chalk road.
2. Stop at the sign.
3. Go when the sign says go.

**Safety:** Keep speeds slow and the area clear. Stop the game if children run into each other.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Drive beside the child and show the signs.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the road helper with the signs.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do at stop?
  **Expected response:** Stop.
- **Ask:** What do we do at go?
  **Expected response:** Go slowly.

### Expected outcomes
- Stops and goes on signal
- Takes a turn as the driver

### Conclusion
Park the cars and say well done.

### Home connection
Play stop and go games at home.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 5 road safety check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W05-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Holding hands near roads

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child follows road safety rules.

### Success criteria
- Holds an adult’s hand
- Stops at a pretend kerb

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt safety at school and know community helpers.

**Key vocabulary:** road, car, stop, look, listen, hand

**Instructional materials:** Chalk road; Signs; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the road safety song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: hold hands, kerb and traffic game. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child follows the rule. Children: Child follows with a prompt.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Road safety stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and use a closed safe area only. Never practise on real roads.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to remind a friend.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: holds an adult’s hand; stops at a kerb; looks and listens; walks safely.

- **Ask:** What do we hold near roads?
  **Expected response:** A grown-up’s hand.
- **Ask:** What do we do at the kerb?
  **Expected response:** Stop, look and listen.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 5
- Follows road safety rules with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say stay safe.

### Home connection
Keep practising road safety with your child.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
