# Toddler Social Habits — Term 3, Week 4: Community

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W04 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Teachers and nurses; Drivers, farmers and market sellers; Thanking helpers

## Learning objectives
- Children will name community helpers and what they do.
- Children will thank a helper.

**Key vocabulary:** nurse, teacher, driver, farmer, helper, thank you

**Content:** Teachers and nurses, drivers, farmers and market sellers, and thanking helpers.

**Teaching activities:** Use local helpers children know (the nurse at the clinic, the bus driver, the market seller, the farmer, the gateman); invite a helper to visit if possible; keep role play positive; avoid gender stereotypes about jobs; use real objects such as a stethoscope toy.

**Learner activities:** Name community helpers and what they do and say thank you to a helper.

**Resources:** Pictures of community helpers; Dress-up items and hats; Toy medical kit; Toy vegetables and a play market; A thank-you card

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child names two helpers and says thank you to a helper.

## Expected outcomes
- Names two or more community helpers.
- Says thank you to a helper.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Understanding the World: people and jobs; Literacy: job words; Creative Development: role play

## Day 1 (Monday): Teachers and nurses

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W04-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Teachers and nurses

### Learning objectives
- Children will name teachers and nurses and say how they help.

### Success criteria
- Names a teacher and a nurse
- Says what each does

**Previous knowledge:** Children can cooperate and show respect.

**Key vocabulary:** nurse, teacher, driver, farmer, helper, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Pictures of a teacher and a nurse; A toy medical kit; Hats

### Starter
Show a picture of a nurse and say "A nurse helps us when we are sick."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show pictures of a teacher and a nurse and say what they do. Children: Children look and repeat the job.

Step 2. Teacher: Show a toy medical kit and name each item. Children: Children touch and name items.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children be nurse and patient with a doll. Children: Child plays nurse or patient.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Thank you, nurse. Thank you, teacher." Children: Children say thank you.

### Practical activity: Nurse role play
1. Look at the kit.
2. Be the nurse.
3. Care for a doll.

**Safety:** Use only a toy kit with no real medicines or sharp items. Keep play gentle and wash hands after.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play together and name the job.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say how a nurse helps.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who helps us when we are sick?
  **Expected response:** The nurse.
- **Ask:** Who teaches us?
  **Expected response:** The teacher.

### Expected outcomes
- Names a teacher and a nurse
- Says how each helps

### Conclusion
Say "Thank you, helpers" and wave.

### Home connection
Talk about the nurse and teacher your child knows.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "name teachers and nurses and say how they help" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- NURSE
- TEACHER

**Picture suggestions:**
- A nurse
- A teacher

**Song or rhyme:** Helpers, helpers, helpers in our town.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): The market seller

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W04-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Drivers, farmers and market sellers

### Learning objectives
- Children will play at a market and use manners with the seller.

### Success criteria
- Plays a buyer or seller
- Says please and thank you

**Previous knowledge:** Children can cooperate and show respect.

**Key vocabulary:** nurse, teacher, driver, farmer, helper, thank you

**Instructional materials:** A play market stall; Toy fruit and vegetables; Play money or tokens; Baskets

### Starter
Set up a play market and say "The seller helps us get food. We say please and thank you."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to ask the seller for a tomato. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children buy and sell toy food. Children: Child buys or sells.

Step 3. Teacher: Remind children of manners. Children: Child says please and thank you.

Step 4. Teacher: Swap roles so everyone has a go. Children: Child swaps and plays again.

### Practical activity: Market play
1. Set up the stall.
2. Buy and sell food.
3. Say please and thank you.

**Safety:** Use only toy food and tokens too large to swallow. Supervise swapping and keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Be the seller while the child is the buyer.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the seller and give change with tokens.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who sells food?
  **Expected response:** The market seller.
- **Ask:** What do you say?
  **Expected response:** Please and thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Plays a buyer or seller
- Uses manners at the market

### Conclusion
Tidy the market and say "Thank you."

### Home connection
Visit a market with your child and greet the seller.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "play at a market and use manners with the seller" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Thanking helpers

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W04-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Thanking helpers

### Learning objectives
- Children will make a thank-you card for a community helper.

### Success criteria
- Makes a card with marks or drawing
- Says thank you to a helper

**Previous knowledge:** Children can cooperate and show respect.

**Key vocabulary:** nurse, teacher, driver, farmer, helper, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Folded paper cards; Crayons; Stickers; A list of helpers

### Starter
Say "Today we make a card to say thank you to a helper."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show a sample card and say who it is for. Children: Children look and listen.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child choose a helper and draw or mark a card. Children: Child makes a card.

Step 3. Teacher: Help each child add their name. Children: Child adds a mark.

Step 4. Teacher: Deliver the cards to the helpers or display them. Children: Child gives or points to the card.

### Practical activity: Thank-you cards
1. Choose a helper.
2. Draw on the card.
3. Give or show the card.

**Safety:** Use non-toxic crayons and keep stickers away from mouths. Deliver cards with adult supervision.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Draw together and choose the helper.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what the helper does.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is your card for?
  **Expected response:** A helper.
- **Ask:** What do you say?
  **Expected response:** Thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes a thank-you card
- Says thank you to a helper

### Conclusion
Hold the cards up and say "Thank you, helpers."

### Home connection

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 4 helpers check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W04-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Thanking helpers

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child talks about helpers.

### Success criteria
- Names two helpers
- Says thank you

**Previous knowledge:** Children can cooperate and show respect.

**Key vocabulary:** nurse, teacher, driver, farmer, helper, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Pictures; Hats; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the helper song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: nurse, market and card. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "Who is the helper?" Children: Child answers with a word or action.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Helper stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use toys and safe materials only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to name three helpers.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names community helpers; says what they do; plays a helper role; says thank you.

- **Ask:** Who helps us?
  **Expected response:** Nurse, driver or teacher.
- **Ask:** What do we say?
  **Expected response:** Thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 4
- Names helpers with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep noticing and thanking helpers.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child talks about helpers" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**
- Helpers
