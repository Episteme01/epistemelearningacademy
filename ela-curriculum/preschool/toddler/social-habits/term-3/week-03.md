# Toddler Social Habits — Term 3, Week 3: Cooperation

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W03 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Building together; Carrying together; Group games

## Learning objectives
- Children will work with a friend or a small group on a simple task.
- Children will say "we did it".

**Key vocabulary:** together, team, build, carry, help, we

**Content:** Building together, carrying together, and group games.

**Teaching activities:** Choose tasks that need two or more children; give each child a clear part; praise the team ("We did it"); keep groups small; step back as children cooperate; rotate partners.

**Learner activities:** Work with a friend on a simple task and say "we did it".

**Resources:** Large cardboard boxes; Large blocks; A light mat to carry; A parachute; A teamwork song

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child works with a friend on a simple task and joins a group game.

## Expected outcomes
- Works with a friend on a simple task.
- Joins a group game.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Physical Development: carrying; Numeracy: counting together; Literacy: we words

## Day 1 (Monday): Building together

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W03-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Building together

### Learning objectives
- Children will build something together with a friend.

### Success criteria
- Adds to a shared building
- Says we built it

**Previous knowledge:** Children can try new things and calm big feelings.

**Key vocabulary:** together, team, build, carry, help, we

**Instructional materials:** Large blocks; Large cardboard boxes; A puppet

### Starter
Show a pile of blocks and say "One child can build a little. Two children can build a lot."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how two children pass blocks to build a wall. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Pair children and give them blocks. Children: Child builds with a friend.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "What are you building?" Children: Child says a word or points.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We built it together." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Build a wall
1. Sit with a friend.
2. Pass blocks.
3. Build together.

**Safety:** Use large light blocks only and keep towers low. Seat children safely and supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Build the first layer together.
- **Extension:** Ask the pair to name what they built.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did you build?
  **Expected response:** A wall or house.
- **Ask:** Who built it?
  **Expected response:** We did.

### Expected outcomes
- Builds with a friend
- Says we built it

### Conclusion
Look at the building and say "Well done, team."

### Home connection
Build with blocks together at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "build something together with a friend" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**

**Picture suggestions:**

**Song or rhyme:** Together, together, we can do it together.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Carrying together

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W03-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Carrying together

### Learning objectives
- Children will carry a light object together.

### Success criteria
- Carries a light object with a friend
- Says we carried it

**Previous knowledge:** Children can try new things and calm big feelings.

**Key vocabulary:** together, team, build, carry, help, we

**Instructional materials:** A light mat or sheet; A large soft toy; A light box

### Starter
Show a light box and say "It is too big for one. Two children can carry it."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how two children hold opposite sides. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Pair children and let them carry the box. Children: Child carries the box with a friend.

Step 3. Teacher: Walk slowly across the room. Children: Children walk together.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We carried it together." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Carry the box
1. Hold the box with a friend.
2. Walk slowly.
3. Put it down gently.

**Safety:** Use a very light box and keep the route clear. Supervise every pair and never carry anything heavy.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Carry the box together with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the pair to carry the box around a cone.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who carried the box?
  **Expected response:** We did.
- **Ask:** Was it too big for one?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Carries with a friend
- Says we carried it

### Conclusion
Put the box away and say "Team."

### Home connection
Carry light things together at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "carry a light object together" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Group games

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W03-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Group games

### Learning objectives
- Children will join a group game and cooperate.

### Success criteria
- Joins a group game
- Waits and takes turns

**Previous knowledge:** Children can try new things and calm big feelings.

**Key vocabulary:** together, team, build, carry, help, we

**Instructional materials:** A parachute or large cloth; A soft ball; A circle song

### Starter
Hold the cloth and say "Hold on tight. Everyone together."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to hold the cloth edge and lift together. Children: Children hold the cloth.

Step 2. Teacher: Lift and lower the cloth with a song. Children: Children lift together.

Step 3. Teacher: Roll a ball on the cloth. Children: Children watch and shake gently.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We played together." Children: Children cheer.

### Practical activity: Cloth game
1. Hold the edge.
2. Lift together.
3. Roll the ball.

**Safety:** Use a soft light cloth and a very soft ball. Keep the group small and away from furniture.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the cloth beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead the lifting count.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did we do?
  **Expected response:** We lifted the cloth.
- **Ask:** Who played?
  **Expected response:** All of us.

### Expected outcomes
- Joins a group game
- Takes turns in the group

### Conclusion
Fold the cloth and say well done.

### Home connection
Play a simple group game with the family.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "join a group game and cooperate" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Team project

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W03-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Building together

### Learning objectives
- Children will complete a small project together.

### Success criteria
- Works with others to finish a project
- Says we did it

**Previous knowledge:** Children can try new things and calm big feelings.

**Key vocabulary:** together, team, build, carry, help, we

**Instructional materials:** A big sheet of paper; Crayons or paint; Collage materials

### Starter
Show a large sheet of paper and say "Today we make one big picture together."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Put the paper on the floor and show each child a space. Children: Children choose a space.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children add colour or collage materials. Children: Child adds to the picture.

Step 3. Teacher: Talk about the picture. Children: Child says what they added.

Step 4. Teacher: Hang the picture and say "We made it together." Children: Children cheer.

### Practical activity: Team picture
1. Sit around the paper.
2. Add your part.
3. Look at the big picture.

**Safety:** Use non-toxic washable materials and supervise closely. Keep small items out of mouths.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Add your part beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what their part is.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did we make?
  **Expected response:** A big picture.
- **Ask:** Who made it?
  **Expected response:** We did.

### Expected outcomes
- Contributes to a team project
- Says we did it

### Conclusion
Wash hands and say "Team."

### Home connection

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 3 teamwork check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W03-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Group games

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child works with others.

### Success criteria
- Works with a friend
- Joins a group game

**Previous knowledge:** Children can try new things and calm big feelings.

**Key vocabulary:** together, team, build, carry, help, we

**Instructional materials:** Blocks; A box; A cloth; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the teamwork song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: build, carry and cloth. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how children cooperate. Children: Child works with a friend.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Teamwork stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station closely and keep floors clear. Use light objects only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to include a quiet friend.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: builds with a friend; carries with a friend; joins a group game; says we did it.

- **Ask:** Who did it?
  **Expected response:** We did.
- **Ask:** What did we do?
  **Expected response:** We worked together.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 3
- Works with others with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep doing things together at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child works with others" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Teamwork
