# Toddler Social Habits — Term 3, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising feelings and bravery; Revising safety and helping; Celebration and end-of-year record

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Year learning through games and a class celebration.
- Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note.

**Key vocabulary:** brave, calm, team, helper, safe, thank you

**Content:** Revising feelings and bravery, safety and helping, and celebrating the year with end-of-year records and a transition note.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly; record what children can do; invite parents to the celebration; complete the transition note honestly for the Nursery 1 teacher; handle any safeguarding or welfare concern through the school’s policy and not through the transition note alone.

**Learner activities:** Show learning from the year through games and a class celebration.

**Resources:** Feelings cards; Calm corner; Helper hats; Road safety signs; Certificates or stickers; End-of-year record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s end-of-year social learning and complete the transition note.

## Expected outcomes
- Takes part confidently in familiar routines and games.
- Each child has an end-of-year observation record.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and stories; Creative Development: music; Numeracy: counting and turns

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising feelings and bravery

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising feelings and bravery

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit feelings, calming, trying and teamwork through games.

### Success criteria
- Names a feeling and uses a calming idea
- Tries a new activity

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about feelings, bravery, teamwork, helpers, safety, gratitude, culture and independence this year.

**Key vocabulary:** brave, calm, team, helper, safe, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Feelings cards; A calm corner; Cups; A cloth

### Starter
Say "Today we play our favourite feelings and trying games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play show me a feeling with the feelings cards. Children: Child shows a feeling face.

Step 2. Teacher: Practise slow breathing with a teddy. Children: Child breathes slowly.

Step 3. Teacher: Try stacking cups and say I can try. Children: Child tries and says I can try.

Step 4. Teacher: Play the cloth game together. Children: Child lifts the cloth with friends.

### Practical activity: Feelings and trying games
1. Set up three areas.
2. Children play each for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes responses.

**Safety:** Supervise each area closely and keep floors clear. Stop any game that upsets a child.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child and use one game.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a calming game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How do we calm down?
  **Expected response:** Breathe slowly.
- **Ask:** What do you say before you try?
  **Expected response:** I can try.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows feelings and calming ideas
- Tries and keeps going

### Conclusion
Take a slow breath and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep naming feelings and praising trying.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit feelings, calming, trying and teamwork through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Year-end stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising feelings and bravery

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s end-of-year social learning at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows manners, feelings, teamwork and independence

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about feelings, bravery, teamwork, helpers, safety, gratitude, culture and independence this year.

**Key vocabulary:** brave, calm, team, helper, safe, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Six play stations; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Say "Today we play at six stations and show what we know."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: manners, feelings, teamwork, safety, helping and independence. Children: Children rotate every three minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Social stations
1. Set up six stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep floors clear and use large safe toys only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: uses manners; names feelings and calms with help; works with others; follows safety rules; helps; does self-help tasks.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a game.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an end-of-year observation record
- Shows familiar social skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite game at home during the holiday.

## Day 4 (Thursday): Class celebration

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Celebration and end-of-year record

### Learning objectives

Say "Today we celebrate our year. Let us sing our favourite songs."

Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite songs and rhymes with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Invite children to show their work to a visitor or parent. Children: Child shows their work.

## Day 5 (Friday): Transition note and records

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T3-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Celebration and end-of-year record

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will complete a transition note for each child for the Nursery 1 teacher.

### Success criteria
- Records what each child can do
- Records a next step

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about feelings, bravery, teamwork, helpers, safety, gratitude, culture and independence this year.

**Key vocabulary:** brave, calm, team, helper, safe, thank you

**Instructional materials:** Transition note template; Term records; Children’s portfolios; A quiet table

### Starter
Today the children enjoy free play with familiar materials while the teacher completes the transition notes.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set out free play with blocks, a play house, books and a play market. Children: Children play freely and happily.

Step 2. Teacher: Review each child’s three term records and portfolio. Children: Children play while the teacher reviews.

Step 3. Teacher: Complete the note: strengths, friendships, feelings, routines and next steps. Children: Children play and talk with the helper.

### Practical activity: Free play and records
1. Set out four play areas with a helper.
2. The teacher reviews records at a quiet table.
3. The teacher completes each transition note.

**Safety:** A second adult must supervise play while the teacher completes the notes. Keep all areas safe and tidy.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer familiar toys and a helper nearby.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to help a friend tidy up.

### Assessment
Write for each child: how they settle, relate to others, manage feelings, follow routines, look after themselves and keep safe; their interests; any support needed; and a next step. Use the four-level scale and facts you have seen, and pass any welfare concern to the safeguarding lead separately.

- **Ask:** What do you like to play?
  **Expected response:** A favourite game or toy.
- **Ask:** What did you enjoy this year?
  **Expected response:** A word, game or song.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a transition note for each child
- Plays happily in familiar activities

### Conclusion
Tidy up together and say goodbye to the year.

### Home connection
Share the end-of-year report with your child and keep practising kind habits.
