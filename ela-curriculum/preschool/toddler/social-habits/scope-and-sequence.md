# Toddler Social Habits — annual scope and sequence

Three terms of ten teaching weeks. Five planned lessons a week (150 in the year). Dates are configured by ELA's academic calendar, not written here.

## Term 1: Term 1 focuses on belonging and basic habits: settling in, greetings, please and thank you, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness, toileting and safety

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Belonging | My name and my friends; My teacher and my classroom; Saying goodbye and hello again | Children will say or show their name and recognise their teacher. Children will separate from a parent with support and return happily. | name, teacher, friend, school, class, goodbye | Responds to their name. Settles in the classroom with adult support. |
| 2 | Greetings | Saying good morning; Greeting my teacher and friends; Saying goodbye and waving | Children will greet people with words or a wave. Children will say goodbye politely. | hello, good morning, goodbye, wave, smile, friend | Greets the teacher with a word or gesture. Waves goodbye. |
| 3 | Good manners | Saying please; Saying thank you; Polite asking and taking | Children will say please when asking for something. Children will say thank you when they receive something. | please, thank you, give, take, ask, kind | Says please with a prompt. Says thank you with a prompt. |
| 4 | Feelings | Happy and sad; Angry and scared; Showing feelings with faces | Children will name happy, sad, angry and scared. Children will show feelings with their faces and bodies. | happy, sad, angry, scared, face, feel | Names two or more feelings. Shows a feeling with the face. |
| 5 | Routines | Circle time and listening; Lining up and walking; Tidy-up time | Children will follow simple classroom routines. Children will help to tidy up when asked. | line, sit, listen, tidy, circle, walk | Sits in the circle and joins a line with support. Helps to tidy toys. |
| 6 | Sharing | Sharing toys; Taking turns; Waiting for my turn | Children will share a toy with a friend with support. Children will take turns in a simple game. | share, turn, wait, my turn, your turn, toy | Shares a toy with adult support. Waits for a turn for a short time. |
| 7 | Kindness | Kind hands; Kind words; Helping a friend | Children will use gentle hands with friends and toys. Children will say a kind word or help a friend. | kind, gentle, help, hug, nice, friend | Uses gentle hands with a friend. Says or does a kind thing with support. |
| 8 | Health habits | Telling the teacher I need the toilet; Washing my hands; Wiping and flushing with help | Children will tell an adult when they need the toilet. Children will wash their hands after using the toilet. | toilet, wash, soap, flush, clean, towel | Tells an adult they need the toilet with a word or sign. Washes hands with support. |
| 9 | Safety | Staying with my teacher; Hot, sharp and dirty things; Safe play | Children will stay with their group and an adult. Children will say stop or hot for dangerous things. | safe, stop, hot, danger, together, walk | Stays with the teacher in the compound. Stops when told "stop". |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising greetings and manners; Revising feelings and routines; Revising sharing and safety | Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | hello, please, thank you, share, happy, safe | Completes familiar routines with growing confidence. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. |

## Term 2: Term 2 focuses on relationships and responsibility: family, friends, listening, saying sorry, helping, honesty, looking after things, eating manners and respect

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Family | Who is in my family; What my family does for me; Showing love to my family | Children will name people in their family. Children will say one way they show love to their family. | mother, father, baby, grandma, family, love | Names at least two family members. Shows or says love to a family member. |
| 2 | Friends | Playing with friends; Being a good friend; Making friends | Children will play beside and with friends. Children will say what a good friend does. | friend, play, together, kind, share, help | Plays with a friend for a short time. Says one thing a good friend does. |
| 3 | Listening | Listening ears; Following one instruction; Following two instructions | Children will stop and listen when an adult speaks. Children will follow simple one-step and two-step instructions. | listen, ears, look, do, stop, quiet | Stops and looks at the teacher when called. Follows a one-step instruction. |
| 4 | Good manners | Saying sorry; Saying "it is okay"; Making things better | Children will say sorry when they have hurt someone, with support. Children will say "it is okay" and help to make things better. | sorry, hurt, okay, forgive, help, kind | Says sorry with a prompt. Helps a friend who is upset. |
| 5 | Responsibility | Helping my teacher; Helping at home; Our class helpers | Children will do small helping jobs. Children will say how they helped. | help, job, carry, clean, tidy, helper | Does a small job with support. Says "I helped". |
| 6 | Honesty | What is true; Telling the truth when something goes wrong; Being brave to say the truth | Children will say what is true about simple pictures and events. Children will tell an adult the truth about a small accident with support. | true, truth, honest, oops, brave, tell | Says what is true about a simple event. Tells an adult about a spill or mistake. |
| 7 | Responsibility | My bag and my things; Caring for toys and books; Putting things back | Children will care for toys, books and their own belongings. Children will put things back in the right place. | bag, book, toy, gentle, back, place | Puts a toy back where it belongs. Handles a book gently. |
| 8 | Good manners | Washing hands before eating; Sitting and eating nicely; Not wasting food | Children will wash hands before eating and sit to eat. Children will say please and thank you at mealtimes. | eat, plate, cup, wash, sit, thank you | Washes hands before eating with support. Sits to eat and says thank you. |
| 9 | Respect | Respecting elders and teachers; Respecting helpers; Respecting different people | Children will greet and speak kindly to elders and helpers. Children will accept that people are different and treat them kindly. | respect, elder, helper, different, kind, greet | Greets an elder or helper in a family-appropriate way. Uses kind words with a child who looks different. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising family and friends; Revising manners and honesty; Revising helping and respect | Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record. | family, friend, sorry, truth, help, respect | Completes familiar social tasks with growing independence. Each child has an observation record for Term 2. |

## Term 3: Term 3 focuses on confidence and community: bravery, calming big feelings, teamwork, helpers, road safety, body safety, gratitude, culture and independence

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confidence | Trying something new; Saying "I can try"; Keeping going | Children will try a new activity with encouragement. Children will say "I can try" and keep going for a short time. | try, brave, new, again, can, proud | Tries a new activity with encouragement. Says "I can try" with a prompt. |
| 2 | Feelings | Big feelings in my body; Breathing and calming; Asking for help | Children will notice big feelings in their bodies. Children will use slow breathing or a calm place with an adult. | calm, breathe, cross, upset, help, quiet | Uses a calming strategy with adult support. Asks an adult for help when upset. |
| 3 | Cooperation | Building together; Carrying together; Group games | Children will work with a friend or a small group on a simple task. Children will say "we did it". | together, team, build, carry, help, we | Works with a friend on a simple task. Joins a group game. |
| 4 | Community | Teachers and nurses; Drivers, farmers and market sellers; Thanking helpers | Children will name community helpers and what they do. Children will thank a helper. | nurse, teacher, driver, farmer, helper, thank you | Names two or more community helpers. Says thank you to a helper. |
| 5 | Safety | Holding hands near roads; Stop, look and listen; Safe walking | Children will hold an adult’s hand near roads. Children will stop at a pretend kerb and look and listen. | road, car, stop, look, listen, hand | Holds an adult’s hand to walk. Stops at a pretend kerb on request. |
| 6 | Safety | My body is mine; Saying no and telling a trusted adult; My safe grown-ups | Children will say "my body is mine" and say "stop" to touch they do not like. Children will name trusted adults they can tell. | body, mine, stop, no, tell, safe | Says stop to touch they do not like. Names a trusted adult. |
| 7 | Values | Saying thank you for gifts and help; Giving to others; Sharing food and toys | Children will say thank you and say what they are thankful for. Children will give or share with others. | thank you, give, gift, thankful, share, kind | Says what they are thankful for. Gives or shares an item with a friend. |
| 8 | Community | Our clothes and food; Songs and dances; Festivals and special days | Children will enjoy and name familiar cultural clothes, foods, songs and dances. Children will say that families celebrate in different ways. | dance, song, food, clothes, party, celebrate | Joins in a song or dance. Names a food or clothing from home. |
| 9 | Independence | Putting on shoes and bag; Washing and drying hands; Packing away and helping | Children will do simple self-help tasks with less help. Children will say "I can do it". | shoes, bag, myself, dress, help, proud | Does one self-help task with little support. Says "I can do it". |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising feelings and bravery; Revising safety and helping; Celebration and end-of-year record | Children will show Year learning through games and a class celebration. Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note. | brave, calm, team, helper, safe, thank you | Takes part confidently in familiar routines and games. Each child has an end-of-year observation record. |
