# Toddler Social Habits — Term 1, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising greetings and manners; Revising feelings and routines; Revising sharing and safety

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record.

**Key vocabulary:** hello, please, thank you, share, happy, safe

**Content:** Revising greetings and manners, feelings and routines, and sharing and safety, with end-of-term records.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly during play; record what children can do without pressure; share records with parents in plain language with a strength, a next step and a home idea.

**Learner activities:** Show learning from the term through play stations and a celebration.

**Resources:** Puppets; Feelings cards; A play shop; A toy door; Baskets; Stickers; Class record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s Term 1 social learning.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar routines with growing confidence.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 1.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs; Numeracy: counting and turns; Science: hygiene

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising greetings and manners

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising greetings and manners

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit greetings, please, thank you and good manners through games.

### Success criteria
- Greets with a word or wave
- Uses please and thank you with a prompt

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness, toileting and safety.

**Key vocabulary:** hello, please, thank you, share, happy, safe

**Instructional materials:** A puppet; A play shop; A toy door; A greeting song

### Starter
Sing the greeting song and say "Today we play our favourite manners games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the door greeting game with the puppet. Children: Child greets the puppet.

Step 2. Teacher: Play the shop game with please and thank you. Children: Child asks and thanks.

Step 3. Teacher: Play the goodbye game with waves. Children: Child waves goodbye.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Manners games
1. Set up three game areas.
2. Children play each for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes manners.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and keep floors clear. Use large toys only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play with the child and use one game.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we say in the morning?
  **Expected response:** Good morning.
- **Ask:** What are the magic words?
  **Expected response:** Please and thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows greetings and manners
- Uses magic words with a prompt

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say thank you.

### Home connection
Keep using greetings and magic words at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit greetings, please, thank you and good manners through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- HELLO
- PLEASE
- THANK YOU

**Picture suggestions:**
- A play shop
- A toy door

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Revising feelings and routines

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising feelings and routines

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit feelings, circle time, lining up and tidying through games.

### Success criteria
- Names a feeling
- Follows a simple routine

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness, toileting and safety.

**Key vocabulary:** hello, please, thank you, share, happy, safe

**Instructional materials:** Feelings cards; A mat; Baskets; Footprints

### Starter
Show a feelings card and say "How do you feel today?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play show me a feeling with the feelings cards. Children: Child shows a feeling face.

Step 2. Teacher: Sit in the circle and sing the circle song. Children: Children sit and sing.

Step 3. Teacher: Line up on footprints and walk. Children: Children walk in a line.

Step 4. Teacher: Tidy up with the tidy-up song. Children: Children put toys in baskets.

### Practical activity: Feelings and routines games
1. Show feelings faces.
2. Sit in the circle.
3. Tidy up together.

**Safety:** Keep walkways clear and supervise each game. Stop any game that upsets a child.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child and name each feeling.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead the line.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How do you feel?
  **Expected response:** A feeling word.
- **Ask:** What do we do at tidy-up?
  **Expected response:** Put toys away.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows feelings and routines
- Follows routines with support

### Conclusion
Smile and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Talk about feelings and keep simple routines at home.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Social stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising greetings and manners

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 1 social learning at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows manners, feelings and routines

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness, toileting and safety.

**Key vocabulary:** hello, please, thank you, share, happy, safe

**Instructional materials:** Six play stations; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Say "Today we play at six stations and show what we know."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: greetings, manners, feelings, sharing, kindness and hygiene. Children: Children rotate every three minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Social stations
1. Set up six stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep water shallow and floors clear and use only large toys.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: greets; uses please and thank you; names feelings; shares and takes turns; shows kindness; washes hands.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a game.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 1 observation record
- Shows familiar social skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite game at home during the holiday.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 1 social learning at play stations" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Revising sharing and safety

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising sharing and safety

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit sharing, taking turns, kindness and safety through games.

### Success criteria
- Shares and takes turns
- Stops on the word stop

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness, toileting and safety.

**Key vocabulary:** hello, please, thank you, share, happy, safe

**Instructional materials:** Blocks; A ball; A stop sign; A rope

### Starter
Say "Let us build a tower together and be safe."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Build a tower with blocks, taking turns. Children: Child adds a block and waits.

Step 2. Teacher: Play stop and go with a stop sign. Children: Children stop on stop.

Step 3. Teacher: Walk with the rope and count children. Children: Children walk with the rope.

Step 4. Teacher: Say a kind word to a friend. Children: Child says a kind word.

### Practical activity: Sharing and safety games
1. Build a tower.
2. Play stop and go.
3. Walk with the rope.

**Safety:** Use large, light blocks and keep floors clear. Supervise closely and keep a second adult at the rope walk.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Take turns together with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the stop sign holder.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do with toys?
  **Expected response:** Share them.
- **Ask:** What do we do at stop?
  **Expected response:** Stop still.

### Expected outcomes
- Shares and takes turns
- Follows safety rules with support

### Conclusion
Count the children and wave goodbye.

### Home connection

## Day 5 (Friday): Term celebration and records

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising feelings and routines

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate Term 1 with songs, and teachers will complete each child’s Term 1 record.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows something they enjoyed

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about greetings, manners, feelings, routines, sharing, kindness, toileting and safety.

**Key vocabulary:** hello, please, thank you, share, happy, safe

**Instructional materials:** Favourite songs; Stickers; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate our term. Let us sing our favourite songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite songs and rhymes with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Give each child a sticker and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

Step 3. Teacher: Complete each child’s record and next step. Children: Children play quietly.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing and clap.
3. Give out stickers.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Keep the floor clear and supervise all play.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and sing together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s Term 1 record from the term’s observations. Note a strength, a next step and a home idea for parents.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite thing?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** What do you like to do?
  **Expected response:** A game or song.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed Term 1 record
- Enjoys the celebration

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you next term."

### Home connection
Share the Term 1 record and keep practising manners over the holiday.
