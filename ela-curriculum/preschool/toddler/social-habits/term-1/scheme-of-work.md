# Toddler Social Habits — Term 1 scheme of work

| Week | Topic and subtopics | Learning objectives | Content | Teaching activities | Learner activities | Resources | Assessment | Expected outcomes | Cross-curricular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Belonging**: My name and my friends; My teacher and my classroom; Saying goodbye and hello again | Children will say or show their name and recognise their teacher. Children will separate from a parent with support and return happily. | Learning names, meeting the teacher and the classroom, and practising saying goodbye and hello again. | Greet every child by name at the door; keep the first days calm and predictable; allow a parent to stay briefly if needed; never rush a crying child; give a comfort object a safe place; sing the same songs every day. | Respond to their name, recognise the teacher and settle in the classroom with adult support. | Name cards with photographs; A welcome song; Soft toys and comfort objects; A hello-goodbye puppet; A picture timetable | Observe whether each child responds to their name, settles with support and says or waves goodbye. | Responds to their name. Settles in the classroom with adult support. | Literacy: names and songs; Numeracy: counting friends; Creative Development: singing |
| 2 | **Greetings**: Saying good morning; Greeting my teacher and friends; Saying goodbye and waving | Children will greet people with words or a wave. Children will say goodbye politely. | Saying good morning, greeting the teacher and friends, and saying goodbye with a wave. | Greet every child by name each morning and expect a response of any kind; model "Good morning" with eye contact; respect that families greet elders in different ways (a wave, a bow, a kneel or a handshake) and never correct a family’s greeting; praise every attempt. | Greet people with a word or a wave and say goodbye politely. | A greeting song; Puppets; Pictures of morning, afternoon and evening; A teacher’s bell | Observe whether each child greets the teacher with a word or gesture and waves goodbye. | Greets the teacher with a word or gesture. Waves goodbye. | Literacy: greeting words; Numeracy: counting the greeters; Creative Development: song and movement |
| 3 | **Good manners**: Saying please; Saying thank you; Polite asking and taking | Children will say please when asking for something. Children will say thank you when they receive something. | Saying please when asking, thank you when receiving, and polite asking and taking. | Model please and thank you constantly in daily routines; praise the words, not only the result; never force a child to say them; use a puppet to show the right and wrong way; link to snack time and play. | Say please and thank you with prompting in play and snack routines. | Snack items such as biscuits or fruit; A puppet; Toys; A picture of a child asking nicely; A magic words song | Observe whether each child says please and thank you with a prompt. | Says please with a prompt. Says thank you with a prompt. | Literacy: magic words; Numeracy: one each; Physical Development: giving and receiving |
| 4 | **Feelings**: Happy and sad; Angry and scared; Showing feelings with faces | Children will name happy, sad, angry and scared. Children will show feelings with their faces and bodies. | Naming happy and sad, angry and scared, and showing feelings with faces and bodies. | Use real faces, mirrors and picture cards; name feelings as they happen ("You look sad"); never say a feeling is wrong; keep feelings words simple; model calm responses; use a feelings chart every day. | Name happy, sad, angry and scared and show them with the face and body. | Feelings face cards; A mirror; A feelings chart; Pictures of children with different feelings; A puppet | Observe whether each child names two or more feelings and shows a feeling with their face. | Names two or more feelings. Shows a feeling with the face. | Literacy: feelings words; Creative Development: drama and faces; Numeracy: sorting feelings cards |
| 5 | **Routines**: Circle time and listening; Lining up and walking; Tidy-up time | Children will follow simple classroom routines. Children will help to tidy up when asked. | Circle time and listening, lining up and walking, and tidy-up time. | Use the same routine words and songs every day; show before you tell; give a warning before change; praise children who follow the routine; keep each routine short; let children have jobs. | Follow simple classroom routines such as sitting in the circle, lining up and tidying. | A mat for circle time; A tidy-up song; A picture timetable; Baskets for toys; A line of footprints on the floor | Observe whether each child sits in the circle, joins a line and helps to tidy with support. | Sits in the circle and joins a line with support. Helps to tidy toys. | Physical Development: walking in a line; Literacy: routine songs; Numeracy: counting in line |
| 6 | **Sharing**: Sharing toys; Taking turns; Waiting for my turn | Children will share a toy with a friend with support. Children will take turns in a simple game. | Sharing toys, taking turns, and waiting for my turn. | Expect toddlers to say "mine"; do not force sharing but teach turns with a timer or song; praise every turn taken; keep duplicate toys available; use "my turn, your turn" language; stay close to guide gently. | Share a toy with support, take turns in a simple game and wait for a short time. | Two of each toy; A ball; A sand timer or song; A puppet; A turn-taking mat with footprints | Observe whether each child shares with support and waits for a turn for a short time. | Shares a toy with adult support. Waits for a turn for a short time. | Numeracy: one each and first, next; Literacy: my turn and your turn; Physical Development: rolling and passing |
| 7 | **Kindness**: Kind hands; Kind words; Helping a friend | Children will use gentle hands with friends and toys. Children will say a kind word or help a friend. | Kind hands, kind words and helping a friend. | Teach "gentle hands" with a soft toy; notice and name every kind act ("You helped Ada, that was kind"); never label a child as unkind; show kind words with a puppet; use a kindness chart with stickers. | Use gentle hands, say a kind word and help a friend with support. | A soft toy or doll; A puppet; A kindness chart; Stickers; Pictures of kind actions | Observe whether each child uses gentle hands and says or does a kind thing with support. | Uses gentle hands with a friend. Says or does a kind thing with support. | Literacy: kind words; Creative Development: singing and acting; Numeracy: counting kind acts |
| 8 | **Health habits**: Telling the teacher I need the toilet; Washing my hands; Wiping and flushing with help | Children will tell an adult when they need the toilet. Children will wash their hands after using the toilet. | Telling the teacher when I need the toilet, washing my hands, and wiping and flushing with help. | Treat accidents as normal and never shame a child; ask every child regularly and let them go when they need to; use simple words agreed with parents; model hand washing with a song; ensure a second adult is available; follow the school hygiene and safeguarding policy for toilet help. | Tell an adult about toilet needs, use the toilet with support and wash hands with soap. | A potty or child toilet; A doll; Soap, a basin and towels; A hand washing song; Pictures of toilet steps; Spare clothes | Observe whether each child tells an adult they need the toilet and washes hands with support. | Tells an adult they need the toilet with a word or sign. Washes hands with support. | Science: keeping clean; Literacy: toilet words; Physical Development: self-care |
| 9 | **Safety**: Staying with my teacher; Hot, sharp and dirty things; Safe play | Children will stay with their group and an adult. Children will say stop or hot for dangerous things. | Staying with my teacher, hot, sharp and dirty things, and safe play. | Keep rules few and clear; use the words stop, hot and wait; practise rules by play; praise safe behaviour; never frighten children; check the compound for hazards daily; keep gates secure and count children often. | Stay with the group and an adult and stop when told stop. | A stop sign; A hot sign with a picture of a hot pot; Rope loops for walking; A safety song; Pictures of safe and unsafe play | Observe whether each child stays with the group and stops when told stop. | Stays with the teacher in the compound. Stops when told "stop". | Physical Development: stopping and starting; Literacy: safety words; Numeracy: counting the group |
| 10 | **Revision and assessment**: Revising greetings and manners; Revising feelings and routines; Revising sharing and safety | Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | Revising greetings and manners, feelings and routines, and sharing and safety, with end-of-term records. | Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly during play; record what children can do without pressure; share records with parents in plain language with a strength, a next step and a home idea. | Show learning from the term through play stations and a celebration. | Puppets; Feelings cards; A play shop; A toy door; Baskets; Stickers; Class record sheets | Observe and record each child’s Term 1 social learning. | Completes familiar routines with growing confidence. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. | Literacy: songs; Numeracy: counting and turns; Science: hygiene |
