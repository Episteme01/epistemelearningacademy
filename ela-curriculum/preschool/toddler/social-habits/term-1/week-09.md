# Toddler Social Habits — Term 1, Week 9: Safety

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W09 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Staying with my teacher; Hot, sharp and dirty things; Safe play

## Learning objectives
- Children will stay with their group and an adult.
- Children will say stop or hot for dangerous things.

**Key vocabulary:** safe, stop, hot, danger, together, walk

**Content:** Staying with my teacher, hot, sharp and dirty things, and safe play.

**Teaching activities:** Keep rules few and clear; use the words stop, hot and wait; practise rules by play; praise safe behaviour; never frighten children; check the compound for hazards daily; keep gates secure and count children often.

**Learner activities:** Stay with the group and an adult and stop when told stop.

**Resources:** A stop sign; A hot sign with a picture of a hot pot; Rope loops for walking; A safety song; Pictures of safe and unsafe play

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child stays with the group and stops when told stop.

## Expected outcomes
- Stays with the teacher in the compound.
- Stops when told "stop".

**Cross-curricular connections:** Physical Development: stopping and starting; Literacy: safety words; Numeracy: counting the group

## Day 1 (Monday): Staying with my teacher

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W09-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Staying with my teacher

### Learning objectives
- Children will stay with their teacher and group.

### Success criteria
- Stays near the teacher
- Holds a rope or a hand when walking

**Previous knowledge:** Children can follow routines and use the toilet with support.

**Key vocabulary:** safe, stop, hot, danger, together, walk

**Instructional materials:** A rope with loops; A picture of a group; A safety song

### Starter
Sing "Stay with me, stay with me, hold the rope and walk with me."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to hold a loop of the rope. Children: Children hold a loop.

Step 2. Teacher: Walk slowly around the room together. Children: Children walk with the rope.

Step 3. Teacher: Stop and count the children. Children: Children stand still and look.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We always stay with our teacher." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

### Practical activity: Rope walk
1. Hold a loop of the rope.
2. Walk slowly with the teacher.
3. Stop and count.

**Safety:** Walk slowly and keep a second adult at the back of the line. Use soft rope with loops and never tie children to it.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand at the front of the line.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to help count the group.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who do we stay with?
  **Expected response:** Our teacher.
- **Ask:** What do we hold?
  **Expected response:** The rope or a hand.

### Expected outcomes
- Stays with the teacher and group
- Walks with a rope or hand

### Conclusion
Sit together and count the children.

### Home connection
Practise staying close and holding hands in public places.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "stay with their teacher and group" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Stop and hot

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W09-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Hot, sharp and dirty things

### Learning objectives
- Children will stop when they hear stop and say hot for dangerous things.

### Success criteria
- Stops on the word stop
- Says hot for a hot picture

**Previous knowledge:** Children can follow routines and use the toilet with support.

**Key vocabulary:** safe, stop, hot, danger, together, walk

**Instructional materials:** A stop sign; A hot pot picture; A sharp knife picture; A puppet

### Starter
Hold up the stop sign and say "When I say stop, we stop still like a statue."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play stop and go with music. Children: Children move and stop.

Step 2. Teacher: Show the hot sign and say "Hot! Do not touch." Children: Children say hot and step back.

Step 3. Teacher: Show pictures of sharp or dirty things and say "Ask a grown-up." Children: Children say no touch.

Step 4. Teacher: Praise children who stop quickly. Children: Children smile and clap.

### Practical activity: Stop game
1. Move to music.
2. Stop when the teacher says stop.
3. Say hot to a hot picture.

**Safety:** Use only pictures of hot and sharp objects, never real ones. Keep the floor clear and stop movement games quickly if a child is hurt.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and stop together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find a hot thing in a picture.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do when we hear stop?
  **Expected response:** Stop still.
- **Ask:** Is a hot pot safe to touch?
  **Expected response:** No.

### Expected outcomes
- Stops on the word stop
- Says hot for dangerous things

### Conclusion
Stand still like a statue and then wave.

### Home connection
Teach hot and stop at home with a pretend game.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "stop when they hear stop and say hot for dangerous things" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Safe play outdoors

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W09-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Safe play

### Learning objectives
- Children will play safely outdoors with an adult watching.

### Success criteria
- Plays on equipment safely
- Stays inside the play area

**Previous knowledge:** Children can follow routines and use the toilet with support.

**Key vocabulary:** safe, stop, hot, danger, together, walk

**Instructional materials:** A play area marked with cones or a rope; Safe play equipment; A whistle or bell

### Starter
Show the play area boundary and say "We play inside the rope where it is safe."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the boundary and how to play inside it. Children: Children walk around the boundary.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children play with the equipment. Children: Children play safely.

Step 3. Teacher: Ring the bell and say "Come back." Children: Children come to the teacher.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We play safely and look after friends." Children: Children repeat safe.

### Practical activity: Safe play
1. Look at the boundary.
2. Play inside it.
3. Come back at the bell.

**Safety:** Check the area for hazards before play and keep an adult at every piece of equipment. Never leave children unattended.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child and stay close.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to remind a friend to stay inside.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where do we play?
  **Expected response:** Inside the rope.
- **Ask:** What do we do at the bell?
  **Expected response:** Come back.

### Expected outcomes
- Plays safely inside the boundary
- Comes back at the signal

### Conclusion
Count the children and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play in safe places at home and set clear limits.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "play safely outdoors with an adult watching" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Safe and unsafe pictures

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W09-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Safe play

### Learning objectives
- Children will sort pictures into safe and unsafe.

### Success criteria
- Points to a safe picture
- Says no for an unsafe picture

**Previous knowledge:** Children can follow routines and use the toilet with support.

**Key vocabulary:** safe, stop, hot, danger, together, walk

**Instructional materials:** Pictures of safe and unsafe actions; Two baskets: smiling face and stop sign

### Starter
Show two baskets and say "Safe things go in the happy basket. Unsafe things go in the stop basket."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show a picture and ask "Is it safe?" Children: Children look and say yes or no.

Step 2. Teacher: Put the picture in the correct basket. Children: Child puts the picture in a basket.

Step 3. Teacher: Talk about why one is unsafe. Children: Children point to the danger.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We always stay safe." Children: Children repeat safe.

### Practical activity: Safe sort
1. Look at each picture.
2. Say safe or not safe.
3. Put it in a basket.

**Safety:** Use only gentle pictures with no frightening images. Stop if a child becomes worried and comfort them.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two pictures and say safe or not first.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find another unsafe picture.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Is this safe?
  **Expected response:** Yes or no.
- **Ask:** What do we say to danger?
  **Expected response:** Stop.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts pictures into safe and unsafe
- Says stop to danger

### Conclusion
Put the baskets away and say "Stay safe."

### Home connection
Talk about safe and unsafe things around the house.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "sort pictures into safe and unsafe" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 9 safety check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W09-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Staying with my teacher

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child follows safety rules.

### Success criteria
- Stays with the group
- Stops on the word stop

**Previous knowledge:** Children can follow routines and use the toilet with support.

**Key vocabulary:** safe, stop, hot, danger, together, walk

**Instructional materials:** Rope; Stop sign; Pictures; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the safety song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: rope walk, stop game and safe pictures. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child follows the rule. Children: Child follows with a prompt.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Safety stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use only pictures and soft equipment.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to lead the rope walk.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: stays with the group; stops on the word stop; says hot; plays safely.

- **Ask:** Who do we stay with?
  **Expected response:** Our teacher.
- **Ask:** What do we do at stop?
  **Expected response:** Stop still.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 9
- Follows safety rules with support

### Conclusion
Count the children and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep safety rules clear and simple at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child follows safety rules" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
