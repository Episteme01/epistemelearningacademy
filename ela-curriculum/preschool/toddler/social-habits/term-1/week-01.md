# Toddler Social Habits — Term 1, Week 1: Belonging

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W01 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** My name and my friends; My teacher and my classroom; Saying goodbye and hello again

## Learning objectives
- Children will say or show their name and recognise their teacher.
- Children will separate from a parent with support and return happily.

**Key vocabulary:** name, teacher, friend, school, class, goodbye

**Content:** Learning names, meeting the teacher and the classroom, and practising saying goodbye and hello again.

**Teaching activities:** Greet every child by name at the door; keep the first days calm and predictable; allow a parent to stay briefly if needed; never rush a crying child; give a comfort object a safe place; sing the same songs every day.

**Learner activities:** Respond to their name, recognise the teacher and settle in the classroom with adult support.

**Resources:** Name cards with photographs; A welcome song; Soft toys and comfort objects; A hello-goodbye puppet; A picture timetable

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child responds to their name, settles with support and says or waves goodbye.

## Expected outcomes
- Responds to their name.
- Settles in the classroom with adult support.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: names and songs; Numeracy: counting friends; Creative Development: singing

## Day 1 (Monday): My name

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W01-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** My name and my friends

### Learning objectives
- Children will hear and respond to their own name.

### Success criteria
- Looks up when their name is called
- Says or shows their name with support

**Previous knowledge:** This is the first week of school. Children arrive with different home experiences and some may be upset to leave their parents.

**Key vocabulary:** name, teacher, friend, school, class, goodbye

**Instructional materials:** Name cards with photographs; A soft ball; A welcome song

### Starter
Sit in a circle and sing "Hello, hello, what is your name?" while looking at each child with a smile.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Hold up each name card and say the child’s name warmly. Children: Child looks and smiles or says their name.

Step 2. Teacher: Roll a soft ball to each child and say their name. Children: Child catches or touches the ball.

Step 3. Teacher: Say the names of friends in the circle together. Children: Children look at friends and wave.

Step 4. Teacher: Sing the name song again, using every name. Children: Children clap and sing along.

### Practical activity: Name circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Roll the ball to each child and say their name.
3. Wave and say hello together.

**Safety:** Use a very soft ball and sit children away from walls and furniture. Never push a child to speak; accept a smile or a point.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Sit close to the child and use their photograph and name together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to roll the ball and say a friend’s name.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is your name?
  **Expected response:** The child says or points to their name.
- **Ask:** Where is your friend?
  **Expected response:** Points to a friend.

### Expected outcomes
- Responds to their own name
- Joins the name song with a smile or action

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye to each name and say "See you tomorrow."

### Home connection
Say your child’s name warmly and let them say or show it to family members.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "hear and respond to their own name" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- NAME
- HELLO

**Picture suggestions:**
- Name cards with photos
- A welcome circle

**Song or rhyme:** Hello, hello, what is your name? Hello, hello, I am glad you came.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): My teacher and my classroom

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W01-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** My teacher and my classroom

### Learning objectives
- Children will recognise their teacher and the main areas of the classroom.

### Success criteria
- Points to the teacher when asked
- Visits the book corner and toy area with support

**Previous knowledge:** This is the first week of school. Children arrive with different home experiences and some may be upset to leave their parents.

**Key vocabulary:** name, teacher, friend, school, class, goodbye

**Instructional materials:** A picture timetable; Labels for the book corner, toy area and toilet; A puppet

### Starter
Use the puppet to say "Good morning! I am your teacher’s friend. Let us look at our classroom."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Introduce yourself by name and show a photo of the teacher. Children: Children look and point to the teacher.

Step 2. Teacher: Walk around the room and name each area: books, toys, toilet and mat. Children: Children walk with the teacher.

Step 3. Teacher: Show the picture timetable and say what happens next. Children: Children look at the pictures.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose an area to visit with you. Children: Child chooses and plays for a short time.

### Practical activity: Classroom walk
1. Walk around the room.
2. Name each area.
3. Let each child choose a place to play.

**Safety:** Check the room for hazards before children arrive and stay close to children who wander. Keep doors closed and the gate secure.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and walk to one area at a time.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to show a friend one place in the classroom.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where is the teacher?
  **Expected response:** Points to the teacher.
- **Ask:** Where are the books?
  **Expected response:** Points to the book corner.

### Expected outcomes
- Recognises the teacher
- Finds at least one area of the classroom

### Conclusion
Sing the goodbye song and wave to the classroom.

### Home connection
Talk about school with your child in a happy voice and say you will always come back.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "recognise their teacher and the main areas of the classroom" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- TEACHER
- CLASS

**Picture suggestions:**
- The teacher
- The classroom

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Saying goodbye and hello again

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W01-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Saying goodbye and hello again

### Learning objectives
- Children will practise a short goodbye and a happy hello again.

### Success criteria
- Waves goodbye with support
- Smiles when the adult returns

**Previous knowledge:** This is the first week of school. Children arrive with different home experiences and some may be upset to leave their parents.

**Key vocabulary:** name, teacher, friend, school, class, goodbye

**Instructional materials:** A puppet or soft toy; A hello-goodbye song; A toy door or a screen

### Starter
Show the puppet who goes behind the screen and says "Goodbye!" then pops back with "Hello again!"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the puppet goodbye and hello game. Children: Children wave and say hello.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child hide a soft toy and bring it back. Children: Child hides the toy and finds it.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Mummy always comes back" in a calm, happy voice. Children: Children listen and wave.

Step 4. Teacher: Practise a short goodbye wave at the door with a friend. Children: Child waves and says bye.

### Practical activity: Goodbye and hello
1. Hide a toy.
2. Say goodbye with a wave.
3. Bring it back and say hello again.

**Safety:** Stay close to a child who is upset and never leave a distressed child alone. Keep the play calm and gentle.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Wave together and let the child hold a comfort toy.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say goodbye to a friend and hello again.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we say when we go?
  **Expected response:** Goodbye.
- **Ask:** What do we say when we come back?
  **Expected response:** Hello.

### Expected outcomes
- Waves goodbye with support
- Smiles when a toy or adult returns

### Conclusion
Sing the goodbye song and wave together.

### Home connection
Say a short, loving goodbye every day and promise to return.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "practise a short goodbye and a happy hello again" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- HELLO
- GOODBYE

**Picture suggestions:**
- A hand waving
- A puppet

## Day 4 (Thursday): Playing with new friends

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W01-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** My name and my friends

### Learning objectives
- Children will play beside other children and notice their names.

### Success criteria
- Plays beside another child
- Looks at a friend and smiles

**Previous knowledge:** This is the first week of school. Children arrive with different home experiences and some may be upset to leave their parents.

**Key vocabulary:** name, teacher, friend, school, class, goodbye

**Instructional materials:** Matching toys such as blocks and cars; A play mat; Name cards

### Starter
Put out two of each toy and say "Let us find a friend with the same toy."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show two matching toys and pair children beside each other. Children: Children sit beside a friend.

Step 2. Teacher: Say each friend’s name as they play. Children: Child looks at the friend.

Step 3. Teacher: Show how to offer a toy with a smile. Children: Child offers a toy to a friend.

Step 4. Teacher: Wave to the friend and say bye when finished. Children: Child waves and says bye.

### Practical activity: Play beside a friend
1. Pair children with matching toys.
2. Play for a few minutes.
3. Say each other’s names.

**Safety:** Use large toys with no small parts that can be swallowed and supervise closely. Step in gently if a child grabs.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Sit with the child and play beside one friend.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to name a friend they played with.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is your friend?
  **Expected response:** Says or points to a friend.
- **Ask:** What did you play?
  **Expected response:** A toy name.

### Expected outcomes
- Plays beside a friend
- Says or points to a friend

### Conclusion
Tidy the toys together and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Arrange short play dates or play beside siblings and name the other child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "play beside other children and notice their names" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- FRIEND
- PLAY

**Picture suggestions:**
- Two children playing
- Matching toys

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 1 settling check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SOC-T1-W01-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Saying goodbye and hello again

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child is settling at school.

### Success criteria
- Responds to their name
- Settles in the classroom with support

**Previous knowledge:** This is the first week of school. Children arrive with different home experiences and some may be upset to leave their parents.

**Key vocabulary:** name, teacher, friend, school, class, goodbye

**Instructional materials:** Name cards; Class record sheet; Comfort objects

### Starter
Sing the welcome song and invite children to play at three familiar areas.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three areas: name circle, play and book corner. Children: Children move between areas freely.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child’s response to name, mood and play and note it. Children: Children play and explore.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and a note for parents. Children: Children join the goodbye song.

### Practical activity: Settling observation
1. Set up three calm areas.
2. Let children move freely.
3. The teacher watches and records.

**Safety:** Keep an adult at every area and watch for distressed children. Keep the environment calm and safe.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult close by and a comfort object.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to welcome a new friend.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: responds to name; separates from a parent with support; recognises the teacher; plays near others.

- **Ask:** What is your name?
  **Expected response:** The child says or shows it.
- **Ask:** Who is your teacher?
  **Expected response:** Points to the teacher.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 1
- Settles in class with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you tomorrow."

### Home connection
Share a short note about how your child settled and how to keep goodbyes short.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child is settling at school" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Settling

**Picture suggestions:**
- Children playing happily
