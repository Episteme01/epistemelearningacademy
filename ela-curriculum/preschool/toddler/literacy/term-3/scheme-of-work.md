# Toddler Literacy — Term 3 scheme of work

| Week | Topic and subtopics | Learning objectives | Content | Teaching activities | Learner activities | Resources | Assessment | Expected outcomes | Cross-curricular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **First sounds**: Listening for /s/; Things that start with /s/; The /s/ song | Children will hear and say the sound /s/ at the start of words. Children will find objects that start with /s/ with support. | Listening for the sound /s/ at the start of words, finding things that start with /s/, and the /s/ song. | Say /s/ as a pure sound, a long hiss "sss", with no vowel added; say "the sound sss" and never "es" or "suh"; show your mouth and teeth; use real objects; children hear and say sounds only, with no letter formation. | Hear and say the sound /s/ at the start of words and point to objects that start with /s/ with support. | A toy sun, a sock, a toy snake, a spoon; A tray; A bag; A picture of a snake; A mirror | Observe whether each child says /s/ clearly and points to an object that starts with /s/. | Says /s/ clearly. Points to an object that starts with /s/. | Understanding the World: everyday objects; Music: sound songs; Physical Development: mouth movements |
| 2 | **First sounds**: Listening for /a/; Things that start with /a/; The /a/ song | Children will hear and say the sound /a/ at the start of words. Children will find objects that start with /a/ with support. | Listening for the sound /a/ at the start of words, finding things that start with /a/, and the /a/ song. | Say /a/ as a short, clear sound as in "ant", with no extra sound; show an open mouth; link the sound to an ant that wiggles; use real objects; children hear and say the sound only. | Hear and say the sound /a/ at the start of words and find objects that start with /a/ with support. | A toy ant or a picture of an ant; A toy apple; A toy axe or a picture; A mirror; A tray | Observe whether each child says /a/ clearly and points to an object that starts with /a/. | Says /a/ clearly. Points to an object that starts with /a/. | Understanding the World: insects; Music: sound songs; Physical Development: mouth shapes |
| 3 | **First sounds**: Listening for /t/; Listening for /p/; Which sound do you hear first? | Children will hear and say the sounds /t/ and /p/ at the start of words. Children will tell /t/ from /p/ with support. | Listening for the sounds /t/ and /p/ at the start of words and telling which sound they hear first. | Say /t/ and /p/ as short, crisp sounds with no vowel added; /t/ is made with the tongue behind the teeth and /p/ with the lips; hold a tissue in front of your mouth to show the puff of /p/; keep the sounds clean and brief. | Hear and say /t/ and /p/ at the start of words and tell /t/ from /p/ with support. | A tap picture, a tin, a toy tiger; A pot, a pan, a pen; A tissue; Two baskets | Observe whether each child says /t/ and /p/ clearly and points to the correct picture for each sound. | Says /t/ and /p/ clearly. Points to the right picture for /t/ or /p/. | Understanding the World: kitchen objects; Music: sound songs; Physical Development: lips and tongue |
| 4 | **First sounds**: Sound hunt; Sound sorting; Sound bingo | Children will play games with the sounds /s/ /a/ /t/ /p/. Children will sort objects by their first sound with support. | Playing games with the four sounds: a sound hunt, sorting by first sound and sound bingo. | Keep every game playful; say the sound first before showing the picture; always use pure sounds; accept a point or a sound; keep the number of choices small for children who find it hard. | Play games with /s/ /a/ /t/ /p/ and sort objects by their first sound with support. | Objects and pictures for s, a, t and p; Four baskets labelled with a picture; Sound bingo cards; Counters | Observe whether each child says at least two of the four sounds and sorts by first sound with support. | Sorts pictures by first sound with support. Says at least two of the four sounds. | Mathematics: sorting; Music: sound songs; Social Habits: taking turns in games |
| 5 | **Pre-writing**: Straight lines up and down; Round and curly patterns; Zigzag and wave | Children will make straight lines, circles and zigzags in sand, paint and on paper. Children will copy a simple pattern with support. | Making straight lines up and down, round and curly patterns, and zigzag and wave patterns in sand, paint and on paper. | Draw big in the air first, then on large surfaces; say a rhyme for each pattern; never correct pencil grip or correct the direction; let children use whole arm movements; do not teach letter formation. | Make straight lines, circles and zigzags in sand, paint and on paper and copy a simple pattern with support. | A tray of sand or flour; Paint and large brushes; Large paper; Fat crayons; Chalk; Ribbon sticks | Observe whether each child makes straight and round marks on purpose and copies a simple pattern. | Makes straight and round marks on purpose. Copies a simple pattern. | Creative Development: pattern and art; Physical Development: gross and fine motor; Mathematics: shape and pattern |
| 6 | **Listening**: One-step instructions; Two-step instructions; Giving an instruction to a friend | Children will follow a two-part instruction. Children will give a simple instruction to a friend. | Following one-step and two-step instructions and giving a simple instruction to a friend. | Give one instruction at a time first; use the words first and then; show with actions and wait before repeating; use real objects; never rush a child who needs time to think. | Follow a two-part instruction and give a simple instruction to a friend. | A basket of toys; A box and a mat; A ball, a cup, a spoon and a teddy; A bell; Pictures of instructions | Observe whether each child follows a two-step instruction and gives a one-step instruction. | Follows a two-step instruction. Gives a one-step instruction. | Social Habits: listening and following directions; Physical Development: movement; Mathematics: position words |
| 7 | **Stories**: Three-picture stories; First, then, last; Telling the story back | Children will put three pictures of a story in order. Children will use first, then and last with support. | Putting three pictures of a story in order, using first, then and last, and telling the story back. | Use stories with clear everyday events such as getting dressed or planting a seed; place cards left to right as you speak; use the words first, then and last every time; accept actions and words. | Put three pictures of a story in order and use first, then and last with support. | Three-picture story sets: planting a seed, washing hands, going to sleep; A story box; A real seed, soil, a pot and water; Puppets | Observe whether each child orders three pictures and uses at least one sequencing word. | Orders three pictures. Uses at least one sequencing word. | Science: growing; Social Habits: routines; Mathematics: ordering |
| 8 | **Books and print**: My name card; The first letter of my name; Names on things I own | Children will recognise their name card and the first letter of their name. Children will make marks to show their name with support. | Recognising their name card and the first letter of their name, and making marks to show their name with support. | Always write names with a capital first letter and lower-case letters; use the child’s photo; point to the first letter and say its name and sound only for the four taught sounds; never ask a toddler to write their whole name; accept any mark as name-writing. | Recognise their name card and the first letter of their name and make marks to show their name with support. | Name cards with photos; Name labels for pegs and trays; A tray of sand; Crayons and paper; A mirror | Observe whether each child finds their name among several cards and points to the first letter with support. | Finds their name among several cards. Points to the first letter of their name with support. | Personal, Social and Emotional Development: identity; Understanding the World: belongings; Physical Development: mark-making |
| 9 | **Stories**: Choosing a topic; Making pages with pictures and marks; Sharing my book | Children will make a simple book with pictures and marks. Children will share their book with a friend. | Choosing a topic, making pages with pictures and marks, and sharing the book with a friend. | Make a model book first; let children choose their own topic; accept any picture or mark as a page; write down what children say beside their pages; celebrate every book as a real book. | Make a simple book with pictures and marks and share it with a friend. | Folded paper booklets of four pages; Crayons and stickers; Magazine pictures; Glue sticks; A stapler for the teacher only; A model book | Observe whether each child makes a book with at least three pages and tells a friend about one page. | Makes a book with at least three pages. Tells a friend about one page. | Creative Development: art; Understanding the World: favourite things; Social Habits: sharing |
| 10 | **Revision and assessment**: Revising listening, rhymes and sounds; Revising stories and books; Celebration and end-of-year record | Children will show Year learning through games and stations. Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note. | Revising listening, rhymes and sounds, stories and books, and celebrating the year with end-of-year records and a transition note. | Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly; record what children can do; invite parents to the celebration; complete the transition note honestly for the next teacher. | Show what they know through games and stations and enjoy a literacy celebration. | Songs and rhymes from the year; The four sound objects; Books, name cards and story boxes; Children’s own books; Certificates or stickers; End-of-year record sheets | Observe and record each child’s end-of-year literacy learning and complete the transition note. | Takes part confidently in familiar literacy games. Each child has an end-of-year observation record. | Music: favourite songs; Understanding the World: our school; Social Habits: celebration and thanks |
