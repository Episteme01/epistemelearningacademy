# Toddler Literacy — Term 3, Week 4: First sounds

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W04 · **Week type:** consolidation · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Sound hunt; Sound sorting; Sound bingo

## Learning objectives
- Children will play games with the sounds /s/ /a/ /t/ /p/.
- Children will sort objects by their first sound with support.

**Key vocabulary:** sun, ant, tap, pot, sort, sound

**Content:** Playing games with the four sounds: a sound hunt, sorting by first sound and sound bingo.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every game playful; say the sound first before showing the picture; always use pure sounds; accept a point or a sound; keep the number of choices small for children who find it hard.

**Learner activities:** Play games with /s/ /a/ /t/ /p/ and sort objects by their first sound with support.

**Resources:** Objects and pictures for s, a, t and p; Four baskets labelled with a picture; Sound bingo cards; Counters

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child says at least two of the four sounds and sorts by first sound with support.

## Expected outcomes
- Sorts pictures by first sound with support.
- Says at least two of the four sounds.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Mathematics: sorting; Music: sound songs; Social Habits: taking turns in games

## Day 1 (Monday): Four sounds hunt

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W04-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Sound hunt

### Learning objectives
- Children will find objects that start with the four sounds in a hunt.

### Success criteria
- Finds an object and says its first sound
- Names at least two of the sounds

**Previous knowledge:** Children can hear and say /s/, /a/, /t/ and /p/ and find objects that start with each.

**Key vocabulary:** sun, ant, tap, pot, sort, sound

**Instructional materials:** Eight objects: sun, sock, ant, apple, tap, tin, pot, pan; Four baskets with picture labels; A bag

### Starter
Say "sss, a, t, p. Four sounds. Let us hunt for things that start with them." Show the baskets.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each basket and say its sound: sss for the snake, a for the ant, t for the tap, p for the pot. Children: Children say each sound.

Step 2. Teacher: Hide the eight objects in easy places in the room. Children: Children watch.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Find something" and let each child bring one object to the mat. Children: Child finds an object.

Step 4. Teacher: Say the word, then the first sound, then put it in the matching basket. Children: Child says the first sound and places it.

### Practical activity: Sound hunt
1. Hide eight objects in easy places.
2. Each child finds one object.
3. Say the first sound and put it in the right basket.

**Safety:** Hide objects in clear, low places away from hazards. Supervise the search and keep children together.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use two baskets only and say the sound with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find two objects for the same basket.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What sound does sun start with?
  **Expected response:** Sss.
- **Ask:** Which basket does apple go in?
  **Expected response:** The a basket.

### Expected outcomes
- Finds and sorts objects by first sound with support
- Says at least two of the sounds

### Conclusion
Count the objects in each basket and say the sounds.

### Home connection
Hunt for things at home that start with sss, a, t or p.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "find objects that start with the four sounds in a hunt" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Sorting by sound

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W04-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Sound sorting

### Learning objectives
- Children will sort pictures by their first sound.

### Success criteria
- Puts a picture in the right sound basket
- Says the first sound

**Previous knowledge:** Children can hear and say /s/, /a/, /t/ and /p/ and find objects that start with each.

**Key vocabulary:** sun, ant, tap, pot, sort, sound

**Instructional materials:** Picture cards for each of the four sounds; Four baskets; A mat

### Starter
Say "I have pictures. Which sound do they start with? Let us sort them."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Pick a card, say the word and its first sound, and put it in the right basket. Children: Children say the first sound.

Step 2. Teacher: Pick another card and ask "Which basket?" Children: Child points to the basket.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child pick a card and sort it. Children: Child sorts the card and says the sound.

Step 4. Teacher: Check each basket together by saying the words. Children: Children say the words and the sound.

### Practical activity: Sound sort
1. Put four baskets on the mat.
2. Each child picks a card and says its first sound.
3. Put it in the right basket.

**Safety:** Use large soft cards. Keep children seated with room to reach and supervise turns.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two baskets and say the sound very clearly.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to sort five cards without help.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which basket does tin go in?
  **Expected response:** The t basket.
- **Ask:** What sound does pan start with?
  **Expected response:** The sound p.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts pictures by first sound with support
- Says the first sound

### Conclusion
Put the cards away and say each sound.

### Home connection
Sort toys by their first sound at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "sort pictures by their first sound" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- SORT BY SOUND

**Picture suggestions:**
- Four baskets with pictures

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Sound bingo

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W04-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Sound bingo

### Learning objectives
- Children will play a simple bingo game with the four sounds.

### Success criteria
- Listens to the sound
- Covers the matching picture on the card

**Previous knowledge:** Children can hear and say /s/, /a/, /t/ and /p/ and find objects that start with each.

**Key vocabulary:** sun, ant, tap, pot, sort, sound

**Instructional materials:** Bingo cards with four pictures each; Counters; Picture cards to call

### Starter
Show a bingo card and say "I will say a sound. You cover the picture."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Say "sss" and ask the children to cover the snake picture with a counter. Children: Children cover the picture.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "a" and ask them to cover the ant picture. Children: Children cover the picture.

Step 3. Teacher: Continue with t and p. Children: Children cover each picture as they hear the sound.

Step 4. Teacher: Let a child say a sound and the others cover. Children: Child calls a sound and friends cover.

### Practical activity: Sound bingo
1. Give each child a card and counters.
2. Say a sound and children cover the picture.
3. Play until the card is covered.

**Safety:** Use large counters that cannot be swallowed or use stickers. Supervise counters closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use a card with two pictures and help cover the picture.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to call the sounds for a friend.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which picture do you cover for t?
  **Expected response:** The tap.
- **Ask:** What sound is the ant?
  **Expected response:** The sound a.

### Expected outcomes
- Matches sounds to pictures in a game
- Says some of the four sounds

### Conclusion
Count the covered pictures together and clap.

### Home connection
Play bingo at home with pictures and the four sounds.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "play a simple bingo game with the four sounds" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- BINGO
- S  A  T  P

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 4 (Thursday): Sound song and actions

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W04-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Sound sorting

### Learning objectives
- Children will sing a song with all four sounds and their actions.

### Success criteria
- Does the right action for each sound
- Says at least two sounds

**Previous knowledge:** Children can hear and say /s/, /a/, /t/ and /p/ and find objects that start with each.

**Key vocabulary:** sun, ant, tap, pot, sort, sound

**Instructional materials:** Picture cards: snake, ant, tap, pot; A scarf; A drum

### Starter
Show the four cards and say "Sss for snake. A for ant. T for tap. P for pot."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing "Sss for snake, a for ant, t for tap, p for pot" and show each card. Children: Children listen and look.

Step 2. Teacher: Sing again and do each action: wiggle, crawl, tap, pop. Children: Children do each action.

Step 3. Teacher: Say a sound and let children do the action. Children: Child does the matching action.

Step 4. Teacher: Let a child choose a card and lead. Children: Child leads and friends follow.

### Practical activity: Four sounds game
1. Stand in a circle with space.
2. Say a sound and everyone does the action.
3. Increase the speed gently.

**Safety:** Clear the floor and keep movements small. Stop when children are tired.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Do the actions together and say the sound very clearly.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead the game by choosing the next sound.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do for sss?
  **Expected response:** Wiggle like a snake.
- **Ask:** What do we do for p?
  **Expected response:** Pop.

### Expected outcomes
- Links four sounds to actions
- Sings the song with actions

### Conclusion
Sing quietly and wave goodbye to the four sounds.

### Home connection
Do the four sound actions at home with your child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "sing a song with all four sounds and their actions" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- SSS
- ANT
- TAP
- POP

**Picture suggestions:**
- A snake
- An ant
- A tap
- A pot

**Song or rhyme:** Sss for snake, a for ant, t for tap, p for pot.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 4 four sounds check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W04-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Sound hunt

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child hears, says and sorts the four sounds.

### Success criteria
- Says at least two of the four sounds
- Sorts by first sound with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children can hear and say /s/, /a/, /t/ and /p/ and find objects that start with each.

**Key vocabulary:** sun, ant, tap, pot, sort, sound

**Instructional materials:** Objects and pictures; Baskets; Bingo cards; Class record sheet

### Starter
Say "sss, a, t, p" and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: sort, bingo and hunt. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What sound?" and watch what the child can do. Children: Child says the sound or points.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Four sound stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use large soft cards and counters.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use two sounds.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to teach a friend a sound.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: says /s/ /a/ /t/ /p/; sorts by first sound with support; plays the sound games.

- **Ask:** What sound does sun start with?
  **Expected response:** Sss.
- **Ask:** What sound does pot start with?
  **Expected response:** The sound p.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 4
- Says at least two of the sounds

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye to the four sounds.

### Home connection
Play sound games at home with sss, a, t and p.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child hears, says and sorts the four sounds" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

- S A T P
