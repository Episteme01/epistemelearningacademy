# Toddler Literacy — Term 3, Week 7: Stories

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W07 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Three-picture stories; First, then, last; Telling the story back

## Learning objectives
- Children will put three pictures of a story in order.
- Children will use first, then and last with support.

**Key vocabulary:** first, then, last, story, picture, order

**Content:** Putting three pictures of a story in order, using first, then and last, and telling the story back.

**Teaching activities:** Use stories with clear everyday events such as getting dressed or planting a seed; place cards left to right as you speak; use the words first, then and last every time; accept actions and words.

**Learner activities:** Put three pictures of a story in order and use first, then and last with support.

**Resources:** Three-picture story sets: planting a seed, washing hands, going to sleep; A story box; A real seed, soil, a pot and water; Puppets

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child orders three pictures and uses at least one sequencing word.

## Expected outcomes
- Orders three pictures.
- Uses at least one sequencing word.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Science: growing; Social Habits: routines; Mathematics: ordering

## Day 1 (Monday): Planting a seed

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W07-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Three-picture stories

### Learning objectives
- Children will put three pictures of planting a seed in order.

### Success criteria
- Puts three pictures in order with support
- Names what happens in each picture

**Previous knowledge:** Children can retell a short story with props, use words such as first, then and the end, and follow two-step instructions.

**Key vocabulary:** first, then, last, story, picture, order

**Instructional materials:** Three picture cards: digging, planting a seed, watering; A pot, soil and a seed

### Starter
Show the pot, soil and seed and say "First we put soil in the pot. Then we plant the seed. Then we water it."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the three picture cards in the right order and say first, then, last. Children: Children look and say the words.

Step 2. Teacher: Mix the cards and ask "What happens first?" Children: Child points to the digging card.

Step 3. Teacher: Do the activity: put soil in the pot, plant a seed and water it. Children: Children help with each step.

Step 4. Teacher: Let the children put the cards in order. Children: Child puts cards in order with support.

### Practical activity: Plant a seed
1. Put soil in a pot.
2. Plant a seed and water it.
3. Put the three picture cards in order.

**Safety:** Use clean soil and wash hands after. Do not let children eat soil or seeds. Supervise water and wipe spills.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the cards together and place them in order.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what happens first, then and last.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What happens first?
  **Expected response:** We put soil in the pot.
- **Ask:** What happens last?
  **Expected response:** We water the seed.

### Expected outcomes
- Orders three pictures with support
- Uses first and last with support

### Conclusion
Put the pot in a sunny place and say "We will watch it grow."

### Home connection
Plant a seed with your child at home and talk about what happens first.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "put three pictures of planting a seed in order" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Washing hands

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W07-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** First, then, last

### Learning objectives
- Children will put the steps of washing hands in order.

### Success criteria
- Puts three pictures in order
- Uses first, then or last

**Previous knowledge:** Children can retell a short story with props, use words such as first, then and the end, and follow two-step instructions.

**Key vocabulary:** first, then, last, story, picture, order

**Instructional materials:** Three picture cards: wet hands, soap, dry hands; A basin, soap and a towel

### Starter
Say "First we wet our hands, then we use soap, last we dry them." Show the basin.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the three cards in order and say first, then, last. Children: Children say the words.

Step 2. Teacher: Mix the cards and ask the children to order them. Children: Children put the cards in order.

Step 3. Teacher: Wash hands together and say each step as you do it. Children: Children wash hands in order.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "What did we do first?" and "What did we do last?" Children: Child points to the cards.

### Practical activity: Wash and order
1. Set up a basin with soap and a towel.
2. Wash hands in the right order.
3. Put the cards in order afterwards.

**Safety:** Use mild soap and a shallow basin. Supervise closely, wipe spills and make sure each child has a clean towel.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Do the steps together and name each one.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell a friend how to wash hands.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do first?
  **Expected response:** Wet our hands.
- **Ask:** What do we do last?
  **Expected response:** Dry our hands.

### Expected outcomes
- Orders the steps of washing hands
- Uses sequencing words

### Conclusion
Dry hands and say "First, then, last."

### Home connection
Say first, then, last when washing hands at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "put the steps of washing hands in order" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Telling the story back

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W07-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Telling the story back

### Learning objectives
- Children will tell a three-picture story back using first, then and last.

### Success criteria
- Tells what happens in order
- Uses at least one sequencing word

**Previous knowledge:** Children can retell a short story with props, use words such as first, then and the end, and follow two-step instructions.

**Key vocabulary:** first, then, last, story, picture, order

**Instructional materials:** Three picture card sets; Puppets; A cushion for the storyteller

### Starter
Show a set of three cards and say "Who can tell what happens?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Choose a set and put the cards in order together. Children: Child puts cards in order with support.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "What happens first?" and wait for the child to say it. Children: Child tells the first event.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "What happens next?" and "What happens last?" Children: Child tells the next events.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child tell the story with a puppet. Children: Child tells the story to the group.

### Practical activity: Story telling
1. Put three cards in order on the floor.
2. Each child tells what happens.
3. Use a puppet to help.

**Safety:** Never make a child talk if they do not want to. Accept words, actions or pointing.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Point to each card and offer the first word.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell the story without looking at the cards.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What happens first?
  **Expected response:** The first picture event.
- **Ask:** What happens last?
  **Expected response:** The last picture event.

### Expected outcomes
- Tells a three-part story in order
- Uses first, then or last

### Conclusion
Clap for all the storytellers.

### Home connection
Ask your child to tell you what happened at school using first, then and last.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "tell a three-picture story back using first, then and last" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**

- THEN
- LAST

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 7 order check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T3-W07-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Three-picture stories

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child orders and tells a short story.

### Success criteria
- Orders three pictures
- Uses a sequencing word

**Previous knowledge:** Children can retell a short story with props, use words such as first, then and the end, and follow two-step instructions.

**Key vocabulary:** first, then, last, story, picture, order

**Instructional materials:** Picture sets; Puppets; Class record sheet

### Starter
Say "First, then, last" and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: planting, washing and bedtime. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask the child to order the cards and tell the story. Children: Child orders and tells.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Story stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use large soft cards.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use two pictures.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to tell a four-picture story.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: orders three pictures; uses first, then or last; tells the story back.

- **Ask:** What happens first?
  **Expected response:** The first event.
- **Ask:** What happens last?
  **Expected response:** The last event.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 7
- Orders three pictures

### Conclusion
Say "The end" and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Tell stories at home using first, then and last.

- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child orders and tells a short story" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
