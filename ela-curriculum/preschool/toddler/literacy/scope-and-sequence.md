# Toddler Literacy — annual scope and sequence

Three terms of ten teaching weeks. Five planned lessons a week (150 in the year). Dates are configured by ELA's academic calendar, not written here.

## Term 1: Term 1 builds listening, voice and vocabulary: sounds around us, loud and quiet, animal and instrument sounds, greetings and names, action rhymes, picture books and naming things

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Listening | Listening walk; Inside and outside sounds; Guess the sound | Children will stop, listen and say or show what they hear. Children will match a sound to the thing that makes it with support. | listen, hear, sound, quiet, noise | Stays still to listen for a short time. Names or points to the source of a familiar sound. |
| 2 | Listening | Loud and quiet; Using my voice; High and low voices | Children will make a loud and a quiet sound on request. Children will use their voices in play, such as a whisper and a shout. | loud, quiet, voice, whisper, shout | Changes the loudness of their voice on request. Uses a voice sound to join a game. |
| 3 | Sounds in nature | Farm animals and their sounds; Birds and insects; Animal sound games | Children will match animal pictures to sounds. Children will make animal sounds and say the animal name. | goat, hen, dog, cat, cow, bird | Names at least three animals. Makes the sound of a familiar animal. |
| 4 | Rhythm | Drums and shakers; Clapping and stamping; Fast and slow | Children will copy a short beat with an instrument or their body. Children will say fast or slow with support. | drum, shaker, clap, stamp, fast, slow | Copies a two-beat pattern. Plays an instrument gently. |
| 5 | Speaking | Good morning and goodbye; My name and my friends’ names; Please and thank you | Children will say or sign a greeting to an adult or friend. Children will say or point to their own name and a friend’s name. | hello, goodbye, name, please, thank you | Greets someone with a word or wave. Responds to their own name. |
| 6 | Rhymes and songs | Finger rhymes; Action songs; Rhymes with a story | Children will join in actions and words of a short rhyme. Children will fill in a missing word with support. | rhyme, sing, clap, wave, finger, again | Joins in at least two rhymes with actions. Says the last word of a line when the teacher pauses. |
| 7 | Books | Holding a book and turning pages; Pointing to and naming pictures; A favourite book | Children will hold a book the right way up and turn pages. Children will point to and name pictures. | book, page, picture, turn, look, read | Turns pages one at a time with support. Names three pictures in a book. |
| 8 | Vocabulary | Family and people; Food we eat; Things in our classroom | Children will name familiar people, foods and objects. Children will say a word or two to ask for something. | mummy, daddy, baby, rice, water, chair | Names at least ten familiar things. Uses two words together to ask for something. |
| 9 | Stories | Listening to a short story; Joining in with a repeated line; Acting the story | Children will listen to a story with pictures and objects. Children will join in with a repeated line and actions. | story, once, then, the end, listen, act | Listens to a short story. Joins in with a repeated line. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising listening and sound games; Revising rhymes and books; Revising words and names | Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | listen, loud, quiet, rhyme, book, name | Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. |

## Term 2: Term 2 builds sound play and early print: rhyme, syllables, finger strength, big mark-making, vocabulary themes, songs, retelling and questions, and noticing print

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rhyme | Hearing words that sound the same at the end; Finishing a rhyme; Rhyming pairs with pictures | Children will notice when two words rhyme. Children will finish a familiar rhyme with a rhyming word. | rhyme, sounds the same, cat, hat, dog, log | Says the rhyming word in a familiar rhyme. Matches two pictures that rhyme with support. |
| 2 | Syllables | Clapping my name; Clapping food and animal names; Syllable sorting | Children will clap the beats in their name. Children will clap the beats in simple words. | clap, beat, name, one beat, two beats, three beats | Claps the beats in their own name. Matches a word to the number of claps with support. |
| 3 | Fine motor | Squeezing and pinching; Threading large beads; Finger games with playdough | Children will squeeze, pinch and poke playdough and sponges. Children will thread large objects with support. | squeeze, pinch, poke, roll, thread, fingers | Squeezes and rolls playdough. Threads at least one large bead. |
| 4 | Mark-making | Marks in sand and flour; Chalk and water on the ground; Crayons on big paper | Children will make marks with fingers, chalk and crayons. Children will say what their marks show. | mark, draw, chalk, crayon, sand, paper | Makes marks with a tool. Says a word about their marks. |
| 5 | Vocabulary | Fruits and vegetables; Market people and things; Asking for things politely | Children will name fruits, vegetables and market things. Children will ask for something using a short phrase. | orange, mango, tomato, yam, market, seller | Names at least six fruits or vegetables. Uses a short phrase to ask for an item. |
| 6 | Rhymes and songs | Head, shoulders and body songs; Songs about animals; Songs with counting | Children will sing and do actions to a song. Children will remember words of a favourite song. | sing, song, head, hands, feet, jump | Sings along with several songs. Does the right action at the right word. |
| 7 | Stories | A story with props; Retelling in my own words; Puppets and the story | Children will retell a short story using props or puppets. Children will use words such as first, then and the end. | first, then, the end, puppet, story, tell | Retells part of a story with props. Uses a story word. |
| 8 | Speaking | Who is it?; What is it?; Where is it? | Children will answer simple who, what and where questions. Children will ask a simple question such as "What is that?" | who, what, where, ask, answer, yes, no | Answers who, what and where questions with a word or phrase. Asks one question. |
| 9 | Books and print | Words on signs and labels; My name card; Looking at print in books | Children will notice print on signs, labels and books. Children will recognise their own name card with support. | sign, label, letter, word, name card, read | Points to print and says what it is for. Finds their own name card among three. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising rhyme and syllables; Revising marks and fingers; Revising vocabulary and stories | Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record. | rhyme, clap, mark, story, name, book | Completes familiar tasks with growing independence. Each child has an observation record for Term 2. |

## Term 3: Term 3 introduces the first sounds s, a, t and p as listening and speaking games, develops pre-writing patterns, two-step instructions, story sequencing, own name and a first handmade book

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First sounds | Listening for /s/; Things that start with /s/; The /s/ song | Children will hear and say the sound /s/ at the start of words. Children will find objects that start with /s/ with support. | sun, sock, snake, sound, sss | Says /s/ clearly. Points to an object that starts with /s/. |
| 2 | First sounds | Listening for /a/; Things that start with /a/; The /a/ song | Children will hear and say the sound /a/ at the start of words. Children will find objects that start with /a/ with support. | ant, apple, axe, sound, aaa | Says /a/ clearly. Points to an object that starts with /a/. |
| 3 | First sounds | Listening for /t/; Listening for /p/; Which sound do you hear first? | Children will hear and say the sounds /t/ and /p/ at the start of words. Children will tell /t/ from /p/ with support. | tap, tin, pot, pan, sound, first | Says /t/ and /p/ clearly. Points to the right picture for /t/ or /p/. |
| 4 | First sounds | Sound hunt; Sound sorting; Sound bingo | Children will play games with the sounds /s/ /a/ /t/ /p/. Children will sort objects by their first sound with support. | sun, ant, tap, pot, sort, sound | Sorts pictures by first sound with support. Says at least two of the four sounds. |
| 5 | Pre-writing | Straight lines up and down; Round and curly patterns; Zigzag and wave | Children will make straight lines, circles and zigzags in sand, paint and on paper. Children will copy a simple pattern with support. | line, circle, zigzag, wave, up, down | Makes straight and round marks on purpose. Copies a simple pattern. |
| 6 | Listening | One-step instructions; Two-step instructions; Giving an instruction to a friend | Children will follow a two-part instruction. Children will give a simple instruction to a friend. | first, then, go, bring, put, listen | Follows a two-step instruction. Gives a one-step instruction. |
| 7 | Stories | Three-picture stories; First, then, last; Telling the story back | Children will put three pictures of a story in order. Children will use first, then and last with support. | first, then, last, story, picture, order | Orders three pictures. Uses at least one sequencing word. |
| 8 | Books and print | My name card; The first letter of my name; Names on things I own | Children will recognise their name card and the first letter of their name. Children will make marks to show their name with support. | name, letter, first, mine, card, write | Finds their name among several cards. Points to the first letter of their name with support. |
| 9 | Stories | Choosing a topic; Making pages with pictures and marks; Sharing my book | Children will make a simple book with pictures and marks. Children will share their book with a friend. | book, page, cover, picture, share, author | Makes a book with at least three pages. Tells a friend about one page. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising listening, rhymes and sounds; Revising stories and books; Celebration and end-of-year record | Children will show Year learning through games and stations. Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note. | listen, rhyme, sound, story, book, name | Takes part confidently in familiar literacy games. Each child has an end-of-year observation record. |
