# Toddler Literacy — Term 1 scheme of work

| Week | Topic and subtopics | Learning objectives | Content | Teaching activities | Learner activities | Resources | Assessment | Expected outcomes | Cross-curricular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Listening**: Listening walk; Inside and outside sounds; Guess the sound | Children will stop, listen and say or show what they hear. Children will match a sound to the thing that makes it with support. | Stopping to listen, finding the sources of everyday sounds inside and outside, and guessing hidden sounds. | Model listening with a finger to your ear and a calm face; whisper the instruction "Shh, listen"; name each sound clearly and give children time to point. | Stop, listen, point to or name what they hear, and join a guess-the-sound game. | A bell, a spoon and a cup, a rattle; A cloth bag; Pictures of a door, a bird, a car and a tap; A small drum | Observe whether each child stops to listen, turns towards a sound and points to its source. | Stays still to listen for a short time. Names or points to the source of a familiar sound. | Science: sounds in the environment; Social Habits: being quiet together; Physical Development: walking in a line |
| 2 | **Listening**: Loud and quiet; Using my voice; High and low voices | Children will make a loud and a quiet sound on request. Children will use their voices in play, such as a whisper and a shout. | Making loud and quiet sounds, using the voice for whispering, shouting and humming, and high and low voices. | Show loud with big arms and quiet with a finger to the lips; model each voice before children try; praise every attempt and keep shouting short. | Make loud and quiet sounds on request, whisper and shout, and copy high and low voices. | A drum; A shaker; A puppet; Picture cards of a lion and a mouse; A toy telephone | Observe whether each child changes the loudness of a sound on request and joins in voice play. | Changes the loudness of their voice on request. Uses a voice sound to join a game. | Music: dynamics; Social Habits: indoor and outdoor voices; Science: how sounds are made |
| 3 | **Sounds in nature**: Farm animals and their sounds; Birds and insects; Animal sound games | Children will match animal pictures to sounds. Children will make animal sounds and say the animal name. | Farm animals and their sounds, birds and insects, and games that match animal pictures to sounds. | Use real familiar Nigerian animals such as goat, hen, dog, cat and cow; say the name first, then make the sound; let children make the sound before you show the picture. | Name animals, make animal sounds, match pictures to sounds and join animal sound games. | Large pictures of a goat, hen, dog, cat, cow and bird; Soft toy animals; A basket; A recording of animal sounds if available | Observe whether each child names at least three animals and makes at least one animal sound. | Names at least three animals. Makes the sound of a familiar animal. | Science: animals and their homes; Social Habits: kindness to animals; Music: copying sounds |
| 4 | **Rhythm**: Drums and shakers; Clapping and stamping; Fast and slow | Children will copy a short beat with an instrument or their body. Children will say fast or slow with support. | Playing drums and shakers gently, clapping and stamping beats, and fast and slow sounds. | Play two-beat patterns and wait for children to copy; show fast with quick hands and slow with big gentle hands; model gentle playing and put instruments away together. | Play drums and shakers, clap and stamp beats, copy a short pattern and say fast or slow. | Two drums; Shakers made from bottles and rice; Rhythm sticks; A tortoise and a rabbit picture; A scarf | Observe whether each child copies a two-beat pattern and plays an instrument gently. | Copies a two-beat pattern. Plays an instrument gently. | Music: beat and rhythm; Physical Development: coordination; Social Habits: sharing instruments |
| 5 | **Speaking**: Good morning and goodbye; My name and my friends’ names; Please and thank you | Children will say or sign a greeting to an adult or friend. Children will say or point to their own name and a friend’s name. | Greeting adults and friends, learning names, and saying please and thank you. | Greet every child by name each morning; use a wave and a word together; model "Good morning ma" and "Good morning sir" as the school culture requires; praise every attempt. | Say or sign hello and goodbye, say or point to their own name and a friend’s, and use please and thank you. | A hello puppet; Name cards with photos; A basket of toys; A mirror | Observe whether each child greets someone, responds to their name and uses please or thank you with support. | Greets someone with a word or wave. Responds to their own name. | Social Habits: greetings and manners; Personal, Social and Emotional Development: friendship; Understanding the World: me and my family |
| 6 | **Rhymes and songs**: Finger rhymes; Action songs; Rhymes with a story | Children will join in actions and words of a short rhyme. Children will fill in a missing word with support. | Finger rhymes, action songs and a rhyme with a small story, with children filling in missing words. | Say or sing each rhyme slowly with big actions; repeat it several times; pause before the last word of a line so children can fill it in; use the same rhymes all week. | Join in the actions and words of short rhymes and fill in a missing word with support. | A hand puppet; A basket of toy fish, a toy spider and a toy bus; Picture cards for each rhyme; A scarf | Observe whether each child joins in at least two rhymes with actions and says the last word when you pause. | Joins in at least two rhymes with actions. Says the last word of a line when the teacher pauses. | Music: singing; Physical Development: finger control; Social Habits: taking turns |
| 7 | **Books**: Holding a book and turning pages; Pointing to and naming pictures; A favourite book | Children will hold a book the right way up and turn pages. Children will point to and name pictures. | Holding a book the right way up, turning pages, pointing to and naming pictures, and sharing a favourite book. | Hold the book so every child can see; run your finger under the pictures; name each picture and wait for the child to say it; let children turn pages with you. | Hold a book the right way up, turn pages one at a time, point to and name pictures. | Sturdy board books with large pictures; A book corner with cushions; A basket of books; A big book if available | Observe whether each child holds a book correctly, turns pages and names three pictures. | Turns pages one at a time with support. Names three pictures in a book. | Understanding the World: looking at pictures; Social Habits: caring for books; Physical Development: fine motor |
| 8 | **Vocabulary**: Family and people; Food we eat; Things in our classroom | Children will name familiar people, foods and objects. Children will say a word or two to ask for something. | Naming family, people, food and classroom objects, and using two words to ask for something. | Use real objects first and pictures second; name each item before asking for it; extend single words into two words, such as "more rice"; celebrate every word. | Name familiar people, foods and objects, and use two words together to ask for something. | Family photographs; Real foods or toy foods: rice, bread, banana, water; Classroom objects: chair, table, cup, ball; A basket | Observe whether each child names at least ten things and uses two words together to ask. | Names at least ten familiar things. Uses two words together to ask for something. | Understanding the World: family and community; Social Habits: asking politely; Mathematics: sorting |
| 9 | **Stories**: Listening to a short story; Joining in with a repeated line; Acting the story | Children will listen to a story with pictures and objects. Children will join in with a repeated line and actions. | Listening to a short story with pictures and objects, joining in with a repeated line, and acting the story. | Tell the same story all week using objects and a simple repeated line; use a different voice for each character; pause before the repeated line; let children act each part. | Listen to a short story, join in the repeated line and act the story with actions. | A story with a repeated line: "The Hungry Goat" written by the teacher; A toy goat, a basket, a leaf; Picture cards of the story; A scarf | Observe whether each child listens to the story and joins in the repeated line. | Listens to a short story. Joins in with a repeated line. | Understanding the World: animals; Physical Development: acting; Social Habits: listening to others |
| 10 | **Revision and assessment**: Revising listening and sound games; Revising rhymes and books; Revising words and names | Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | Revising listening and sound games, rhymes and books, and words and names through play stations and gathering Term 1 evidence. | Keep every activity familiar and playful; observe quietly and record without interrupting; offer help only when a child is stuck; celebrate what each child can do. | Show what they know about listening, rhymes, books, words and names through play. | Bell, rattle, drum and shakers; Rhyme picture cards; Board books; Name cards; Animal pictures; Class record sheets | Observe and record each child’s Term 1 literacy learning using the four-level scale. | Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. | Music: rhythm and singing; Understanding the World: family and animals; Social Habits: taking turns |
