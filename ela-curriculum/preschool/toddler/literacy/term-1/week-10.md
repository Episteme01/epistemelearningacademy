# Toddler Literacy — Term 1, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T1-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising listening and sound games; Revising rhymes and books; Revising words and names

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record.

**Key vocabulary:** listen, loud, quiet, rhyme, book, name

**Content:** Revising listening and sound games, rhymes and books, and words and names through play stations and gathering Term 1 evidence.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and playful; observe quietly and record without interrupting; offer help only when a child is stuck; celebrate what each child can do.

**Learner activities:** Show what they know about listening, rhymes, books, words and names through play.

**Resources:** Bell, rattle, drum and shakers; Rhyme picture cards; Board books; Name cards; Animal pictures; Class record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s Term 1 literacy learning using the four-level scale.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 1.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Music: rhythm and singing; Understanding the World: family and animals; Social Habits: taking turns

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising listening

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T1-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising listening and sound games

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit listening, loud and quiet, and guess the sound games.

### Success criteria
- Stops to listen
- Points to or names a sound source

**Previous knowledge:** Children have worked on listening, sounds, rhymes, books, words, names and a story this term.

**Key vocabulary:** listen, loud, quiet, rhyme, book, name

**Instructional materials:** A bell, a rattle, a drum; Picture cards; A cloth bag

### Starter
Whisper "Shh, listen" and ring the bell. Say "Today we play our favourite sound games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play listening statues with the bell. Children: Children stop and listen on the signal.

Step 2. Teacher: Play loud and quiet with the drum. Children: Children match loud and quiet.

Step 3. Teacher: Play guess the sound with the cloth bag. Children: Children point to the matching picture.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Sound games
1. Set up three game spaces.
2. Children play each game for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes who stops and listens.

**Safety:** Keep sounds gentle and the floor clear. Stop a game if a child covers their ears.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play with the child and use one sound.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game for friends.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What can you hear?
  **Expected response:** Bell, drum or rattle.
- **Ask:** Is it loud or quiet?
  **Expected response:** Child answers by word or action.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows listening skills
- Names or points to sound sources

### Conclusion
Ring the bell once and whisper "Good listening."

### Home connection
Play a listening game at home and ask your child to point to the sound.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit listening, loud and quiet, and guess the sound games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**

- LOUD
- QUIET

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Revising rhymes and books

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T1-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising rhymes and books

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit rhymes, songs and picture books.

### Success criteria
- Joins in a rhyme
- Holds a book and names a picture

**Previous knowledge:** Children have worked on listening, sounds, rhymes, books, words, names and a story this term.

**Key vocabulary:** listen, loud, quiet, rhyme, book, name

**Instructional materials:** Rhyme cards; Board books; A hen puppet

### Starter
Sing "Ten little fingers" and say "Let us sing our favourite rhymes."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing two favourite rhymes and pause for the last word. Children: Children join in and fill in the word.

Step 2. Teacher: Look at a book together and ask "What is this?" Children: Child names a picture.

Step 3. Teacher: Give each child a book to look at. Children: Child holds the book and turns pages.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a rhyme to sing. Children: Child chooses and sings.

### Practical activity: Rhyme and book time
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing a rhyme, then look at a book.
3. Repeat with a new rhyme and book.

**Safety:** Keep books safe on cushions. Handle books gently and supervise sharing.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Sing with the child and point to the pictures.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a rhyme.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Can you sing with me?
  **Expected response:** Child sings or does the actions.
- **Ask:** What is this picture?
  **Expected response:** Child names it.

### Expected outcomes
- Joins in rhymes
- Handles a book

### Conclusion
Sing a quiet goodbye rhyme.

### Home connection
Sing a rhyme and share a book at home every day.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit rhymes, songs and picture books" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- RHYMES
- BOOKS

**Picture suggestions:**
- Children singing
- A book

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Revising words and names

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T1-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising words and names

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit greetings, names, animals and familiar words.

### Success criteria
- Greets someone
- Names things and animals

**Previous knowledge:** Children have worked on listening, sounds, rhymes, books, words, names and a story this term.

**Key vocabulary:** listen, loud, quiet, rhyme, book, name

**Instructional materials:** Name cards; Animal pictures; Toy foods

### Starter
Wave and say "Hello, everyone. Let us say hello and name things."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the greeting circle with the puppet. Children: Children greet each other.

Step 2. Teacher: Hold up name cards and ask "Whose card?" Children: Child finds their card.

Step 3. Teacher: Show animal pictures and toy foods and ask "What is this?" Children: Child names the item.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child ask for something with please. Children: Child asks with a word or phrase.

### Practical activity: Words circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Pass a basket of objects and name them.
3. Greet each friend and say please and thank you.

**Safety:** Use only safe, large objects. Supervise passing and turn-taking.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two choices and say the word with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to name five things.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is your name?
  **Expected response:** Child says or points to their name.
- **Ask:** What is this?
  **Expected response:** Child names it.

### Expected outcomes
- Names familiar things
- Greets and uses polite words

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye to each friend by name.

### Home connection
Name things at home and greet family members with your child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit greetings, names, animals and familiar words" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**

- PLEASE

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 4 (Thursday): Play stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T1-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising listening and sound games

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 1 literacy learning at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows listening, speaking and book skills

**Previous knowledge:** Children have worked on listening, sounds, rhymes, books, words, names and a story this term.

**Key vocabulary:** listen, loud, quiet, rhyme, book, name

**Instructional materials:** Six station sets; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Sing the hello song and show six stations to the class.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: listening, rhymes, books, names, animals and story. Children: Children rotate every three minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they can do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Six stations
1. Set up six stations with familiar materials.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep floors clear and materials large and soft.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: listens; joins in rhymes; handles a book; names things; greets and responds to name; joins in a story.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a station.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 1 observation record
- Shows familiar skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Look at the term report and play a favourite game from this term.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 1 literacy learning at play stations" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 5 (Friday): Term 1 celebration

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-LIT-T1-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising words and names

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate Term 1 with songs, rhymes and a story, and teachers will complete their records.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and rhymes
- Shows what they enjoy

**Previous knowledge:** Children have worked on listening, sounds, rhymes, books, words, names and a story this term.

**Key vocabulary:** listen, loud, quiet, rhyme, book, name

**Instructional materials:** Favourite songs and rhymes; A book and a toy goat; Stickers or a certificate; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate. Let us sing our favourite songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite rhymes and songs with actions. Children: Children join in and choose a song.

Step 2. Teacher: Tell the goat story and wait for "munch, munch". Children: Children join in the story.

Step 3. Teacher: Give each child a sticker or praise and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing, tell the story and say each name.
3. Hand out stickers or certificates.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and sing along.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s Term 1 literacy record using today’s observations and the week’s notes. Record a next step for Term 2.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite song?
  **Expected response:** Child sings or points.
- **Ask:** Did you like the story?
  **Expected response:** Yes with a smile or word.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed Term 1 record
- Enjoys songs, rhymes and stories

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you in Term 2."

### Home connection
Keep singing and sharing books during the holiday and say hello to people you meet.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
