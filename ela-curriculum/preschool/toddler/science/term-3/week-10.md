# Toddler Science — Term 3, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising body, senses and materials; Revising living things; Celebration and end-of-year record

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Year learning through games and a science fair.
- Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note.

**Key vocabulary:** senses, plants, animals, push, light, clean

**Content:** Revising the body, senses and materials, living things, and celebrating the year with end-of-year records and a transition note.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly; record what children can do; invite parents to the celebration; complete the transition note honestly for the next teacher.

**Learner activities:** Show what they know through games and a small science fair and enjoy a celebration.

**Resources:** Body and senses cards; Float and sink objects; Plants and seeds; Push and pull toys; Torch and shadow puppets; Certificates or stickers; End-of-year record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s end-of-year science learning and complete the transition note.

## Expected outcomes
- Takes part confidently in familiar science games.
- Each child has an end-of-year observation record.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and stories; Numeracy: sorting and counting; Social Habits: celebration and thanks

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising body, senses and materials

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising body, senses and materials

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit the body, senses, water, sand, soil and materials through games.

### Success criteria
- Names body parts and senses
- Uses words such as wet, dry, float and sink

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about the body, senses, materials, living things, movement, light and caring this year.

**Key vocabulary:** senses, plants, animals, push, light, clean

**Instructional materials:** Five sense cards; A mystery bag; A water basin with float and sink objects; Material baskets

### Starter
Sing the body song and say "Today we play our favourite body, senses and materials games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the mystery bag game with touch. Children: Child feels and guesses.

Step 2. Teacher: Play float and sink with the basin. Children: Child puts an object in the water and says float or sink.

Step 3. Teacher: Sort objects into natural and made. Children: Child sorts the objects.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Body and materials games
1. Set up three game spaces.
2. Children play each for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes which words each child uses.

**Safety:** Keep water shallow and the floor clear. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play with the child and use two objects.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we see with?
  **Expected response:** Our eyes.
- **Ask:** Does it float or sink?
  **Expected response:** Float or sink.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows knowledge of body, senses and materials
- Uses science words

### Conclusion
Dry hands and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play body and materials games at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit the body, senses, water, sand, soil and materials through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Revising living things

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising living things

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit animals, plants, seeds and caring for living things.

### Success criteria
- Names animals and plants
- Cares for a plant

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about the body, senses, materials, living things, movement, light and caring this year.

**Key vocabulary:** senses, plants, animals, push, light, clean

**Instructional materials:** Animal and home cards; Grown plants; A watering can; Food pictures

### Starter
Look at our plants and say "Our plants have grown. Let us look after them."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Match animals to homes and babies to mothers. Children: Child matches the cards.

Step 2. Teacher: Look at the grown plants and name the parts. Children: Child names a leaf or flower.

Step 3. Teacher: Water the plants together. Children: Child waters a plant.

Step 4. Teacher: Sort foods into plant and animal foods. Children: Child sorts food pictures.

### Practical activity: Living things games
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate every five minutes.
3. The teacher notes names and care.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors dry. Wash hands after touching plants.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child and name each item.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to explain how to care for a plant.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What does a plant need?
  **Expected response:** Water, soil and sun.
- **Ask:** Where does the hen live?
  **Expected response:** In a nest.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows knowledge of living things
- Cares for plants gently

### Conclusion
Wash hands and say "We care for living things."

### Home connection
Keep caring for plants and animals at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit animals, plants, seeds and caring for living things" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Science fair stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising body, senses and materials

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s end-of-year science learning at a small science fair.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows body, senses, living things, movement and light knowledge

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about the body, senses, materials, living things, movement, light and caring this year.

**Key vocabulary:** senses, plants, animals, push, light, clean

**Instructional materials:** Six science stations; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Say "Today is our science fair. We visit six stations and show what we know."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: senses, float and sink, plants, push and pull, shadows and caring. Children: Children rotate every three minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they can do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Science fair
1. Set up six stations with familiar materials.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep water shallow, torches covered and floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: uses senses; says float or sink; names plants and animals; pushes and pulls; makes a shadow; cares for living things.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite station.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a station.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an end-of-year observation record
- Shows familiar skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite science game at home during the holiday.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s end-of-year science learning at a small science fair" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Science celebration

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Celebration and end-of-year record

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate the year with songs, experiments and plants, and show their work to visitors.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows something they enjoyed to an adult

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about the body, senses, materials, living things, movement, light and caring this year.

**Key vocabulary:** senses, plants, animals, push, light, clean

**Instructional materials:** Favourite songs; The grown plants and children’s work; Certificates or stickers; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate everything we discovered. Let us sing our favourite songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite songs and rhymes with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Invite children to show their plants or work to a visitor or parent. Children: Child shows their work.

Step 3. Teacher: Give each child a sticker or certificate and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle with visitors.
2. Sing and share work.
3. Hand out certificates or stickers.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Supervise visitors and keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and share their work together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s end-of-year science record from the year’s observations. Record the child’s strengths and a next step for Nursery 1.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite thing?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** What did you learn?
  **Expected response:** A word, fact or action.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed end-of-year record
- Enjoys science and nature

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you in Nursery 1."

## Day 5 (Friday): Transition note and records

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Celebration and end-of-year record

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will complete a transition note for each child for the Nursery 1 teacher.

### Success criteria
- Records what each child can do
- Records a next step

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about the body, senses, materials, living things, movement, light and caring this year.

**Key vocabulary:** senses, plants, animals, push, light, clean

**Instructional materials:** Transition note template; Term records; Children’s portfolios; A quiet table

### Starter
Today the children enjoy free play with familiar materials while the teacher completes the transition notes.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set out free play with sand, water, plants and puzzles. Children: Children play freely and happily.

Step 2. Teacher: Review each child’s three term records and portfolio. Children: Children play while the teacher reviews.

Step 3. Teacher: Complete the note: strengths, interests, language, science skills and next steps. Children: Children play and talk with the helper.

### Practical activity: Free play and records
1. Set out four play areas with a helper.
2. The teacher reviews records at a quiet table.
3. The teacher completes each transition note.

**Safety:** A second adult must supervise play while the teacher completes the notes. Keep water shallow and all areas safe and tidy.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer familiar toys and a helper nearby.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to help a friend tidy up.

### Assessment
Write for each child: what they can do in using the senses, exploring materials, knowing plants and animals, movement and caring; their interests; any support needed; and a next step. Use the four-level scale and facts you have seen.

- **Ask:** What do you like to play?
  **Expected response:** A favourite game or toy.
- **Ask:** What did you discover this year?
  **Expected response:** A word, fact or action.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a transition note for each child
- Plays happily in familiar activities

### Conclusion
Tidy up together and say goodbye to the year.

### Home connection
Share the end-of-year report with your child and keep exploring together.
