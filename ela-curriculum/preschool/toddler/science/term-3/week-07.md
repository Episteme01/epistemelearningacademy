# Toddler Science — Term 3, Week 7: Nature

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W07 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Colour walk; Colours of flowers and leaves; Making a nature collage

## Learning objectives
- Children will find colours in nature on a walk.
- Children will make a collage with natural materials.

**Key vocabulary:** red, green, yellow, brown, colour, nature

**Content:** Finding colours in nature on a walk, noticing the colours of flowers and leaves, and making a nature collage.

**Teaching activities:** Use colour cards on a walk; let children collect fallen items only; name each colour; link to colour words learnt in Literacy and Numeracy; accept all creative collages.

**Learner activities:** Find colours in nature and make a collage with leaves and flowers.

**Resources:** Colour cards: red, green, yellow, brown; Baskets; Fallen leaves and flowers; Glue sticks; Large paper; Clipboards

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child finds three colours in nature and makes a collage.

## Expected outcomes
- Finds three colours in nature.
- Makes a collage with leaves or flowers.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Creative Development: collage; Literacy: colour words; Numeracy: sorting by colour

## Day 1 (Monday): Colour walk

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W07-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Colour walk

### Learning objectives
- Children will find colours in nature on a walk.

### Success criteria
- Finds something that matches a colour card
- Names the colour

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name materials and sort natural and made things.

**Key vocabulary:** red, green, yellow, brown, colour, nature

**Instructional materials:** Colour cards: red, green, yellow, brown; Baskets; A safe outdoor area

### Starter
Show the green card and say "Let us find something green in nature."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each colour card and say its name. Children: Children look and say the colour.

Step 2. Teacher: Walk to the garden in a line with colour cards. Children: Children walk in a line.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Find something green" and let children search. Children: Child finds a green leaf and holds it to the card.

Step 4. Teacher: Find one thing for each colour. Children: Child finds and names the colour.

### Practical activity: Colour hunt
1. Give each pair a colour card.
2. Find a matching natural thing.
3. Put it in the basket.

**Safety:** Check the area for sharp objects, thorns and insects first. Collect only fallen items and wash hands after.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Walk with the child and point to something that matches.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find two things for each colour.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What colour is the leaf?
  **Expected response:** Green.
- **Ask:** What is brown?
  **Expected response:** A stick or soil.

### Expected outcomes
- Finds colours in nature
- Names at least three colours

### Conclusion
Put the objects in a basket and say "Colours everywhere."

### Home connection
Look for colours in nature on a walk with your child.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Colours of flowers and leaves

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W07-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Colours of flowers and leaves

### Learning objectives
- Children will compare the colours of flowers and leaves.

### Success criteria
- Names flower and leaf colours
- Sorts by colour

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name materials and sort natural and made things.

**Key vocabulary:** red, green, yellow, brown, colour, nature

**Instructional materials:** Fallen flowers and leaves; Colour trays or paper; A magnifying glass

### Starter
Hold up a yellow flower and a green leaf and say "The flower is yellow. The leaf is green."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show different flowers and leaves and name the colours. Children: Children look and say the colours.

Step 2. Teacher: Look closely with a magnifying glass at the colours and patterns. Children: Children look and point.

Step 3. Teacher: Sort the flowers and leaves by colour onto colour paper. Children: Child sorts by colour.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "How many yellow things do we have?" Children: Child counts with support.

### Practical activity: Colour sort
1. Put coloured paper on the mat.
2. Sort flowers and leaves by colour.
3. Count each group.

**Safety:** Use only fallen leaves and flowers and wash hands after. Do not allow flowers in mouths.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two colours and say the colour first.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find leaves that are not green.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What colour is the flower?
  **Expected response:** Red, yellow or pink.
- **Ask:** What colour is the leaf?
  **Expected response:** Green or brown.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts flowers and leaves by colour
- Names colours of plants

### Conclusion
Put the flowers in a vase and say "Thank you."

### Home connection
Look at the colours of flowers and leaves at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Making a nature collage

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W07-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Making a nature collage

### Learning objectives
- Children will make a collage with natural materials.

### Success criteria
- Sticks natural materials on paper
- Says what they made

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name materials and sort natural and made things.

**Key vocabulary:** red, green, yellow, brown, colour, nature

**Instructional materials:** Fallen leaves, petals, seeds and sticks; Large paper; Glue sticks or flour paste; Aprons

### Starter
Show a collage made of leaves and petals and say "We can make pictures from nature."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to put glue on paper and stick leaves. Children: Children watch and get glue.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child choose natural things and stick them. Children: Child sticks leaves and petals on paper.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "What did you make?" Children: Child says a word such as flower or tree.

Step 4. Teacher: Display the collages together. Children: Child shows their collage.

### Practical activity: Nature collage
1. Put out natural materials.
2. Stick them on large paper.
3. Talk about the picture.

**Safety:** Use non-toxic glue and avoid small seeds for children who put things in their mouths. Wash hands after.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Stick the leaf together and name the picture.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a collage of a tree with leaves.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did you make?
  **Expected response:** A tree, flower or animal.
- **Ask:** What did you use?
  **Expected response:** Leaves and petals.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes a collage with natural materials
- Says what they made

### Conclusion
Put the collages on the wall and say "Well done."

### Home connection
Make a nature picture at home with leaves and paper.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 4 (Thursday): Colour sorting game

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W07-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Colour walk

### Learning objectives
- Children will sort natural objects and pictures by colour.

### Success criteria
- Sorts by colour
- Says the colour

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name materials and sort natural and made things.

**Key vocabulary:** red, green, yellow, brown, colour, nature

**Instructional materials:** Coloured baskets or paper; A mix of natural objects and pictures; A bag

### Starter
Say "I have a bag of nature things. Let us sort them by colour."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Pick an object from the bag and say its colour. Children: Children say the colour.

Step 2. Teacher: Put it in the matching coloured basket. Children: Children watch and say the colour.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child pick an object and sort it. Children: Child sorts by colour.

Step 4. Teacher: Check each basket together. Children: Children name the colours.

### Practical activity: Colour baskets
1. Put coloured baskets on the mat.
2. Each child picks an object.
3. Put it in the matching basket.

**Safety:** Use only safe, large objects. Supervise turns and keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two baskets and say the colour first.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find an object that is two colours.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What colour is this?
  **Expected response:** The colour of the object.
- **Ask:** Which basket does it go in?
  **Expected response:** The matching colour.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts objects by colour
- Names colours

### Conclusion
Put the objects away and say the colours.

### Home connection
Sort things by colour at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "sort natural objects and pictures by colour" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 7 colours check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W07-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Making a nature collage

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child finds colours in nature and makes a collage.

### Success criteria
- Finds three colours in nature
- Makes a collage

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name materials and sort natural and made things.

**Key vocabulary:** red, green, yellow, brown, colour, nature

**Instructional materials:** Colour cards; Collage materials; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the colour song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: colour sort, flowers and leaves, and collage. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What colour is it?" and "What did you make?" Children: Child answers with a word or gesture.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children wash hands and tidy.

### Practical activity: Colour stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use non-toxic materials and wash hands.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use two colours.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to describe their collage.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: finds colours in nature; sorts by colour; makes a collage.

- **Ask:** What colour is this?
  **Expected response:** A colour name.
- **Ask:** What did you make?
  **Expected response:** A collage picture.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 7
- Finds and names colours in nature

### Conclusion
Wash hands and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Look for colours in nature each day.

- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child finds colours in nature and makes a collage" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
