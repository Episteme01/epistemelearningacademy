# Toddler Science — Term 3 scheme of work

| Week | Topic and subtopics | Learning objectives | Content | Teaching activities | Learner activities | Resources | Assessment | Expected outcomes | Cross-curricular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Movement**: Pushing a toy; Pulling a toy; Push and pull games | Children will push and pull toys and say push or pull. Children will notice that pushing moves things away and pulling brings them closer. | Pushing and pulling toys and objects, noticing that pushing moves things away and pulling brings them closer, and push and pull games. | Use large light objects that children can move easily; say push or pull as the action happens; let children feel the effort; link to everyday actions such as opening a door. | Push and pull toys on request and say push or pull with support. | Toy carts and boxes with string; Large soft balls; A light box; A rope; A door that opens both ways | Observe whether each child pushes and pulls a toy on request and says push or pull. | Pushes and pulls a toy on request. Says push or pull with support. | Physical Development: strength and control; Literacy: action words; Numeracy: near and far |
| 2 | **Movement**: Things that roll; Things that slide; Ramps and slopes | Children will test objects to see which roll and which slide. Children will roll a ball or car down a ramp. | Finding which things roll and which things slide, and rolling a ball or car down a ramp. | Let children test objects freely; name each test result; use a ramp made from a board on a low box; keep the slope gentle; let children predict by pointing. | Test objects to see which roll and which slide and roll a car down a ramp. | A smooth board ramp; A ball, a toy car, a block, a tin and a cup; Cones or a finish line; A tray | Observe whether each child says roll or slide after trying and rolls a car down a ramp. | Says roll or slide after trying. Rolls a car down a ramp. | Numeracy: fast and slow; Physical Development: rolling and sliding; Literacy: describing words |
| 3 | **Light**: Light and dark; Making shadows; Shadow puppets | Children will notice that light comes from the sun and a torch. Children will make and watch shadows. | Noticing light and dark, making shadows with the sun and a torch, and shadow puppets. | Never let children look at the sun; use a torch with a firm cover and shine it on a wall; darken the room only as much as needed; stay close in dim light; use simple words. | Say light or dark for a place, make a shadow and play with shadow puppets. | A torch; A white wall or sheet; A dark box or tent; Cardboard shapes on sticks; Chalk for outdoor shadows | Observe whether each child says light or dark for a place and makes a shadow with a hand or toy. | Says light or dark for a place. Makes a shadow with a hand or toy. | Literacy: stories with shadows; Creative Development: puppets; Understanding the World: sun and sky |
| 4 | **Light**: What we see in the day; What we see at night; Sleep and rest | Children will say what happens in the day and what happens at night. Children will match pictures to day or night. | What we see in the day, what we see at night, and why we sleep and rest. | Use large day and night pictures; link to children’s own routines; sing a goodnight song; be sensitive to different family routines; never frighten children about the dark. | Sort pictures into day and night and say that we sleep at night. | Day and night pictures: sun, moon, stars, birds, a bed, a school; A yellow and a dark blue sheet; A soft toy and a blanket; Two baskets | Observe whether each child sorts pictures into day and night and says we sleep at night. | Sorts pictures into day and night. Says that we sleep at night. | Literacy: bedtime story; Numeracy: sorting; Social Habits: routines |
| 5 | **Materials**: What gets wet; What stays dry; Drying in the sun | Children will test which materials get wet and which stay dry. Children will notice that wet things dry in the sun. | Testing which materials get wet and which stay dry, and noticing that wet things dry in the sun. | Let children test with a spray bottle or a dripping sponge; use the words wet and dry for every result; hang cloths in the sun and look again later; keep water use small and controlled. | Test which materials get wet and which stay dry and see that wet things dry in the sun. | A spray bottle or sponge; Paper, cloth, plastic, foil and a leaf; An umbrella and a raincoat; A washing line; A tray | Observe whether each child says wet or dry after testing and notices a wet cloth drying in the sun. | Says wet or dry for materials after testing. Notices that a wet cloth dries in the sun. | Numeracy: sorting; Literacy: describing words; Understanding the World: weather |
| 6 | **Materials**: Things from nature; Things people make; Sorting natural and made | Children will sort objects into natural and made with support. Children will name materials such as wood, cloth and plastic. | Things from nature, things people make, and sorting natural and made things. | Use real objects such as leaves, stones, wood, cloth and plastic; say what each thing is made of; keep the sorting simple with two baskets; link to what children see around the school. | Sort objects into natural and made and name materials such as wood, cloth and plastic. | Leaves, stones, shells, sticks, a flower; A plastic cup, a cloth, a wooden spoon, a metal key, a toy car; Two baskets; Pictures of natural and made things | Observe whether each child sorts objects into natural and made with support and names two materials. | Sorts objects into natural and made with support. Names two materials. | Numeracy: sorting; Literacy: describing words; Creative Development: making with materials |
| 7 | **Nature**: Colour walk; Colours of flowers and leaves; Making a nature collage | Children will find colours in nature on a walk. Children will make a collage with natural materials. | Finding colours in nature on a walk, noticing the colours of flowers and leaves, and making a nature collage. | Use colour cards on a walk; let children collect fallen items only; name each colour; link to colour words learnt in Literacy and Numeracy; accept all creative collages. | Find colours in nature and make a collage with leaves and flowers. | Colour cards: red, green, yellow, brown; Baskets; Fallen leaves and flowers; Glue sticks; Large paper; Clipboards | Observe whether each child finds three colours in nature and makes a collage. | Finds three colours in nature. Makes a collage with leaves or flowers. | Creative Development: collage; Literacy: colour words; Numeracy: sorting by colour |
| 8 | **Health**: Washing hands; Brushing teeth; Healthy habits | Children will wash hands and say when we wash them. Children will practise brushing teeth with a model. | Washing hands, brushing teeth with a model, and healthy habits. | Use real soap and water for hand washing; use a model mouth and a big toothbrush for teeth; sing a short washing song; model each step; make hand washing part of the daily routine. | Wash hands with soap, practise brushing teeth with a model and name healthy habits. | A basin, soap and towels; A teeth model and a large toothbrush; Pictures of healthy habits; A mirror; Glitter or flour to show germs | Observe whether each child washes hands with soap with support and says one healthy habit. | Washes hands with soap with support. Says one healthy habit. | Social Habits: hygiene routines; Literacy: washing song; Physical Development: self-care |
| 9 | **Environment**: Litter in the bin; Keeping our school clean; Saving water | Children will put litter in a bin and say why we keep places clean. Children will turn off a tap after use. | Putting litter in the bin, keeping the school clean, and saving water. | Use clean, safe litter such as paper and clean plastic for practice; never touch real litter; model putting litter in the bin; show a tap being turned off; link to the school compound. | Put litter in a bin, say why we keep places clean and turn off a tap after use. | Clean paper and empty plastic bottles for pretend litter; A class bin and a recycling box; Gloves for adults; A basin with a tap; Pictures of a clean and a littered place | Observe whether each child puts litter in a bin and turns off a tap with support. | Puts litter in a bin. Turns off a tap with support. | Social Habits: responsibility; Literacy: clean and tidy words; Understanding the World: our environment |
| 10 | **Revision and assessment**: Revising body, senses and materials; Revising living things; Celebration and end-of-year record | Children will show Year learning through games and a science fair. Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note. | Revising the body, senses and materials, living things, and celebrating the year with end-of-year records and a transition note. | Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly; record what children can do; invite parents to the celebration; complete the transition note honestly for the next teacher. | Show what they know through games and a small science fair and enjoy a celebration. | Body and senses cards; Float and sink objects; Plants and seeds; Push and pull toys; Torch and shadow puppets; Certificates or stickers; End-of-year record sheets | Observe and record each child’s end-of-year science learning and complete the transition note. | Takes part confidently in familiar science games. Each child has an end-of-year observation record. | Literacy: songs and stories; Numeracy: sorting and counting; Social Habits: celebration and thanks |
