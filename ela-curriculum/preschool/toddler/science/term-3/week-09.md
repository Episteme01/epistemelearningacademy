# Toddler Science — Term 3, Week 9: Environment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W09 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Litter in the bin; Keeping our school clean; Saving water

## Learning objectives
- Children will put litter in a bin and say why we keep places clean.
- Children will turn off a tap after use.

**Key vocabulary:** litter, bin, clean, tidy, tap, save

**Content:** Putting litter in the bin, keeping the school clean, and saving water.

**Teaching activities:** Use clean, safe litter such as paper and clean plastic for practice; never touch real litter; model putting litter in the bin; show a tap being turned off; link to the school compound.

**Learner activities:** Put litter in a bin, say why we keep places clean and turn off a tap after use.

**Resources:** Clean paper and empty plastic bottles for pretend litter; A class bin and a recycling box; Gloves for adults; A basin with a tap; Pictures of a clean and a littered place

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child puts litter in a bin and turns off a tap with support.

## Expected outcomes
- Puts litter in a bin.
- Turns off a tap with support.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Social Habits: responsibility; Literacy: clean and tidy words; Understanding the World: our environment

## Day 1 (Monday): Litter in the bin

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W09-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Litter in the bin

### Learning objectives
- Children will put litter in a bin and say why.

### Success criteria
- Puts pretend litter in a bin
- Says litter goes in the bin

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to care for plants, animals and themselves.

**Key vocabulary:** litter, bin, clean, tidy, tap, save

**Instructional materials:** Clean paper and empty plastic bottles; A bin; Pictures of a clean and a littered place

### Starter
Show a picture of a littered playground and say "This place is dirty. How can we make it clean?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show a picture of a clean place and say "This is nice." Children: Children look and say clean.

Step 2. Teacher: Drop a clean paper on the floor and say "Oh no, litter!" Pick it up and put it in the bin. Children: Children watch and say bin.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child pick up pretend litter and put it in the bin. Children: Child puts litter in the bin.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "Where does litter go?" Children: Child points to the bin.

### Practical activity: Litter in the bin
1. Scatter clean paper on the floor.
2. Children pick it up and put it in the bin.
3. Look at the clean floor.

**Safety:** Use only clean paper and plastic and never let children touch real litter. Wash hands after. An adult handles anything unsafe.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Pick up the paper together and put it in the bin.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell a friend why we use bins.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where does litter go?
  **Expected response:** In the bin.
- **Ask:** Is the floor clean now?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Puts litter in a bin
- Says litter goes in the bin

### Conclusion
Look at the clean floor and say "Well done."

### Home connection
Show your child how to put litter in a bin at home and outside.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "put litter in a bin and say why" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**

- BIN
- CLEAN

**Picture suggestions:**

**Song or rhyme:** Pick it up, pick it up, put it in the bin.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Keeping our school clean

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W09-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Keeping our school clean

### Learning objectives
- Children will help to keep their classroom and playground tidy.

### Success criteria
- Tidies toys away
- Helps to sweep or wipe with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to care for plants, animals and themselves.

**Key vocabulary:** litter, bin, clean, tidy, tap, save

**Instructional materials:** A small broom and dustpan; Cloths; Baskets for toys; Pictures of a tidy classroom

### Starter
Look around the room and say "Our classroom is messy. Let us make it tidy."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to put toys in baskets. Children: Children put toys in baskets.

Step 2. Teacher: Show how to wipe a table with a cloth. Children: Child wipes a table.

Step 3. Teacher: Show how to sweep with a small broom. Children: Child sweeps with support.

Step 4. Teacher: Look at the tidy room together. Children: Child says tidy or clean.

### Practical activity: Tidy up time
1. Put toys in baskets.
2. Wipe the tables.
3. Sweep the floor with a small broom.

**Safety:** Use child-size tools and supervise closely. Wipe only with clean water and never use cleaning chemicals with children.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Tidy together and name each job.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say which job they like best.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do with toys?
  **Expected response:** Put them in the basket.
- **Ask:** Is the room tidy?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Helps to keep the room tidy
- Says tidy and clean

### Conclusion
Look at the tidy room and say "Thank you, helpers."

### Home connection
Give your child a small tidying job at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Saving water

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W09-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Saving water

### Learning objectives
- Children will turn off a tap after use and say why we save water.

### Success criteria
- Turns off a tap
- Says that we save water

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to care for plants, animals and themselves.

**Key vocabulary:** litter, bin, clean, tidy, tap, save

**Instructional materials:** A basin with a tap or a jug with a tap; A bucket; Pictures of a dripping tap and a tap turned off

### Starter
Show a dripping tap picture and say "Drip, drip, drip. The water is wasted."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to turn off a tap tightly after washing. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child turn on a tap, wash hands and turn it off. Children: Child turns off the tap.

Step 3. Teacher: Collect a cup of water from the tap and talk about how precious water is. Children: Children look at the water.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We save water." Children: Children say the sentence.

### Practical activity: Turn off the tap
1. Wash hands at the tap.
2. Turn it off tightly.
3. Collect used water for plants.

**Safety:** Supervise every child at the tap and keep floors dry. Use only clean water for plants.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Turn off the tap together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell a friend why we save water.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do after washing?
  **Expected response:** Turn off the tap.
- **Ask:** Why do we save water?
  **Expected response:** Because water is precious.

### Expected outcomes
- Turns off a tap
- Says that we save water

### Conclusion
Look at the dry tap and say "No drips."

### Home connection
Turn off the tap together at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "turn off a tap after use and say why we save water" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 4 (Thursday): Our clean-up team

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W09-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Keeping our school clean

### Learning objectives
- Children will work as a team to tidy the playground.

### Success criteria
- Helps to tidy outdoors
- Works with a friend

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to care for plants, animals and themselves.

**Key vocabulary:** litter, bin, clean, tidy, tap, save

**Instructional materials:** Child-size gloves; Buckets; Clean pretend litter; A bin

### Starter
Say "We are the clean-up team. Let us make our playground clean and safe."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Check the playground first and remove any real litter or sharp things yourself. Children: Children wait together.

Step 2. Teacher: Put out clean pretend litter and let children collect it. Children: Child picks up pretend litter and puts it in a bucket.

Step 3. Teacher: Put the pretend litter in the bin. Children: Child puts litter in the bin.

Step 4. Teacher: Look at the clean playground and cheer. Children: Children clap and say well done.

### Practical activity: Clean-up team
1. Spread clean pretend litter on the playground.
2. Collect it in buckets.
3. Put it in the bin.

**Safety:** An adult must clear real litter and sharp objects first. Children touch only the clean pretend litter and wash hands after.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Work beside the child and pick up together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say how they helped.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did we do?
  **Expected response:** We cleaned up.
- **Ask:** Is the playground clean?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Helps to tidy outdoors
- Works with a friend

### Conclusion
Wash hands and say "We are a team."

### Home connection

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 9 environment check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W09-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Saving water

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child cares for the environment.

### Success criteria
- Puts litter in a bin
- Turns off a tap with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt to care for plants, animals and themselves.

**Key vocabulary:** litter, bin, clean, tidy, tap, save

**Instructional materials:** Pretend litter; A bin; A basin with a tap; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the pick it up song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: litter, tidying and tap. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What do we do?" and watch. Children: Child answers with a word or action.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children wash hands and tidy.

### Practical activity: Environment stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors dry. Use only clean pretend litter.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to tell a friend how to care for the world.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: puts litter in a bin; helps to tidy; turns off a tap; says we save water.

- **Ask:** Where does litter go?
  **Expected response:** In the bin.
- **Ask:** What do we do with a tap?
  **Expected response:** Turn it off.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 9
- Cares for the environment

### Conclusion
Wash hands and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep caring for the world at home.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
