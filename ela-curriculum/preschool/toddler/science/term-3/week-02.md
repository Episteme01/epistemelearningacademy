# Toddler Science — Term 3, Week 2: Movement

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W02 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Things that roll; Things that slide; Ramps and slopes

## Learning objectives
- Children will test objects to see which roll and which slide.
- Children will roll a ball or car down a ramp.

**Key vocabulary:** roll, slide, ball, ramp, fast, slow

**Content:** Finding which things roll and which things slide, and rolling a ball or car down a ramp.

**Teaching activities:** Let children test objects freely; name each test result; use a ramp made from a board on a low box; keep the slope gentle; let children predict by pointing.

**Learner activities:** Test objects to see which roll and which slide and roll a car down a ramp.

**Resources:** A smooth board ramp; A ball, a toy car, a block, a tin and a cup; Cones or a finish line; A tray

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child says roll or slide after trying and rolls a car down a ramp.

## Expected outcomes
- Says roll or slide after trying.
- Rolls a car down a ramp.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Numeracy: fast and slow; Physical Development: rolling and sliding; Literacy: describing words

## Day 1 (Monday): Things that roll

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W02-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Things that roll

### Learning objectives
- Children will find things that roll.

### Success criteria
- Rolls a ball and a tin
- Says roll

**Previous knowledge:** Children can push and pull objects and say the words.

**Key vocabulary:** roll, slide, ball, ramp, fast, slow

**Instructional materials:** A ball, a tin and a toy wheel; A flat floor space; A block

### Starter
Roll a ball across the floor and say "Roll, roll, roll. The ball rolls."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Roll the ball, the tin and the wheel one at a time and say "Roll." Children: Children watch and say roll.

Step 2. Teacher: Try to roll a block and see what happens. Children: Children watch it stop.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child roll an object. Children: Child rolls an object and says roll.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "What rolls?" and point to objects. Children: Child points to rolling things.

### Practical activity: Roll it
1. Sit in a circle and roll objects to a friend.
2. Say roll as it goes.
3. Try rolling a block.

**Safety:** Sit with legs apart and roll objects slowly. Use soft items and clear the floor of obstacles.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Roll the ball together and watch it go.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find another thing that rolls.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Does the ball roll?
  **Expected response:** Yes.
- **Ask:** Does the block roll?
  **Expected response:** No.

### Expected outcomes
- Says roll for things that roll
- Finds objects that roll

### Conclusion
Roll the ball slowly to each child and say goodbye.

### Home connection
Roll different things at home and see which roll.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "find things that roll" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- ROLL
- BALL

**Picture suggestions:**
- A rolling ball
- A tin on its side

**Song or rhyme:** Roll, roll, roll the ball. Roll, roll, roll.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Things that slide

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W02-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Things that slide

### Learning objectives
- Children will find things that slide.

### Success criteria
- Slides an object on a smooth surface
- Says slide

**Previous knowledge:** Children can push and pull objects and say the words.

**Key vocabulary:** roll, slide, ball, ramp, fast, slow

**Instructional materials:** A block, a book and a cloth on a smooth tray; A smooth board; A playground slide if available

### Starter
Slide a block along the smooth board and say "Slide, slide, slide."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Slide objects along a smooth surface and say "Slide." Children: Children watch and say slide.

Step 2. Teacher: Try a rough surface and see that it is harder to slide. Children: Children watch and say stop.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child slide an object. Children: Child slides an object.

Step 4. Teacher: Go to the playground slide with an adult and slide down safely. Children: Child slides down and says slide.

### Practical activity: Slide it
1. Put a smooth board on a table.
2. Slide objects along it.
3. Compare with a rough mat.

**Safety:** Supervise the playground slide closely and let children slide one at a time. Keep the board low and steady.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Slide the object together and say slide.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find which surface is best for sliding.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Does the block slide?
  **Expected response:** Yes.
- **Ask:** Is the rough mat good for sliding?
  **Expected response:** No.

### Expected outcomes
- Says slide for things that slide
- Slides an object

### Conclusion
Put the objects away and say "Slide, slide."

### Home connection
Slide a toy on a smooth floor or table at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "find things that slide" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- SLIDE
- SMOOTH

**Picture suggestions:**

- A child on a slide

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Roll or slide?

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W02-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Things that roll

### Learning objectives
- Children will test objects and decide if they roll or slide.

### Success criteria
- Tests an object
- Says roll or slide

**Previous knowledge:** Children can push and pull objects and say the words.

**Key vocabulary:** roll, slide, ball, ramp, fast, slow

**Instructional materials:** Six objects: ball, tin, cup, block, book, toy car; Two hoops labelled roll and slide

### Starter
Show a tin and a block and ask "Which one will roll? Let us try."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Hold up an object and ask for a guess. Children: Children guess with a word or point.

Step 2. Teacher: Test the object on the floor. Children: Children watch and say roll or slide.

Step 3. Teacher: Put the object in the roll or slide hoop. Children: Child places the object in the right hoop.

Step 4. Teacher: Check each hoop together. Children: Children say roll or slide for each object.

### Practical activity: Test and sort
1. Choose an object and guess.
2. Test it on the floor.
3. Sort it into roll or slide hoops.

**Safety:** Use large objects only and keep the floor clear. Supervise sharing.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two objects and test them together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find a new object and test it.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Does the tin roll?
  **Expected response:** Yes.
- **Ask:** Does the book roll?
  **Expected response:** No, it slides.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts objects into roll and slide
- Tests objects with support

### Conclusion
Put the objects away and say "Roll and slide."

### Home connection
Test which things roll and which slide at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "test objects and decide if they roll or slide" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- ROLL
- SLIDE

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 4 (Thursday): Ramp rolling

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W02-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Ramps and slopes

### Learning objectives
- Children will roll a ball or a car down a ramp.

### Success criteria
- Rolls a car down a ramp
- Says fast or slow with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children can push and pull objects and say the words.

**Key vocabulary:** roll, slide, ball, ramp, fast, slow

**Instructional materials:** A smooth board ramp on a low box; A toy car and a ball; A cone at the finish

### Starter
Roll a ball down a ramp and say "Down the ramp it goes! Fast, fast."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up a gentle ramp and roll a ball down. Children: Children watch and say fast.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child roll a car down the ramp. Children: Child rolls the car and watches.

Step 3. Teacher: Make the ramp steeper and then flatter and see what changes. Children: Children watch and say fast or slow.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "Which is faster, the car or the block?" Children: Child points to the faster one.

### Practical activity: Ramp race
1. Set up a low ramp.
2. Roll cars and balls down.
3. See which goes fastest.

**Safety:** Keep the ramp low and steady. Put a cone or a box at the end so objects do not roll away. Keep fingers out of the way.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Roll the car together and watch it go.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to guess which toy will go the furthest.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Does the car go fast or slow?
  **Expected response:** Fast.
- **Ask:** What happens on a flat ramp?
  **Expected response:** It goes slowly.

### Expected outcomes
- Rolls a car down a ramp
- Says fast or slow

### Conclusion
Put the ramp away and say "Down, down, down."

### Home connection
Make a ramp with a book and roll a toy car down it.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "roll a ball or a car down a ramp" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- RAMP
- FAST
- SLOW

**Picture suggestions:**
- A ball on a ramp
- A toy car on a ramp

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 2 roll and slide check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W02-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Ramps and slopes

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child tests objects and uses roll and slide words.

### Success criteria
- Says roll or slide after trying
- Rolls a car down a ramp

**Previous knowledge:** Children can push and pull objects and say the words.

**Key vocabulary:** roll, slide, ball, ramp, fast, slow

**Instructional materials:** Objects; A ramp; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the roll and slide song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: roll, slide and ramp. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "Roll or slide?" Children: Child answers with a word or action.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Roll and slide stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Keep ramps low.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one object.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to explain roll and slide to a friend.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: says roll or slide; tests objects; rolls a car down a ramp.

- **Ask:** Does it roll or slide?
  **Expected response:** Roll or slide.
- **Ask:** Can you roll it down the ramp?
  **Expected response:** Child rolls it.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 2
- Tests and describes movement

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye with a roll and a slide.

### Home connection
Roll and slide toys at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child tests objects and uses roll and slide words" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Roll and slide

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of stations
