# Toddler Science — Term 3, Week 4: Light

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W04 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** What we see in the day; What we see at night; Sleep and rest

## Learning objectives
- Children will say what happens in the day and what happens at night.
- Children will match pictures to day or night.

**Key vocabulary:** day, night, sun, moon, stars, sleep

**Content:** What we see in the day, what we see at night, and why we sleep and rest.

**Teaching activities:** Use large day and night pictures; link to children’s own routines; sing a goodnight song; be sensitive to different family routines; never frighten children about the dark.

**Learner activities:** Sort pictures into day and night and say that we sleep at night.

**Resources:** Day and night pictures: sun, moon, stars, birds, a bed, a school; A yellow and a dark blue sheet; A soft toy and a blanket; Two baskets

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child sorts pictures into day and night and says we sleep at night.

## Expected outcomes
- Sorts pictures into day and night.
- Says that we sleep at night.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: bedtime story; Numeracy: sorting; Social Habits: routines

## Day 1 (Monday): What we see in the day

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W04-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** What we see in the day

### Learning objectives
- Children will name things we see in the day.

### Success criteria
- Names the sun and one other daytime thing
- Says day

**Previous knowledge:** Children can say light and dark and have made shadows.

**Key vocabulary:** day, night, sun, moon, stars, sleep

**Instructional materials:** A yellow sheet; Pictures of the sun, a bird, a school and children playing; A basket

### Starter
Lay a yellow sheet and say "This is the day. The sun shines. We play and learn."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture and say what we do or see in the day. Children: Children look and say the names.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "In the day we see the sun and the birds." Children: Children point to the sun and the bird.

Step 3. Teacher: Act out waking up, going to school and playing. Children: Children act out each part of the day.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "What do we see in the day?" Children: Child points or says a word.

### Practical activity: Day pictures
1. Lay pictures on a yellow sheet.
2. Say what we see and do in the day.
3. Act out a daytime action.

**Safety:** Keep the floor clear for acting and never look at the sun. Supervise movement.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Point to the picture and say the word together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to name three things they do in the day.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What shines in the day?
  **Expected response:** The sun.
- **Ask:** What do we do in the day?
  **Expected response:** Play and learn.

### Expected outcomes
- Names things in the day
- Says the word day

### Conclusion
Wave to the sun and say "Hello, day."

### Home connection
Talk about what you do during the day with your child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "name things we see in the day" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): What we see at night

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W04-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** What we see at night

### Learning objectives
- Children will name things we see at night.

### Success criteria
- Names the moon and stars
- Says night

**Previous knowledge:** Children can say light and dark and have made shadows.

**Key vocabulary:** day, night, sun, moon, stars, sleep

**Instructional materials:** A dark blue sheet; Pictures of the moon, stars, a bed and a sleeping child; Paper stars

### Starter
Lay a dark blue sheet and say "This is the night. We see the moon and stars."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture and say what we see at night. Children: Children look and say the names.

Step 2. Teacher: Put paper stars on the dark sheet and count them. Children: Children place stars and count.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "At night it is dark and quiet." Children: Children say shh and whisper.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "What do we see at night?" Children: Child points or says a word.

### Practical activity: Night sky
1. Lay a dark blue sheet.
2. Put stars and a moon on it.
3. Whisper goodnight.

**Safety:** Do not make the room too dark and never frighten children about the night. Stay close to every child.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Place the stars together and say the word.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what the moon looks like.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we see at night?
  **Expected response:** The moon and stars.
- **Ask:** Is it light or dark at night?
  **Expected response:** Dark.

### Expected outcomes
- Names things at night
- Says the word night

### Conclusion
Whisper "Goodnight" and sit quietly.

### Home connection
Look at the moon and stars together at night.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Day or night?

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W04-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** What we see in the day

### Learning objectives
- Children will sort pictures into day and night.

### Success criteria
- Puts a picture on the correct sheet
- Says day or night

**Previous knowledge:** Children can say light and dark and have made shadows.

**Key vocabulary:** day, night, sun, moon, stars, sleep

**Instructional materials:** A yellow sheet and a dark blue sheet; Eight pictures; A basket

### Starter
Show the two sheets and say "One is for day. One is for night."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Pick up a picture of the sun and put it on the day sheet. Children: Children watch and say day.

Step 2. Teacher: Pick up a picture of the moon and put it on the night sheet. Children: Children watch and say night.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child pick a picture and choose a sheet. Children: Child sorts the picture.

Step 4. Teacher: Check each sheet together. Children: Children say day or night.

### Practical activity: Day and night sort
1. Put two sheets on the floor.
2. Each child picks a picture.
3. Put it on the day or night sheet.

**Safety:** Use large pictures and keep the floor clear. Supervise turns.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two pictures and say the time first.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to add one more picture to each sheet.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Does the sun come out in the day?
  **Expected response:** Yes.
- **Ask:** Do stars come out at night?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts pictures into day and night
- Says day or night

### Conclusion
Put the sheets away and say "Day and night."

### Home connection
Talk about day and night activities at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "sort pictures into day and night" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Sleep and rest

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W04-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Sleep and rest

### Learning objectives
- Children will say that we sleep at night and that sleep helps us grow.

### Success criteria
- Says we sleep at night
- Acts out getting ready for bed

**Previous knowledge:** Children can say light and dark and have made shadows.

**Key vocabulary:** day, night, sun, moon, stars, sleep

**Instructional materials:** A soft toy and a blanket; Pictures of washing, pyjamas, a story and sleeping; A quiet corner

### Starter
Hold a soft toy and say "Teddy is tired. It is night. It is time to sleep."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show pictures of the bedtime steps and say them in order. Children: Children look and say the steps.

Step 2. Teacher: Act out washing, putting on pyjamas and a story. Children: Children act each step.

Step 3. Teacher: Put Teddy to bed and say "Sleep helps us grow." Children: Children whisper and tuck Teddy in.

Step 4. Teacher: Rest quietly for one minute. Children: Children lie down quietly.

### Practical activity: Bedtime for Teddy
1. Make a bed for Teddy with a blanket.
2. Act out each bedtime step.
3. Rest quietly together.

**Safety:** Keep the area calm and ensure each child can see an adult at all times. Do not make children lie down if they are anxious.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Act out the steps together beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell their own bedtime steps.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** When do we sleep?
  **Expected response:** At night.
- **Ask:** What helps us grow?
  **Expected response:** Sleep and food.

### Expected outcomes
- Says that we sleep at night
- Acts out bedtime steps

### Conclusion
Whisper "Goodnight, Teddy" and sit up.

### Home connection
Talk about your child’s bedtime routine and say goodnight.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 4 day and night check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W04-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** What we see at night

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child sorts day and night and talks about sleep.

### Success criteria
- Sorts pictures into day and night
- Says we sleep at night

**Previous knowledge:** Children can say light and dark and have made shadows.

**Key vocabulary:** day, night, sun, moon, stars, sleep

**Instructional materials:** Day and night pictures; Soft toy; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the day and night song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: day, night and sleep. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "Is it day or night?" and "What do we do?" Children: Child answers with a word or action.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Day and night stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use soft toys and large pictures.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use two pictures.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to tell a friend about day and night.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: sorts pictures into day and night; names things seen at night; says we sleep at night.

- **Ask:** Is the sun out at night?
  **Expected response:** No.
- **Ask:** What do we do at night?
  **Expected response:** Sleep.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 4
- Sorts day and night

### Conclusion
Whisper goodnight and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Talk about day and night at home.

- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child sorts day and night and talks about sleep" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
