# Toddler Science — Term 3, Week 8: Health

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W08 · **Week type:** consolidation · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Washing hands; Brushing teeth; Healthy habits

## Learning objectives
- Children will wash hands and say when we wash them.
- Children will practise brushing teeth with a model.

**Key vocabulary:** wash, soap, clean, teeth, germs, towel

**Content:** Washing hands, brushing teeth with a model, and healthy habits.

**Teaching activities:** Use real soap and water for hand washing; use a model mouth and a big toothbrush for teeth; sing a short washing song; model each step; make hand washing part of the daily routine.

**Learner activities:** Wash hands with soap, practise brushing teeth with a model and name healthy habits.

**Resources:** A basin, soap and towels; A teeth model and a large toothbrush; Pictures of healthy habits; A mirror; Glitter or flour to show germs

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child washes hands with soap with support and says one healthy habit.

## Expected outcomes
- Washes hands with soap with support.
- Says one healthy habit.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Social Habits: hygiene routines; Literacy: washing song; Physical Development: self-care

## Day 1 (Monday): Washing hands

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W08-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Washing hands

### Learning objectives
- Children will wash their hands with soap and say when we wash them.

### Success criteria
- Washes hands with soap
- Says one time we wash hands

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored the senses, food and caring and have practised looking after living things.

**Key vocabulary:** wash, soap, clean, teeth, germs, towel

**Instructional materials:** A basin of water; Soap and towels; A picture of times to wash hands

### Starter
Show your dirty hands and say "My hands are dirty. We need to wash them."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to wet hands, rub with soap and rinse. Children: Children watch and copy the actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Sing a washing song while rubbing: "Rub, rub, rub your hands." Children: Children sing and rub.

Step 3. Teacher: Dry hands on a clean towel. Children: Child dries hands.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "When do we wash hands?" Children: Child says after toilet or before eating.

### Practical activity: Hand washing
1. Set up a basin, soap and towels.
2. Wash hands step by step.
3. Dry hands.

**Safety:** Use mild soap and keep water shallow. Wipe spills quickly and supervise closely. Use a clean towel for each child.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Wash hands together with a hand over the child’s hand.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to teach a friend how to wash hands.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Why do we wash hands?
  **Expected response:** To get clean and stay healthy.
- **Ask:** When do we wash hands?
  **Expected response:** Before eating and after the toilet.

### Expected outcomes
- Washes hands with soap with support
- Says when to wash hands

### Conclusion
Show clean hands and say "Clean hands."

### Home connection
Wash hands together at home before meals and after the toilet.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Brushing teeth

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W08-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Brushing teeth

### Learning objectives
- Children will practise brushing teeth with a model.

### Success criteria
- Brushes the model teeth
- Says that we brush teeth

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored the senses, food and caring and have practised looking after living things.

**Key vocabulary:** wash, soap, clean, teeth, germs, towel

**Instructional materials:** A large teeth model; A big toothbrush; A mirror; Pictures of morning and night

### Starter
Show the teeth model and say "These are teeth. We brush them to keep them clean."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to brush the model teeth up and down and round. Children: Children watch and copy in the air.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child brush the model teeth. Children: Child brushes the model.

Step 3. Teacher: Look at your own teeth in the mirror. Children: Child looks and says teeth.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "We brush in the morning and at night." Children: Children say morning and night.

### Practical activity: Brush the teeth
1. Look at the model teeth.
2. Brush with a big brush.
3. Look at your own teeth in a mirror.

**Safety:** Use only the model for brushing in class unless parents have agreed to supervised brushing with personal brushes. Never share toothbrushes.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Brush the model together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say how long we should brush.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we brush?
  **Expected response:** Our teeth.
- **Ask:** When do we brush?
  **Expected response:** Morning and night.

### Expected outcomes
- Practises brushing teeth
- Says that we brush teeth

### Conclusion
Smile and show your clean teeth.

### Home connection
Brush teeth together morning and night at home.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Healthy habits

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W08-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Healthy habits

### Learning objectives
- Children will name healthy habits.

### Success criteria
- Names two healthy habits
- Acts out a healthy habit

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored the senses, food and caring and have practised looking after living things.

**Key vocabulary:** wash, soap, clean, teeth, germs, towel

**Instructional materials:** Pictures: washing, brushing, sleeping, eating well, playing outside, drinking water; A mat

### Starter
Show a picture of washing hands and say "This is a healthy habit. It keeps us well."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture and say the healthy habit. Children: Children look and say the habit.

Step 2. Teacher: Act out each habit. Children: Children copy the actions.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "How do we stay healthy?" Children: Child points to a picture.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a picture and act it. Children: Child acts and friends guess.

### Practical activity: Healthy habits
1. Put pictures on the floor.
2. Act out each habit.
3. Say the name.

**Safety:** Keep the floor clear and supervise movement. Be sensitive to families’ different circumstances.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Act together and say the habit.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say which habit they do every day.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How do we stay healthy?
  **Expected response:** Wash, sleep, eat well and play.
- **Ask:** What do we drink?
  **Expected response:** Water.

### Expected outcomes
- Names healthy habits
- Acts out healthy habits

### Conclusion
Say "I am healthy" together and wave.

### Home connection
Talk about healthy habits at home each day.

## Day 4 (Thursday): Germs and clean hands

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W08-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Washing hands

### Learning objectives
- Children will see why washing hands with soap matters.

### Success criteria
- Sees that soap removes dirt
- Says clean or dirty

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored the senses, food and caring and have practised looking after living things.

**Key vocabulary:** wash, soap, clean, teeth, germs, towel

**Instructional materials:** Flour or a little washable paint on hands to show dirt; A basin, soap and water; A mirror

### Starter
Show hands with a little flour and say "These hands are dirty. How can we clean them?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Wash one hand with water only and one with soap. Children: Children watch what happens.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "Soap helps to take away dirt and germs." Children: Children say soap.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child wash their own floury hands. Children: Child washes and sees clean hands.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Clean hands help us stay healthy." Children: Children say the sentence.

### Practical activity: Clean hands test
1. Put a little flour on hands.
2. Wash with water and then with soap.
3. Compare hands.

**Safety:** Use only a little washable flour or paint and mild soap. Keep water shallow and wipe spills.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Wash together and name each step.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say why soap is better than water alone.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What takes away the dirt?
  **Expected response:** Soap and water.
- **Ask:** Are your hands clean?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Sees that soap cleans hands
- Says clean and dirty

### Conclusion
Show your clean hands and cheer.

### Home connection
Make hand washing a family routine.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 8 health check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T3-W08-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Healthy habits

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child practises hygiene.

### Success criteria
- Washes hands with soap with support
- Says one healthy habit

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored the senses, food and caring and have practised looking after living things.

**Key vocabulary:** wash, soap, clean, teeth, germs, towel

**Instructional materials:** Basin, soap and towels; Teeth model; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the washing song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: hand washing, teeth and healthy habits. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What do we do?" and "Why?" Children: Child answers with a word or action.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Health stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and wipe spills at once. Use mild soap and clean towels.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to explain hand washing to a friend.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: washes hands with soap; practises brushing; names healthy habits.

- **Ask:** When do we wash hands?
  **Expected response:** Before eating and after the toilet.
- **Ask:** What do we brush?
  **Expected response:** Our teeth.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 8
- Practises hygiene routines

### Conclusion
Show clean hands and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Practise hygiene routines every day at home.
