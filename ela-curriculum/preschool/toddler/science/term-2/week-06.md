# Toddler Science — Term 2, Week 6: Plants

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W06 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Looking at seeds; Planting a bean seed; Watering and waiting

## Learning objectives
- Children will plant a seed and water it.
- Children will notice and talk about changes as the seed grows.

**Key vocabulary:** seed, soil, water, grow, plant, sun

**Content:** Looking at seeds, planting a bean seed, and watering and waiting while it grows.

**Teaching activities:** Use large bean seeds that are easy to handle; plant in clear cups so children can see roots; keep a growth chart with pictures; talk about waiting; never let children eat seeds.

**Learner activities:** Plant a seed, water it and notice and talk about changes as it grows.

**Resources:** Large bean seeds; Clear plastic cups; Soil; A small watering can; Cotton wool; A growth chart; Pictures of seed, root and shoot

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child plants a seed with support and says grow or points to change.

## Expected outcomes
- Plants a seed with support.
- Says "grow" or points to change.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Numeracy: counting days; Literacy: story of a seed; Social Habits: patience and care

## Day 1 (Monday): Looking at seeds

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W06-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Looking at seeds

### Learning objectives
- Children will look at seeds and say that plants grow from seeds.

### Success criteria
- Looks at a seed closely
- Says seed with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name plants and plant parts and know that plants are alive and grow.

**Key vocabulary:** seed, soil, water, grow, plant, sun

**Instructional materials:** Large bean seeds; A magnifying glass; A picture of a bean plant; A tray

### Starter
Hold up a large bean seed and say "This is a seed. A plant is asleep inside."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show different seeds and let children touch them gently. Children: Children touch the seeds and say seed.

Step 2. Teacher: Look at a seed with a magnifying glass. Children: Children look closely and point.

Step 3. Teacher: Show a picture of a bean plant growing from a seed. Children: Children look and say plant.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "Seeds need water and soil to grow." Children: Children say water and soil.

### Practical activity: Seed tray
1. Put large seeds on a tray.
2. Look at them with a magnifying glass.
3. Sort them by size or colour.

**Safety:** Use only large seeds that cannot be swallowed and supervise closely. Remind children not to put seeds in their mouths or noses.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold a seed together and say the word.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find two seeds that look different.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is this?
  **Expected response:** A seed.
- **Ask:** What grows from a seed?
  **Expected response:** A plant.

### Expected outcomes
- Looks at seeds and says seed
- Says that a plant grows from a seed

### Conclusion
Put the seeds in a box and say "Tomorrow we plant them."

### Home connection
Look at seeds in fruit such as an orange or a pawpaw with your child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "look at seeds and say that plants grow from seeds" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**

- PLANT

**Picture suggestions:**

**Song or rhyme:** A little seed, a little seed, grows into a plant.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Planting a seed

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W06-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Planting a bean seed

### Learning objectives
- Children will plant a bean seed in a cup of soil.

### Success criteria
- Puts soil in a cup
- Plants a seed and covers it

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name plants and plant parts and know that plants are alive and grow.

**Key vocabulary:** seed, soil, water, grow, plant, sun

**Instructional materials:** Clear plastic cups; Soil; Bean seeds; A small watering can; A labelling pen

### Starter
Say "Today we plant a seed. First soil, then the seed, then more soil." Show the three steps.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to fill a cup with soil with a spoon. Children: Children fill their cups with soil.

Step 2. Teacher: Show how to push a seed into the soil with a finger. Children: Child pushes a seed in.

Step 3. Teacher: Cover the seed with soil and write the child’s name on the cup. Children: Child pats the soil and sees their name.

Step 4. Teacher: Water the soil gently with a watering can. Children: Child waters their cup.

### Practical activity: Plant a seed
1. Put soil in a clear cup.
2. Plant a bean seed and cover it.
3. Water gently and put the cup in the sun.

**Safety:** Use clean soil and wash hands after planting. Keep seeds out of mouths. Wipe spilled water.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Plant the seed together with a hand over the child’s hand.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to plant two seeds and say which one will grow first.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we put in the cup first?
  **Expected response:** Soil.
- **Ask:** What does the seed need?
  **Expected response:** Water and sun.

### Expected outcomes
- Plants a seed in soil
- Waters the seed gently

### Conclusion
Put the cups in a sunny place and say "Grow, seed, grow."

### Home connection
Plant a seed at home with your child in a small pot.

- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Watering and waiting

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W06-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Watering and waiting

### Learning objectives
- Children will water their seeds and notice changes.

### Success criteria
- Waters the seed gently
- Says if anything has changed

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name plants and plant parts and know that plants are alive and grow.

**Key vocabulary:** seed, soil, water, grow, plant, sun

**Instructional materials:** The planted cups; A small watering can; A growth chart; Pictures of a seed, root and shoot

### Starter
Look at the cups together and say "Our seeds are asleep. Let us give them a drink."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Water each cup gently and say "Drink, seed." Children: Children water their cups.

Step 2. Teacher: Look at the soil and ask "Has anything changed?" Children: Child looks and says yes or no.

Step 3. Teacher: Show the growth chart and talk about waiting. Children: Children listen and look.

Step 4. Teacher: Draw or stick a picture on the chart for today. Children: Child adds a picture to the chart.

### Practical activity: Water the seeds
1. Give each child their cup.
2. Water gently.
3. Look closely and record on the chart.

**Safety:** Do not overwater; one small splash is enough. Keep the floor dry and wipe spills.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Water together and point to the soil.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to guess when the seed will grow.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What does the seed need?
  **Expected response:** Water.
- **Ask:** Has the seed grown?
  **Expected response:** Not yet or yes, with a shoot.

### Expected outcomes
- Waters a seed gently
- Looks for changes

### Conclusion
Put the cups back and say "Wait for the seed."

### Home connection
Water a plant at home with your child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "water their seeds and notice changes" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- WATER
- WAIT

**Picture suggestions:**
- A child watering a cup
- A growth chart

## Day 4 (Thursday): Seeds in cotton wool

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W06-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Watering and waiting

### Learning objectives
- Children will plant a seed in cotton wool and watch the root and shoot appear.

### Success criteria
- Plants a seed in damp cotton wool
- Looks at the roots through the cup

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name plants and plant parts and know that plants are alive and grow.

**Key vocabulary:** seed, soil, water, grow, plant, sun

**Instructional materials:** A clear cup; Damp cotton wool; Bean seeds; A tray; A picture of a root and a shoot

### Starter
Show a clear cup and say "We can see the seed grow if we plant it in cotton wool."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Put damp cotton wool in the cup and push a seed against the glass. Children: Child pushes the seed against the cup.

Step 2. Teacher: Put the cup on a sunny windowsill. Children: Child places the cup in a sunny spot.

Step 3. Teacher: Look each day for a root going down and a shoot going up. Children: Child looks and points to the root.

Step 4. Teacher: Show the picture of a root and a shoot. Children: Child matches the picture to the seed.

### Practical activity: See it grow
1. Put damp cotton wool in a clear cup.
2. Push a seed against the glass.
3. Look each day for roots and shoots.

**Safety:** Keep the cups on a tray to catch spills and wash hands after touching. Do not let children eat cotton wool or seeds.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the cup together and look closely.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to draw the seed each day.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which way does the root grow?
  **Expected response:** Down.
- **Ask:** Which way does the shoot grow?
  **Expected response:** Up.

### Expected outcomes
- Plants a seed in cotton wool
- Notices root and shoot with support

### Conclusion
Put the cups on the windowsill and say "We will watch each day."

### Home connection
Grow a bean seed in damp cotton wool at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "plant a seed in cotton wool and watch the root and shoot appear" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**

- SHOOT

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 6 seeds check

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W06-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Planting a bean seed

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child plants and cares for a seed.

### Success criteria
- Plants a seed with support
- Says grow or points to change

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name plants and plant parts and know that plants are alive and grow.

**Key vocabulary:** seed, soil, water, grow, plant, sun

**Instructional materials:** The planted cups; A growth chart; Class record sheet

### Starter
Look at the cups and sing the seed song.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: looking at seeds, watering and the growth chart. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What does the seed need?" and "What has changed?" Children: Child answers with a word or gesture.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level and take photographs. Children: Children wash hands and tidy.

### Practical activity: Seed stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors dry. Use large seeds only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one station.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to explain how to plant a seed.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: plants a seed; waters gently; notices changes; says what a seed needs.

- **Ask:** What does a seed need?
  **Expected response:** Water, soil and sun.
- **Ask:** What grows from a seed?
  **Expected response:** A plant.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 6
- Plants and waters a seed

### Conclusion
Wash hands and wave goodbye to the seeds.

### Home connection
Keep watching plants grow at home.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
