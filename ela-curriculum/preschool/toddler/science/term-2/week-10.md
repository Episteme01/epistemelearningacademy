# Toddler Science — Term 2, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising animals and homes; Revising plants and seeds; Revising caring and food

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record.

**Key vocabulary:** animal, plant, seed, home, baby, care

**Content:** Revising animals and homes, plants and seeds, and caring and food through play stations and gathering Term 2 evidence.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and playful; observe quietly and record without interrupting; offer help only when a child is stuck; celebrate what each child can do.

**Learner activities:** Show what they know about animals, plants, seeds, food and caring through play.

**Resources:** Animal and home cards; Baby animal pairs; Plants and seeds; Food pictures; Class record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s Term 2 science learning using the four-level scale.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar tasks with growing independence.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 2.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and describing words; Numeracy: matching and sorting; Social Habits: kindness

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising animals and homes

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising animals and homes

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit animals, their homes and their babies through games.

### Success criteria
- Names animals
- Matches animals to homes and babies

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about animals, their homes and babies, plants and seeds, food and caring this term.

**Key vocabulary:** animal, plant, seed, home, baby, care

**Instructional materials:** Animal cards; Home cards; Baby and mother pairs

### Starter
Sing the animal song and say "Today we play our favourite animal games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the home matching game. Children: Children match animals to homes.

Step 2. Teacher: Play find your mother with baby cards. Children: Children match babies to mothers.

Step 3. Teacher: Move like animals to the drum. Children: Children move like animals.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Animal games
1. Set up three game spaces.
2. Children play each for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes who names animals and homes.

**Safety:** Keep the floor clear and supervise movement. Use soft toys and large cards.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play with the child and use three animals.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Where does the hen live?
  **Expected response:** In a nest.
- **Ask:** What is the baby of a cow?
  **Expected response:** A calf.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows knowledge of animals
- Matches animals to homes and babies

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye to the animals.

### Home connection
Play animal games at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit animals, their homes and their babies through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Revising plants and seeds

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising plants and seeds

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit plants, seeds, leaves, flowers and fruits.

### Success criteria
- Names plants and plant parts
- Says what a seed needs

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about animals, their homes and babies, plants and seeds, food and caring this term.

**Key vocabulary:** animal, plant, seed, home, baby, care

**Instructional materials:** Planted seeds; Leaves, flowers and fruits; Plant part cards

### Starter
Look at the seeds we planted and say "Let us see what has grown."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Look at the growing seeds and talk about changes. Children: Children look and say grow.

Step 2. Teacher: Sort leaves into big and small. Children: Child sorts the leaves.

Step 3. Teacher: Match fruits to trees. Children: Child matches fruit to tree.

Step 4. Teacher: Water the plants together. Children: Child waters a plant.

### Practical activity: Plants and seeds
1. Set up three plant stations.
2. Children rotate every five minutes.
3. The teacher notes names and care.

**Safety:** Wash hands after touching plants and soil. Keep floors dry.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Look together and name each plant.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to explain how a seed grows.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What does a seed need?
  **Expected response:** Water, soil and sun.
- **Ask:** What grows from a seed?
  **Expected response:** A plant.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows knowledge of plants
- Says what a seed needs

### Conclusion
Wash hands and say thank you.

### Home connection
Keep watching plants grow at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit plants, seeds, leaves, flowers and fruits" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Revising caring and food

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising caring and food

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit caring for living things and foods from plants and animals.

### Success criteria
- Sorts foods into plant and animal
- Shows gentle caring

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about animals, their homes and babies, plants and seeds, food and caring this term.

**Key vocabulary:** animal, plant, seed, home, baby, care

**Instructional materials:** Food pictures; Soft toys; Plants and a watering can

### Starter
Say "Today we care for living things and talk about food."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sort food pictures into plant and animal foods. Children: Child sorts the foods.

Step 2. Teacher: Water a plant and care for a toy pet. Children: Child waters and cares gently.

Step 3. Teacher: Choose foods for a healthy plate. Children: Child chooses healthy foods.

Step 4. Teacher: Say how we are kind to animals. Children: Child says kind or gentle.

### Practical activity: Care and food
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate every five minutes.
3. The teacher notes sorting and gentle care.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors dry. Use soft toys and pictures.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two choices at each station.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell a friend how to look after a plant.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What helps us grow strong?
  **Expected response:** Healthy food.
- **Ask:** How do we touch animals?
  **Expected response:** Gently.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows caring behaviour
- Sorts foods by where they come from

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "We care."

### Home connection
Care for plants and pets at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 4 (Thursday): Living things play stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising animals and homes

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 2 science learning at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with growing independence
- Shows animal, plant, food and caring knowledge

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about animals, their homes and babies, plants and seeds, food and caring this term.

**Key vocabulary:** animal, plant, seed, home, baby, care

**Instructional materials:** Six station sets; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Sing a favourite song and show six stations to the class.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: animals, homes, babies, seeds, food and caring. Children: Children rotate every three minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they can do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Six stations
1. Set up six stations with familiar materials.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep floors dry and clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names animals; matches animals to homes; names plants; waters a plant; sorts foods; handles animals gently.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a station.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 2 observation record
- Shows familiar skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite game from this term at home.

## Day 5 (Friday): Term 2 science celebration

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T2-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising caring and food

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate Term 2 with songs, plants and games, and teachers will complete their records.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows what they enjoy

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt about animals, their homes and babies, plants and seeds, food and caring this term.

**Key vocabulary:** animal, plant, seed, home, baby, care

**Instructional materials:** Favourite songs; The grown seeds; Stickers or a certificate; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate. Let us look at our plants and sing our favourite songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite songs with actions. Children: Children join in and choose a song.

Step 2. Teacher: Look at the plants we grew and talk about how they changed. Children: Children look and say grow.

Step 3. Teacher: Give each child a sticker or praise and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing and look at the plants.
3. Hand out stickers or certificates.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and sing along.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s Term 2 science record using today’s observations and the week’s notes. Record a next step for Term 3.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite plant or animal?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Did you like our plants?
  **Expected response:** Yes with a smile or word.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed Term 2 record
- Enjoys science and nature

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you in Term 3."

### Home connection
Keep exploring plants and animals during the holiday.
