# Toddler Science — annual scope and sequence

Three terms of ten teaching weeks. Five planned lessons a week (150 in the year). Dates are configured by ELA's academic calendar, not written here.

## Term 1: Term 1 focuses on me and my world: the body, the senses, water, sand and soil, and weather

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | My body | Naming body parts; What my body can do; Songs about my body | Children will point to and name main parts of the body. Children will show what different body parts can do. | head, hands, feet, eyes, ears, mouth | Points to at least six body parts when named. Says what a body part can do with support. |
| 2 | My senses | Looking with my eyes; Listening with my ears; Spot and guess games | Children will use their eyes to find, match and name objects. Children will use their ears to find and name sounds. | see, look, hear, listen, eyes, ears | Finds a hidden object by looking. Names a sound heard in the room. |
| 3 | My senses | Touching soft things; Touching hard things; Rough and smooth | Children will touch and describe soft and hard objects. Children will describe rough and smooth with support. | soft, hard, rough, smooth, touch, feel | Says soft or hard for a familiar object. Sorts objects into soft and hard with support. |
| 4 | My senses | Smelling safe things; Tasting safe foods; Sweet, sour and salty | Children will smell safe items and say if they like the smell. Children will taste safe foods and describe them with a word. | smell, taste, nose, tongue, sweet, salty | Names a smell with support. Says one word for how a food tastes. |
| 5 | My senses | Mystery bag; Sense stations; Which sense do I use? | Children will use different senses at stations to explore objects. Children will link a sense to a body part. | see, hear, touch, smell, taste, senses | Names at least three senses with support. Points to the body part for each sense. |
| 6 | Water | Pouring and splashing; Wet and dry; Water in my day | Children will pour, splash and play with water safely. Children will say whether things are wet or dry. | water, wet, dry, pour, splash, drink | Pours water from one container to another. Says wet or dry for objects. |
| 7 | Water | Things that float; Things that sink; Floating boats | Children will put objects in water and see which float or sink. Children will say float or sink with support. | float, sink, boat, heavy, light, water | Says float or sink after seeing an object in water. Puts a floating object on the water. |
| 8 | Earth materials | Playing with sand; Digging in soil; Wet sand and dry sand | Children will play with sand and soil using hands and tools. Children will notice that wet sand and dry sand feel different. | sand, soil, dig, wet, dry, scoop | Digs or scoops sand or soil with a tool. Says wet or dry for sand. |
| 9 | Weather | Sunny and cloudy; Rain and wind; Dressing for the weather | Children will notice and name the weather each day. Children will choose clothes for different weather with support. | sun, rain, wind, cloud, hot, cold | Names the weather today with a word or gesture. Chooses suitable clothes for rain or sun with support. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising my body and senses; Revising water, sand and soil; Revising weather | Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | body, senses, water, sand, weather, float | Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. |

## Term 2: Term 2 focuses on living things: animals, their homes and babies, insects, plants, seeds, food and caring for living things

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Animals | Pets and farm animals; Wild animals in pictures; Animal movements | Children will name familiar animals and say where they see them. Children will move like different animals. | dog, cat, goat, hen, cow, lion | Names at least six animals. Moves like an animal on request. |
| 2 | Animals | Homes on the farm; Homes in the wild; Matching animals to homes | Children will match animals to their homes. Children will say simple words for animal homes. | nest, pen, kennel, hole, water, tree | Matches at least three animals to their homes. Names one animal home. |
| 3 | Animals | Mothers and babies; Eggs and chicks; Caring for babies | Children will match animal babies to their mothers. Children will say that mothers care for their babies. | baby, chick, kid, calf, egg, mother | Matches at least three animal babies to their mothers. Says "baby" and the animal name. |
| 4 | Animals | Ants and beetles; Butterflies and bees; Looking carefully and not touching | Children will look closely at small creatures with a magnifying glass. Children will say that small creatures are looked at and not hurt. | ant, bee, butterfly, beetle, look, tiny | Names two small creatures. Looks at a small creature gently without touching. |
| 5 | Plants | Trees and flowers; Leaves and grass; The school garden | Children will name trees, flowers and leaves and say that plants are alive. Children will explore the school garden with their senses. | tree, flower, leaf, grass, plant, green | Names a tree, a flower and a leaf. Says that plants grow. |
| 6 | Plants | Looking at seeds; Planting a bean seed; Watering and waiting | Children will plant a seed and water it. Children will notice and talk about changes as the seed grows. | seed, soil, water, grow, plant, sun | Plants a seed with support. Says "grow" or points to change. |
| 7 | Plants | Leaves of different shapes; Flowers and their colours; Fruits we eat | Children will sort leaves by size and shape with support. Children will name fruits and say they come from plants. | leaf, flower, petal, fruit, mango, big | Sorts leaves into big and small. Names two fruits that grow on trees. |
| 8 | Food | Foods from plants; Foods from animals; Healthy foods | Children will say which foods come from plants and which from animals. Children will name foods that help them grow. | yam, rice, egg, milk, fish, healthy | Names two plant foods and two animal foods. Says that healthy food helps us grow. |
| 9 | Caring | Watering plants; Feeding and caring for pets; Being kind to animals | Children will water plants and say that plants need water. Children will show kind and gentle handling of animals. | water, feed, kind, gentle, care, pet | Waters a plant with support. Handles a toy animal gently. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising animals and homes; Revising plants and seeds; Revising caring and food | Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record. | animal, plant, seed, home, baby, care | Completes familiar tasks with growing independence. Each child has an observation record for Term 2. |

## Term 3: Term 3 focuses on how things work: push and pull, rolling and sliding, light and shadow, wet and dry, natural and made things, colours in nature, health and caring for the environment

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Movement | Pushing a toy; Pulling a toy; Push and pull games | Children will push and pull toys and say push or pull. Children will notice that pushing moves things away and pulling brings them closer. | push, pull, toy, cart, move, stop | Pushes and pulls a toy on request. Says push or pull with support. |
| 2 | Movement | Things that roll; Things that slide; Ramps and slopes | Children will test objects to see which roll and which slide. Children will roll a ball or car down a ramp. | roll, slide, ball, ramp, fast, slow | Says roll or slide after trying. Rolls a car down a ramp. |
| 3 | Light | Light and dark; Making shadows; Shadow puppets | Children will notice that light comes from the sun and a torch. Children will make and watch shadows. | light, dark, shadow, sun, torch, shade | Says light or dark for a place. Makes a shadow with a hand or toy. |
| 4 | Light | What we see in the day; What we see at night; Sleep and rest | Children will say what happens in the day and what happens at night. Children will match pictures to day or night. | day, night, sun, moon, stars, sleep | Sorts pictures into day and night. Says that we sleep at night. |
| 5 | Materials | What gets wet; What stays dry; Drying in the sun | Children will test which materials get wet and which stay dry. Children will notice that wet things dry in the sun. | wet, dry, cloth, sun, umbrella, rain | Says wet or dry for materials after testing. Notices that a wet cloth dries in the sun. |
| 6 | Materials | Things from nature; Things people make; Sorting natural and made | Children will sort objects into natural and made with support. Children will name materials such as wood, cloth and plastic. | wood, cloth, plastic, stone, leaf, made | Sorts objects into natural and made with support. Names two materials. |
| 7 | Nature | Colour walk; Colours of flowers and leaves; Making a nature collage | Children will find colours in nature on a walk. Children will make a collage with natural materials. | red, green, yellow, brown, colour, nature | Finds three colours in nature. Makes a collage with leaves or flowers. |
| 8 | Health | Washing hands; Brushing teeth; Healthy habits | Children will wash hands and say when we wash them. Children will practise brushing teeth with a model. | wash, soap, clean, teeth, germs, towel | Washes hands with soap with support. Says one healthy habit. |
| 9 | Environment | Litter in the bin; Keeping our school clean; Saving water | Children will put litter in a bin and say why we keep places clean. Children will turn off a tap after use. | litter, bin, clean, tidy, tap, save | Puts litter in a bin. Turns off a tap with support. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising body, senses and materials; Revising living things; Celebration and end-of-year record | Children will show Year learning through games and a science fair. Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note. | senses, plants, animals, push, light, clean | Takes part confidently in familiar science games. Each child has an end-of-year observation record. |
