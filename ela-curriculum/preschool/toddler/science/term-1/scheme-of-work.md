# Toddler Science — Term 1 scheme of work

| Week | Topic and subtopics | Learning objectives | Content | Teaching activities | Learner activities | Resources | Assessment | Expected outcomes | Cross-curricular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **My body**: Naming body parts; What my body can do; Songs about my body | Children will point to and name main parts of the body. Children will show what different body parts can do. | Naming main body parts, finding out what the body can do, and singing body songs. | Touch each body part as you name it; use a mirror so children see their own body; sing and move together; use the same words every day. | Point to and name body parts and show what each part can do. | A large mirror; A doll or soft toy; A body picture; A scarf; A drum | Observe whether each child points to at least six body parts when named. | Points to at least six body parts when named. Says what a body part can do with support. | Literacy: action songs; Physical Development: body awareness; Social Habits: respecting our bodies |
| 2 | **My senses**: Looking with my eyes; Listening with my ears; Spot and guess games | Children will use their eyes to find, match and name objects. Children will use their ears to find and name sounds. | Using eyes to find, match and name objects, using ears to find and name sounds, and playing spot and guess games. | Ask children to look and listen slowly; give time; use real objects and everyday sounds; never test with loud sounds. | Use eyes to find hidden objects and ears to name sounds. | A tray of objects; A cloth; Pictures hidden around the room; A bell, a rattle and a spoon with a cup; A cloth bag | Observe whether each child finds a hidden object by looking and names a sound by listening. | Finds a hidden object by looking. Names a sound heard in the room. | Literacy: listening games; Numeracy: matching; Physical Development: moving carefully |
| 3 | **My senses**: Touching soft things; Touching hard things; Rough and smooth | Children will touch and describe soft and hard objects. Children will describe rough and smooth with support. | Touching soft and hard objects, and describing rough and smooth. | Let children touch with hands and say each word as they touch; use a mystery bag; never force a child to touch something they dislike; offer a tool instead. | Touch objects, say soft, hard, rough or smooth and sort objects. | Cotton wool, a sponge, a teddy; A stone, a wooden block, a spoon; Sandpaper, a rough mat, a smooth tile and a leaf; A cloth bag; Two baskets | Observe whether each child says soft or hard for an object and sorts objects with support. | Says soft or hard for a familiar object. Sorts objects into soft and hard with support. | Literacy: describing words; Numeracy: sorting; Creative Development: textures |
| 4 | **My senses**: Smelling safe things; Tasting safe foods; Sweet, sour and salty | Children will smell safe items and say if they like the smell. Children will taste safe foods and describe them with a word. | Smelling safe items, tasting safe foods, and noticing sweet, sour and salty. | Check allergies and parental consent before any tasting; wash hands first; offer tiny tastes only; never force children to smell or taste; use a spoon and individual cups. | Smell safe things and taste safe foods and say one word to describe them. | Orange, lemon, cloves or mint leaves; Sugar, salt and a slice of lemon in small cups; Spoons; Napkins; A cloth to cover items | Observe whether each child names a smell and says one word about how a food tastes. | Names a smell with support. Says one word for how a food tastes. | Literacy: describing words; Social Habits: trying new foods politely; Understanding the World: food |
| 5 | **My senses**: Mystery bag; Sense stations; Which sense do I use? | Children will use different senses at stations to explore objects. Children will link a sense to a body part. | Using different senses at stations, a mystery bag and linking each sense to a body part. | Run stations with simple rules; ask which sense the child used; point to the body part each time; use the same five picture cards daily. | Use different senses to explore objects and link each sense to a body part. | Five sense picture cards: eye, ear, hand, nose, tongue; A mystery bag; Objects for each station; A doll | Observe whether each child names at least three senses and points to the right body part. | Names at least three senses with support. Points to the body part for each sense. | Literacy: describing words; Numeracy: counting five senses; Physical Development: sensory play |
| 6 | **Water**: Pouring and splashing; Wet and dry; Water in my day | Children will pour, splash and play with water safely. Children will say whether things are wet or dry. | Pouring and splashing water, deciding whether things are wet or dry, and noticing where water is used in our day. | Keep water shallow and supervise every moment; use aprons and towels; name each action; let children pour from small jugs; wipe spills at once. | Pour, splash and play with water safely and say wet or dry. | A shallow basin; Small jugs, cups and funnels; Aprons and towels; A sponge; A cloth and a dry towel; Pictures of water uses | Observe whether each child pours water safely and says wet or dry for objects. | Pours water from one container to another. Says wet or dry for objects. | Numeracy: full and empty; Social Habits: cleaning up spills; Literacy: describing words |
| 7 | **Water**: Things that float; Things that sink; Floating boats | Children will put objects in water and see which float or sink. Children will say float or sink with support. | Putting objects in water to see which float and which sink, and making boats that float. | Let children predict by saying or pointing; put each object in the water together and say "float" or "sink"; use large objects only; keep a tray of towels ready. | Put objects in water, say float or sink and put a floating object on the water. | A clear basin of water; A cork, a leaf, a plastic cup, a wooden spoon; A stone, a metal spoon, a key, a coin-sized large washer; A paper boat; Towels | Observe whether each child says float or sink after seeing an object in water. | Says float or sink after seeing an object in water. Puts a floating object on the water. | Numeracy: heavy and light; Literacy: describing words; Creative Development: making boats |
| 8 | **Earth materials**: Playing with sand; Digging in soil; Wet sand and dry sand | Children will play with sand and soil using hands and tools. Children will notice that wet sand and dry sand feel different. | Playing with sand and soil, digging, and noticing that wet and dry sand feel different. | Let children dig and scoop freely; name each action; use small tools; keep sand away from eyes; model washing hands after play. | Dig and scoop sand and soil with tools and say wet or dry for sand. | A sand tray; A tray of soil; Spoons, scoops, small buckets and trowels; A watering can; Aprons; Towels | Observe whether each child digs or scoops with a tool and says wet or dry for sand. | Digs or scoops sand or soil with a tool. Says wet or dry for sand. | Numeracy: full and empty; Physical Development: scooping and digging; Social Habits: washing hands |
| 9 | **Weather**: Sunny and cloudy; Rain and wind; Dressing for the weather | Children will notice and name the weather each day. Children will choose clothes for different weather with support. | Noticing and naming weather, and choosing clothes for sun and rain. | Look at the sky every morning with the children; use a weather chart; name the weather each day; link to real experience of rain, harmattan and sun; never go outside in dangerous weather. | Name the weather and choose suitable clothes for rain or sun with support. | A weather chart with sun, rain, cloud and wind pictures; A raincoat, an umbrella, a sun hat and a jumper; A ribbon streamer; A bowl for rain | Observe whether each child names the weather today and chooses suitable clothes. | Names the weather today with a word or gesture. Chooses suitable clothes for rain or sun with support. | Literacy: weather songs; Numeracy: counting days; Social Habits: dressing appropriately |
| 10 | **Revision and assessment**: Revising my body and senses; Revising water, sand and soil; Revising weather | Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | Revising the body and senses, water, sand and soil, and weather through play stations and gathering Term 1 evidence. | Keep every activity familiar and playful; observe quietly and record without interrupting; offer help only when a child is stuck; celebrate what each child can do. | Show what they know about the body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather through play. | A mirror; Sense objects; A water basin; Sand and soil trays; A weather chart; Class record sheets | Observe and record each child’s Term 1 science learning using the four-level scale. | Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. | Literacy: songs and describing words; Numeracy: sorting; Social Habits: sharing equipment |
