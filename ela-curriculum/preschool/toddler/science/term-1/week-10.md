# Toddler Science — Term 1, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T1-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising my body and senses; Revising water, sand and soil; Revising weather

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record.

**Key vocabulary:** body, senses, water, sand, weather, float

**Content:** Revising the body and senses, water, sand and soil, and weather through play stations and gathering Term 1 evidence.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and playful; observe quietly and record without interrupting; offer help only when a child is stuck; celebrate what each child can do.

**Learner activities:** Show what they know about the body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather through play.

**Resources:** A mirror; Sense objects; A water basin; Sand and soil trays; A weather chart; Class record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s Term 1 science learning using the four-level scale.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 1.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and describing words; Numeracy: sorting; Social Habits: sharing equipment

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising my body and senses

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T1-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising my body and senses

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit body parts and the five senses through games.

### Success criteria
- Points to body parts
- Names senses

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored their body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather this term.

**Key vocabulary:** body, senses, water, sand, weather, float

**Instructional materials:** A mirror; Five sense cards; A mystery bag; A doll

### Starter
Sing the body song and say "Today we play our favourite body and senses games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing the body song and touch each part. Children: Children touch each part.

Step 2. Teacher: Play the mystery bag game with touch. Children: Child feels and guesses.

Step 3. Teacher: Show the five sense cards and ask "Which sense?" Children: Child points to the body part.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Body and senses games
1. Set up three game spaces.
2. Children play each for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes who names parts and senses.

**Safety:** Use safe objects only and keep floors clear. Supervise movement.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play with the child and use three body parts.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we see with?
  **Expected response:** Eyes.
- **Ask:** Where is your nose?
  **Expected response:** Child touches their nose.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows knowledge of body parts
- Names senses with support

### Conclusion
Sing the body song quietly and sit down.

### Home connection
Play body and senses games at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit body parts and the five senses through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Revising water, sand and soil

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T1-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising water, sand and soil

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit water, sand and soil through play.

### Success criteria
- Plays with water, sand and soil safely
- Says wet, dry, float or sink

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored their body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather this term.

**Key vocabulary:** body, senses, water, sand, weather, float

**Instructional materials:** A water basin; Float and sink objects; Trays of wet and dry sand; A tray of soil

### Starter
Say "Today we play with water, sand and soil." Show the three areas.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play float and sink with the water basin. Children: Children put objects in the water.

Step 2. Teacher: Dig in the sand and soil. Children: Children dig and scoop.

Step 3. Teacher: Compare wet and dry sand. Children: Child says wet or dry.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite play area. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Earth and water play
1. Set up three play areas.
2. Children rotate every five minutes.
3. The teacher notes words and actions.

**Safety:** Supervise each area closely. Keep water shallow and wash hands after play.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child and name what they do.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to explain float and sink to a friend.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Does it float or sink?
  **Expected response:** Float or sink.
- **Ask:** Is the sand wet or dry?
  **Expected response:** Wet or dry.

### Expected outcomes
- Plays safely with water, sand and soil
- Uses science words

### Conclusion
Wash hands and say thank you.

### Home connection
Play with water, sand and soil at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit water, sand and soil through play" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Revising weather

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T1-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising weather

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit weather words and dressing for the weather.

### Success criteria
- Names the weather
- Chooses clothes for weather

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored their body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather this term.

**Key vocabulary:** body, senses, water, sand, weather, float

**Instructional materials:** A weather chart; Clothes; A streamer; An umbrella

### Starter
Look out of the window together and say "What is the weather today?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Look at the sky and name the weather. Children: Children say the weather.

Step 2. Teacher: Put the picture on the weather chart. Children: Child places the picture.

Step 3. Teacher: Choose clothes for the weather and dress the doll. Children: Child chooses clothes.

Step 4. Teacher: Play with streamers or an umbrella. Children: Child plays and says wind or rain.

### Practical activity: Weather games
1. Set up the weather chart and clothes.
2. Children name the weather and choose clothes.
3. Play with streamers.

**Safety:** Stay in safe areas and watch for weather hazards. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer two pictures and say the weather.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell a friend what to wear.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is the weather?
  **Expected response:** Sunny, cloudy, rainy or windy.
- **Ask:** What do we wear?
  **Expected response:** A hat or coat.

### Expected outcomes
- Names the weather with a word
- Chooses suitable clothes

### Conclusion
Sing the weather song and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Talk about the weather each day.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit weather words and dressing for the weather" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Science play stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T1-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising my body and senses

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 1 science learning at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows body, senses, water and weather knowledge

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored their body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather this term.

**Key vocabulary:** body, senses, water, sand, weather, float

**Instructional materials:** Six station sets; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Sing the body song and show six stations to the class.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather. Children: Children rotate every three minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they can do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Six stations
1. Set up six stations with familiar materials.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep water shallow and floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names body parts; uses senses; plays with water; says wet or dry; names the weather.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a station.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 1 observation record
- Shows familiar skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite science game at home.

## Day 5 (Friday): Term 1 science celebration

**ID:** ELA-PS-TOD-SCI-T1-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 20 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising weather

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate Term 1 with songs, experiments and games, and teachers will complete their records.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows what they enjoy

**Previous knowledge:** Children have explored their body, senses, water, sand, soil and weather this term.

**Key vocabulary:** body, senses, water, sand, weather, float

**Instructional materials:** Favourite songs; Float and sink objects; Stickers or a certificate; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate. Let us sing and play our favourite science games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite songs with actions. Children: Children join in and choose a song.

Step 2. Teacher: Play float and sink as a class. Children: Child puts an object in the water and says float or sink.

Step 3. Teacher: Give each child a sticker or praise and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing, play float and sink and say each name.
3. Hand out stickers or certificates.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Keep water shallow and supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and sing along.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s Term 1 science record using today’s observations and the week’s notes. Record a next step for Term 2.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite game?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Did you like it?
  **Expected response:** Yes with a smile or word.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed Term 1 record
- Enjoys science play

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you in Term 2."

### Home connection
Keep exploring at home during the holiday and talk about what you notice.
