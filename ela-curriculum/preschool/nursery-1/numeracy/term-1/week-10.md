# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 1, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising counting and numerals; Revising sorting and measures; Revising shapes

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, sort, long, circle, ten

**Content:** Revising counting and numerals, sorting and measures, and shapes, with end-of-term records.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly during play; record what children can do and how far each child counts; share records with parents in plain language with a strength, a next step and a home idea.

**Learner activities:** Show learning from the term through play stations and a celebration.

**Resources:** Counters; Numeral cards; Sorting baskets; Ropes and cubes; Shapes; Stickers; Class record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s Term 1 numeracy learning.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 1.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and rhymes; Social Habits: celebration; Creative Development: shape pictures

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising counting and numerals

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising counting and numerals

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit counting, numerals and number games through play.

### Success criteria
- Counts objects accurately
- Names numerals 1 to 10

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt counting, numerals, sorting, size, length and shapes in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, sort, long, circle, ten

**Instructional materials:** Counters; Numeral cards; A hopscotch grid; Counting bags

### Starter
Sing the counting rhyme and say "Today we play our favourite number games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play counting bags and numeral hunt. Children: Child counts and names numerals.

Step 2. Teacher: Play number hopscotch. Children: Child hops and says numbers.

Step 3. Teacher: Show numbers with fingers. Children: Child shows numbers with fingers.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Number games
1. Set up three game areas.
2. Children play each for five minutes.
3. The teacher notes counting.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and use counters too large to swallow. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child with numbers up to five.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a counting game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many are there?
  **Expected response:** The number.
- **Ask:** What numeral is this?
  **Expected response:** A numeral from 1 to 10.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows counting and numeral knowledge
- Plays counting games confidently

### Conclusion
Count to ten and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep playing counting games at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit counting, numerals and number games through play" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Revising sorting and measures

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising sorting and measures

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit sorting, size and length through play.

### Success criteria
- Sorts by colour or size
- Compares length and size

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt counting, numerals, sorting, size, length and shapes in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, sort, long, circle, ten

**Instructional materials:** Sorting baskets; Cups; Ropes; Cubes

### Starter
Say "Today we sort, compare and measure."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the colour sorting game. Children: Child sorts by colour.

Step 2. Teacher: Order three cups by size. Children: Child orders the cups.

Step 3. Teacher: Measure an object with cubes. Children: Child measures and counts.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Sorting and measuring games
1. Set up three areas.
2. Children play each for five minutes.
3. The teacher notes responses.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and use large objects only. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child and use two objects.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to sort and then count each group.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How did you sort?
  **Expected response:** By colour or size.
- **Ask:** Which is longer?
  **Expected response:** The longer object.

### Expected outcomes
- Sorts and compares with confidence
- Measures with cubes

### Conclusion
Put the objects away and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep sorting and comparing at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Maths stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising counting and numerals

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 1 numeracy at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows counting, sorting, size and shape knowledge

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt counting, numerals, sorting, size, length and shapes in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, sort, long, circle, ten

**Instructional materials:** Six maths stations; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Say "Today we visit six maths stations and show what we know."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: counting, numerals, sorting, size, length and shapes. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Maths stations
1. Set up six stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep floors clear and use large safe materials only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: counts to 10; counts objects; names numerals 1 to 10; sorts; compares size and length; names shapes.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a game.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 1 observation record
- Shows familiar maths skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite maths game at home during the holiday.

## Day 4 (Thursday): Revising shapes

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising shapes

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit circle, square and triangle through play.

### Success criteria

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt counting, numerals, sorting, size, length and shapes in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, sort, long, circle, ten

**Instructional materials:** Shapes; A feely bag; Paper and glue; Straws and playdough

### Starter
Say "Today we play shape games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the feely bag game. Children: Child feels and names a shape.

Step 2. Teacher: Build a triangle with straws. Children: Child builds a triangle.

Step 3. Teacher: Make a shape picture. Children: Child sticks shapes on paper.

### Practical activity: Shape games
1. Set up three areas.
2. Children play each for five minutes.
3. The teacher notes shape names.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and use smooth shapes only. Wash hands after glue and playdough.

### Differentiation

### Assessment

- **Ask:** What shape is this?
  **Expected response:** Circle, square or triangle.
- **Ask:** Where do you see it?

- Makes a shape picture

Wash hands and wave goodbye.

## Day 5 (Friday): Term celebration and records

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising shapes

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate Term 1 with songs and games, and teachers will complete each child’s record.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows something they enjoyed

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt counting, numerals, sorting, size, length and shapes in Term 1.

**Key vocabulary:** count, numeral, sort, long, circle, ten

**Instructional materials:** Favourite number songs; Stickers; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate our term. Let us sing our favourite number songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite number rhymes with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Give each child a sticker and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

Step 3. Teacher: Complete each child’s record and next step. Children: Children play quietly.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing and clap.
3. Give out stickers.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Keep the floor clear and supervise all play.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and sing together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s Term 1 numeracy record from the term’s observations. Note the highest number counted accurately, a strength, a next step and a home idea for parents.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite number game?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** What can you count to?
  **Expected response:** The highest number.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed Term 1 record
- Enjoys the celebration

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you next term."

### Home connection
Share the Term 1 record and keep counting over the holiday.
