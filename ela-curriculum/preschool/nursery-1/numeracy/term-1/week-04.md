# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 1, Week 4: Numerals

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W04 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Looking at numerals 6 to 10; Tracing numerals in sand and air; Matching numerals and groups

## Learning objectives
- Children will recognise the numerals 6 to 10.
- Children will trace numerals in sand and in the air.

**Key vocabulary:** numeral, trace, sand, match, six, ten

**Content:** Looking at numerals 6 to 10, tracing numerals in sand and air, and matching numerals and groups.

**Teaching activities:** Link each numeral to a quantity; model the starting point and direction of each numeral; use sand, clay, large air movements and pipe cleaners before pencils; accept reversed numerals gently; keep sessions short.

**Learner activities:** Recognise the numerals 1 to 10 and trace them in sand and air.

**Resources:** Large numeral cards 1 to 10; A tray of sand or flour; Clay or playdough; Pipe cleaners; Counters

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child names numerals 1 to 10 and traces some of them.

## Expected outcomes
- Names numerals 1 to 10 with support.
- Traces three or more numerals.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: letter-like shapes and fine motor skills; Physical Development: finger strength; Creative Development: clay

## Day 1 (Monday): Numerals 6 to 10

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W04-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Looking at numerals 6 to 10

### Learning objectives
- Children will recognise and name the numerals 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10.

### Success criteria
- Names numerals 6 to 10
- Matches each to a group

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count objects to 10 and recognise numerals 1 to 5.

**Key vocabulary:** numeral, trace, sand, match, six, ten

**Instructional materials:** Large numeral cards 6 to 10; Counters; A tray

### Starter
Hold up the numeral 7 and say "This is the numeral seven. It means seven things."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each numeral and count that many counters. Children: Children count with the teacher.

Step 2. Teacher: Say each numeral name slowly. Children: Children repeat each name.

Step 3. Teacher: Hide a numeral and ask "Which one is missing?" Children: Child says the missing numeral.

Step 4. Teacher: Match numeral cards to groups. Children: Child places a card by a group.

### Practical activity: Match 6 to 10
1. Look at a numeral card.
2. Count that many counters.
3. Put the card by the group.

**Safety:** Use counters too large to swallow and supervise matching. Keep the floor clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Match numerals 6 to 8 only and use the number line.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find two numerals that add to ten.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is this numeral?
  **Expected response:** The numeral name.
- **Ask:** How many counters go here?
  **Expected response:** The same number.

### Expected outcomes
- Names numerals 6 to 10
- Matches numerals to groups

### Conclusion
Hold up each numeral and say it together.

### Home connection
Look for numerals 6 to 10 on a calendar or clock.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Tracing numerals in sand

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W04-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Tracing numerals in sand and air

### Learning objectives
- Children will trace numerals in sand using a finger.

### Success criteria
- Traces a numeral with a finger
- Names the numeral

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count objects to 10 and recognise numerals 1 to 5.

**Key vocabulary:** numeral, trace, sand, match, six, ten

**Instructional materials:** A tray of sand or flour; Numeral cards 1 to 5; A small stick

### Starter
Show a tray of sand and say "Today we draw numbers in the sand with our fingers."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to draw the numeral 1 in the sand. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Say the numeral as you draw it slowly. Children: Children copy the movement.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child trace a numeral on a card then in the sand. Children: Child traces a numeral.

Step 4. Teacher: Smooth the sand and try another numeral. Children: Child smooths and traces again.

### Practical activity: Sand numerals
1. Look at a numeral card.
2. Draw it in the sand.
3. Smooth and try again.

**Safety:** Keep sand low in a tray and wash hands afterwards. Do not let children throw sand or rub eyes.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Trace over the child’s finger with your hand.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to write two numerals without a card.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What numeral did you draw?
  **Expected response:** The numeral drawn.
- **Ask:** Where do we start?
  **Expected response:** At the top.

### Expected outcomes
- Traces numerals in sand
- Names the numeral traced

### Conclusion
Wash hands and say the numerals.

### Home connection
Trace numerals in flour or rice with your child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "trace numerals in sand using a finger" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Numerals in the air

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W04-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Tracing numerals in sand and air

### Learning objectives
- Children will make big numeral movements in the air.

### Success criteria
- Draws a numeral in the air
- Says the numeral

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count objects to 10 and recognise numerals 1 to 5.

**Key vocabulary:** numeral, trace, sand, match, six, ten

**Instructional materials:** Numeral cards 6 to 10; Ribbons or scarves; A drum

### Starter
Hold a ribbon and say "Let us draw a big number in the air."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to draw a large numeral in the air. Children: Children copy with their arms.

Step 2. Teacher: Say the numeral as you draw it. Children: Children say it with you.

Step 3. Teacher: Use ribbons to draw numerals 1 to 5. Children: Child draws with a ribbon.

Step 4. Teacher: Let children choose and draw a numeral. Children: Friends guess the numeral.

### Practical activity: Big air numerals
1. Stand with space around you.
2. Draw a numeral in the air.
3. Say its name.

**Safety:** Leave plenty of space between children and keep ribbons short. Watch that ribbons do not wrap around necks.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Draw the numeral beside the child with a large arm movement.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to draw two numerals in a row.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What numeral is this?
  **Expected response:** The numeral name.
- **Ask:** What do we use to draw?
  **Expected response:** Our arm or a ribbon.

### Expected outcomes
- Draws numerals in the air
- Names the numerals drawn

### Conclusion
Put away the ribbons and say the numerals.

### Home connection
Draw big numerals in the air with a finger at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 4 (Thursday): Playdough numerals

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W04-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Matching numerals and groups

### Learning objectives
- Children will make numerals from playdough and match them to groups.

### Success criteria
- Makes a numeral from playdough
- Puts the matching number of counters

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count objects to 10 and recognise numerals 1 to 5.

**Key vocabulary:** numeral, trace, sand, match, six, ten

**Instructional materials:** Playdough; Numeral cards; Counters

### Starter
Show a numeral made from playdough and say "I can roll and shape a number."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to roll a snake and shape it into a numeral. Children: Children roll playdough.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child make a numeral from a card. Children: Child shapes a numeral.

Step 3. Teacher: Put the matching number of counters by it. Children: Child counts and places counters.

Step 4. Teacher: Say "This is a six. Six counters." Children: Children say the numeral and count.

### Practical activity: Playdough numbers
1. Roll playdough.
2. Shape a numeral.
3. Put counters beside it.

**Safety:** Use non-toxic playdough and check for allergies. Keep playdough away from mouths and wash hands after.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Shape the numeral together with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make two numerals and compare.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What numeral did you make?
  **Expected response:** The numeral name.
- **Ask:** How many counters?
  **Expected response:** The same number.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes a numeral from playdough
- Matches it to a group

### Conclusion
Wash hands and show your numeral.

### Home connection
Make numerals with playdough or clay at home.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 4 numerals check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W04-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Matching numerals and groups

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how well each child recognises and traces numerals.

### Success criteria
- Names numerals 1 to 10
- Traces a numeral

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count objects to 10 and recognise numerals 1 to 5.

**Key vocabulary:** numeral, trace, sand, match, six, ten

**Instructional materials:** Numeral cards; Sand tray; Playdough; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the numeral song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: sand, air and playdough. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What numeral?" and watch. Children: Child names and traces numerals.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children wash hands and tidy.

### Practical activity: Numeral stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep sand and playdough away from eyes and mouths. Wash hands after.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use numerals 1 to 5.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to write numerals with a pencil.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names numerals 1 to 10; traces numerals in sand; makes numerals; matches numerals to groups.

- **Ask:** What numeral is this?
  **Expected response:** A numeral from 1 to 10.
- **Ask:** Show me eight.
  **Expected response:** Child shows the numeral.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 4
- Recognises numerals 1 to 10 with support

### Conclusion
Wash hands and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep spotting numerals in daily life.
