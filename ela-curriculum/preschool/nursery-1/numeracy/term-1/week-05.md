# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 1, Week 5: Consolidation

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W05 · **Week type:** consolidation · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Number hop and jump; Number hunt; Counting bags

## Learning objectives
- Children will play games that practise counting and numerals to 10.
- Children will show how many in a group with fingers or objects.

**Key vocabulary:** hop, jump, find, count, number, game

**Content:** Number hop and jump, number hunt, and counting bags.

**Teaching activities:** Use games to practise rather than teach new ideas; keep rules simple; let children play in pairs; watch for children who guess and not count; note children who need more practice.

**Learner activities:** Play counting games with confidence and count out a number of objects when asked.

**Resources:** Numeral cards 1 to 10; Counting bags; A hopscotch grid; Beanbags; Counters

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child counts out a number of objects when asked and plays games with confidence.

## Expected outcomes
- Plays counting games with confidence.
- Counts out a number of objects when asked.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Physical Development: hopping and throwing; Social Habits: taking turns; Literacy: following instructions

## Day 1 (Monday): Number hopscotch

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W05-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Number hop and jump

### Learning objectives
- Children will hop along a number grid and say each number.

### Success criteria
- Hops on numbers 1 to 10 in order
- Says each number

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 10, count objects and name numerals 1 to 10.

**Key vocabulary:** hop, jump, find, count, number, game

**Instructional materials:** A chalk or tape hopscotch grid; Beanbags; A bell

### Starter
Show a number grid and say "Let us play hopscotch and count as we hop."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to hop from one number to the next. Children: Children watch and count.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children take turns to hop and count. Children: Child hops and says each number.

Step 3. Teacher: Throw a beanbag on a number and say it. Children: Child throws and names the number.

Step 4. Teacher: Cheer for every child who counts. Children: Children clap and cheer.

### Practical activity: Hopscotch
1. Throw a beanbag.
2. Hop to the number.
3. Say each number.

**Safety:** Use a flat non-slip surface and supervise every hop. Keep beanbag throwing gentle and away from faces.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Walk the grid holding hands and say the numbers.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to hop in twos.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What number did your beanbag land on?
  **Expected response:** The number.
- **Ask:** What comes next?
  **Expected response:** The next number.

### Expected outcomes
- Hops along numerals in order
- Says each number

### Conclusion
Count the beanbags back into a basket.

### Home connection
Draw a hopscotch grid with chalk and play together.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "hop along a number grid and say each number" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Number hunt

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W05-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Number hunt

### Learning objectives
- Children will find numerals hidden around the room and say them.

### Success criteria
- Finds hidden numerals
- Names the numeral and counts that many

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 10, count objects and name numerals 1 to 10.

**Key vocabulary:** hop, jump, find, count, number, game

**Instructional materials:** Hidden numeral cards 1 to 10; Baskets; Counters

### Starter
Say "I have hidden numbers around the room. Can you find them?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show a sample card and explain the game. Children: Children look and nod.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children hunt for numeral cards. Children: Child finds a numeral card.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask each child to say the numeral and count out that many counters. Children: Child names the numeral and counts.

Step 4. Teacher: Put the numerals in order together. Children: Children place cards in order.

### Practical activity: Numeral hunt
1. Search for a numeral card.
2. Name the numeral.
3. Count out that many counters.

**Safety:** Hide cards at child height away from hazards and supervise the hunt. Keep the room tidy.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hunt in a small area with the child and find numerals 1 to 5.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find a numeral and write it.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What numeral did you find?
  **Expected response:** The numeral name.
- **Ask:** How many counters?
  **Expected response:** The same number.

### Expected outcomes
- Finds and names numerals
- Counts out that many objects

### Conclusion
Put the numerals in order and say them aloud.

### Home connection
Hide numeral cards at home and hunt for them.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "find numerals hidden around the room and say them" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Counting bags

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W05-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Counting bags

### Learning objectives
- Children will fill a bag with the number of objects on a card.

### Success criteria
- Counts the right number into a bag
- Checks by counting out again

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 10, count objects and name numerals 1 to 10.

**Key vocabulary:** hop, jump, find, count, number, game

**Instructional materials:** Small bags with numeral labels; Counters; A tray

### Starter
Show a bag labelled 6 and say "Put six counters inside this bag."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to count six into a bag. Children: Children watch and count.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child fill a bag to match the label. Children: Child counts into a bag.

Step 3. Teacher: Tip out the bag and check. Children: Child counts again to check.

Step 4. Teacher: Swap bags with a friend and check. Children: Child checks a friend’s bag.

### Practical activity: Fill the bag
1. Read the label.
2. Count counters into the bag.
3. Check by counting out.

**Safety:** Use large counters and supervise tipping. Keep bags away from faces.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use bags labelled 1 to 5 only.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a bag labelled 12.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many are in the bag?
  **Expected response:** The number on the label.
- **Ask:** How do we check?
  **Expected response:** Count again.

### Expected outcomes
- Counts the right number into a bag
- Checks by counting again

### Conclusion
Put the bags away and say the numbers.

### Home connection
Fill a small bag with the number of objects on a card.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "fill a bag with the number of objects on a card" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Show me with fingers

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W05-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Number hop and jump

### Learning objectives
- Children will show numbers to ten with their fingers.

### Success criteria
- Shows a number to 10 with fingers
- Says the number

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 10, count objects and name numerals 1 to 10.

**Key vocabulary:** hop, jump, find, count, number, game

**Instructional materials:** Numeral cards; A bell; A puppet

### Starter
Hold up a numeral card and say "Show me with your fingers."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the numeral 3 and make three with fingers. Children: Children copy with their fingers.

Step 2. Teacher: Call out numbers and let children show them. Children: Children show the number with fingers.

Step 3. Teacher: Use two hands to show numbers over five. Children: Child shows six to ten.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "How many fingers?" Children: Child says the number.

### Practical activity: Fingers game
1. Look at the numeral.
2. Show it with your fingers.
3. Say the number.

**Safety:** Keep the game seated and gentle so that no child is hit. Accept different finger patterns for the same number.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Show the finger pattern beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to show a number in two different ways.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many fingers is this?
  **Expected response:** The number shown.
- **Ask:** Show me seven.
  **Expected response:** The child shows seven.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows numbers with fingers
- Says the number shown

### Conclusion
Put your hands in your lap and wave.

### Home connection
Show numbers with fingers while counting at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "show numbers to ten with their fingers" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 5 number games check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T1-W05-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Counting bags

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how confidently each child plays counting games.

### Success criteria
- Counts out the number asked
- Plays games with confidence

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 10, count objects and name numerals 1 to 10.

**Key vocabulary:** hop, jump, find, count, number, game

**Instructional materials:** Counting bags; Numeral cards; Hopscotch grid; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the number song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: hopscotch, counting bags and numeral hunt. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station watch how the child counts. Children: Child counts with confidence.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Game stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use large counters only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use numbers up to five.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: counts out a number; names numerals; plays counting games; takes turns.

- **Ask:** Can you count out seven?
  **Expected response:** Counts out seven.
- **Ask:** What numeral is this?
  **Expected response:** A numeral from 1 to 10.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 5
- Plays counting games confidently

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep playing number games at home.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
