# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 2, Week 4: Numerals

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W04 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Putting numerals 1 to 10 in order; Missing numbers; Before and after

## Learning objectives
- Children will put numeral cards 1 to 10 in order.
- Children will find a missing number.

**Key vocabulary:** order, before, after, missing, next, between

**Content:** Putting numerals 1 to 10 in order, missing numbers, and before and after.

**Teaching activities:** Use number cards that can be moved; ask "What comes before?" and "What comes after?"; hide one card and ask which is missing; link order to the number line; use songs that count forward and backward.

**Learner activities:** Put numeral cards 1 to 10 in order and find a missing number.

**Resources:** Large numeral cards 1 to 10; A washing line and pegs; A floor number line; Number jigsaws; A blanket to hide cards

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child puts numerals in order and finds a missing number.

## Expected outcomes
- Orders numerals 1 to 10.
- Finds a missing number from 1 to 5.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: before and after words; Physical Development: pegging; Creative Development: number songs

## Day 1 (Monday): Putting numbers in order

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W04-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Putting numerals 1 to 10 in order

### Learning objectives
- Children will put the numeral cards 1 to 10 in order.

### Success criteria
- Puts numerals in order
- Says the numbers as they place them

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 15 and find one more and one less.

**Key vocabulary:** order, before, after, missing, next, between

**Instructional materials:** Numeral cards 1 to 10; A washing line and pegs; A mat

### Starter
Show mixed numeral cards and say "These are in a muddle. Let us put them in order."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Put the cards in order on the line one at a time. Children: Children say each number.

Step 2. Teacher: Mix them up and let children order them. Children: Child places cards in order.

Step 3. Teacher: Peg the cards on a washing line. Children: Child pegs numerals in order.

Step 4. Teacher: Check by counting along the line. Children: Children count and check.

### Practical activity: Washing line numbers
1. Take a card.
2. Peg it in the right place.
3. Count along the line.

**Safety:** Hang the line at child height and supervise pegging. Keep pegs away from mouths.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Order cards 1 to 5 first.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to order cards 1 to 15.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What comes after four?
  **Expected response:** Five.
- **Ask:** What is the first number?
  **Expected response:** One.

### Expected outcomes
- Puts numerals 1 to 10 in order
- Says the numbers as they place them

### Conclusion
Count along the line and wave.

### Home connection
Make a number line at home with cards on string.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "put the numeral cards 1 to 10 in order" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Missing numbers

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W04-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Missing numbers

### Learning objectives
- Children will find which number is missing from a line.

### Success criteria
- Finds a missing number
- Says what is missing

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 15 and find one more and one less.

**Key vocabulary:** order, before, after, missing, next, between

**Instructional materials:** Numeral cards 1 to 10 in a row; A cloth to hide a card; A puppet

### Starter
Put cards 1 to 5 in a row, hide one and say "Which number is missing?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show cards in order, hide one with a cloth and ask which is missing. Children: Children say the missing number.

Step 2. Teacher: Show the hidden card to check. Children: Children cheer.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children hide a card for a friend. Children: Child hides a card.

Step 4. Teacher: Try longer rows with one missing. Children: Child finds the missing card.

### Practical activity: What is missing?
1. Look at the cards.
2. Close your eyes.
3. Say which is missing.

**Safety:** Keep play gentle and seated. Do not allow children to peek in a way that leads to arguments.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use rows of three or four with one missing.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find two missing numbers.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which number is missing?
  **Expected response:** The missing number.
- **Ask:** How do you know?
  **Expected response:** Count along the line.

### Expected outcomes
- Finds a missing number
- Says what is missing

### Conclusion
Put the cards away and say the numbers.

### Home connection
Play what is missing with numeral cards.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "find which number is missing from a line" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Before and after

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W04-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Before and after

### Learning objectives
- Children will say the number before and after a given number.

### Success criteria
- Says the number before
- Says the number after

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 15 and find one more and one less.

**Key vocabulary:** order, before, after, missing, next, between

**Instructional materials:** Numeral cards; A floor number line; A bell

### Starter
Stand on number five and say "What number is before? What number is after?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Stand on a number and step back one to say before. Children: Children say the number before.

Step 2. Teacher: Step forward one to say after. Children: Children say the number after.

Step 3. Teacher: Call out a number and let children say before and after. Children: Child says before and after.

Step 4. Teacher: Play the game with partners. Children: Pairs ask each other.

### Practical activity: Before and after
1. Stand on a number.
2. Step back for before.
3. Step forward for after.

**Safety:** Keep the number line flat and supervise movement. Move one at a time.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Stand with the child and say numbers 1 to 5 only.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say before and after for numbers up to fifteen.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What comes before six?
  **Expected response:** Five.
- **Ask:** What comes after six?
  **Expected response:** Seven.

### Expected outcomes
- Says the number before and after
- Uses before and after correctly

### Conclusion
Walk the line and say the numbers.

### Home connection
Ask your child what comes before and after a number.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say the number before and after a given number" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Number jigsaws

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W04-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Putting numerals 1 to 10 in order

### Learning objectives
- Children will complete number jigsaws and ordering games.

### Success criteria
- Completes a number jigsaw
- Says the numbers in order

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 15 and find one more and one less.

**Key vocabulary:** order, before, after, missing, next, between

**Instructional materials:** Number jigsaws 1 to 10; A tray; A timer or song

### Starter
Show a number jigsaw and say "The pieces go in order from one to ten."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to fit the first pieces and count. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child complete a jigsaw. Children: Child fits the pieces in order.

Step 3. Teacher: Count along the finished jigsaw. Children: Child counts along.

Step 4. Teacher: Swap jigsaws with a friend. Children: Child tries a new jigsaw.

### Practical activity: Number jigsaws
1. Take a jigsaw.
2. Fit the pieces in order.
3. Count along.

**Safety:** Use large pieces with no small parts that can be swallowed. Supervise sharing.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use a jigsaw of five pieces.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a jigsaw with missing pieces for a friend.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is the first piece?
  **Expected response:** One.
- **Ask:** What is the last piece?
  **Expected response:** Ten.

### Expected outcomes
- Completes a number jigsaw
- Says the numbers in order

### Conclusion
Put the jigsaws away and count to ten.

### Home connection
Make a number jigsaw from card at home.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 4 numbers in order check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W04-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Missing numbers

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child orders numbers and finds missing ones.

### Success criteria
- Puts numerals 1 to 10 in order
- Finds a missing number

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 15 and find one more and one less.

**Key vocabulary:** order, before, after, missing, next, between

**Instructional materials:** Numeral cards; Number jigsaws; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the number song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: ordering, missing number and jigsaw. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What comes next?" and watch. Children: Child answers and places cards.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Number order stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and use large pieces. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use numbers up to five.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to order numbers to fifteen.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: orders numerals 1 to 10; finds a missing number; says before and after; completes a number jigsaw.

- **Ask:** What comes before eight?
  **Expected response:** Seven.
- **Ask:** Which is missing?
  **Expected response:** The missing number.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 4
- Orders numerals with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep playing number order games at home.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
