# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 2, Week 1: Comparing

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W01 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Which group has more; Which group has fewer; Matching one to one

## Learning objectives
- Children will say which of two groups has more or fewer.
- Children will match objects one to one to check.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, same, group, match, less

**Content:** Which group has more, which has fewer, and matching one to one to check.

**Teaching activities:** Compare groups by matching objects one to one in two rows before counting; use the words more, fewer and the same; use real objects children care about such as sweets pictures, toy cars and fruit; avoid judging by how spread out a group looks.

**Learner activities:** Say which of two groups has more or fewer and match objects one to one to check.

**Resources:** Counters in two colours; Toy cars and garages; Plates and fruit; Number cards; Two baskets

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child says which group has more or fewer and checks by matching.

## Expected outcomes
- Says which group has more.
- Matches groups one to one to check.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Social Habits: sharing fairly; Literacy: comparison words; Science: comparing amounts

## Day 1 (Monday): Which has more?

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W01-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Which group has more

### Learning objectives
- Children will say which of two groups has more.

### Success criteria
- Says more for the larger group
- Points to the group with more

**Previous knowledge:** Children have counted to 10, named numerals 1 to 10, sorted, compared size and length, and named three shapes.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, same, group, match, less

**Instructional materials:** Two plates with different numbers of counters; Pictures of two groups of fruit; A puppet

### Starter
Show two plates of counters and say "Which plate has more? Let us find out."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show two groups of counters with a big difference and ask which has more. Children: Children point to the plate with more.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "This plate has more counters." Children: Children repeat more.

Step 3. Teacher: Show pairs of groups and ask each child to choose. Children: Child points to the group with more.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "How do you know?" Children: Child says it has more or looks bigger.

### Practical activity: More or fewer plates
1. Look at two plates.
2. Point to the one with more.
3. Say the word more.

**Safety:** Use counters too large to swallow and supervise closely. Keep the floor clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Compare groups with a very clear difference.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a group with more than the teacher’s group.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which plate has more?
  **Expected response:** The plate with more counters.
- **Ask:** How do you know?
  **Expected response:** It has more.

### Expected outcomes
- Says which group has more
- Points to the larger group

### Conclusion
Put the plates away and say more.

### Home connection
Compare two groups of fruit and say which has more.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say which of two groups has more" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Which has fewer?

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W01-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Which group has fewer

### Learning objectives
- Children will say which of two groups has fewer.

### Success criteria
- Says fewer for the smaller group
- Points to the group with fewer

**Previous knowledge:** Children have counted to 10, named numerals 1 to 10, sorted, compared size and length, and named three shapes.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, same, group, match, less

**Instructional materials:** Two plates with different numbers of counters; Toy cars; A puppet

### Starter
Show two groups of toy cars and say "One group has fewer cars. Which one?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show two groups and ask which has fewer. Children: Children point to the smaller group.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "This group has fewer." Children: Children repeat fewer.

Step 3. Teacher: Compare more and fewer for different pairs. Children: Child says more or fewer.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "Which has fewer, the red or the blue?" Children: Child points and says fewer.

### Practical activity: More or fewer cars
1. Look at two groups of cars.
2. Point to fewer.
3. Say the word fewer.

**Safety:** Use large toy cars and supervise closely so cars are not thrown. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Compare groups with a very clear difference and use the words together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a group with fewer than five.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which group has fewer?
  **Expected response:** The smaller group.
- **Ask:** Which has more?
  **Expected response:** The larger group.

### Expected outcomes
- Says which group has fewer
- Uses more and fewer correctly

### Conclusion
Put the cars away and say fewer.

### Home connection
Compare two sets of toys and say which has fewer.

### Teacher reflection (private)

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Matching to check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W01-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Matching one to one

### Learning objectives
- Children will match objects one to one to find which group has more.

### Success criteria
- Matches objects in rows
- Says which row has extra

**Previous knowledge:** Children have counted to 10, named numerals 1 to 10, sorted, compared size and length, and named three shapes.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, same, group, match, less

**Instructional materials:** Counters in two colours; A line on the mat; Toy cars and garages

### Starter
Put a car in each garage and say "Does every car have a garage? Let us match."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Put red counters in a row and place blue counters below each. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "The red row has extra." Children: Children point to the extra counters.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children match cars and garages. Children: Child matches one to one.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "Are there enough garages?" Children: Child says yes or no.

### Practical activity: Match and check
1. Put counters in a row.
2. Match one counter to each.
3. Find the extra.

**Safety:** Use large counters and supervise closely. Keep rows tidy on the mat.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Match five counters with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to match ten counters and say how many extra.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Are there enough garages?
  **Expected response:** Yes or no.
- **Ask:** Which row has extra?
  **Expected response:** The longer row.

### Expected outcomes
- Matches objects one to one
- Finds which group has extra

### Conclusion
Put the counters away and say match.

### Home connection
Match cups to plates at mealtimes.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "match objects one to one to find which group has more" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?

## Day 4 (Thursday): The same number

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W01-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Matching one to one

### Learning objectives
- Children will find groups that have the same number.

### Success criteria
- Says the same number
- Makes a group the same as another

**Previous knowledge:** Children have counted to 10, named numerals 1 to 10, sorted, compared size and length, and named three shapes.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, same, group, match, less

**Instructional materials:** Counters; Plates; Number cards

### Starter
Show two plates with five counters each and say "These plates have the same number."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show two equal groups and say same. Children: Children say same.

Step 2. Teacher: Give a group and ask children to make the same. Children: Child makes a group the same.

Step 3. Teacher: Check by matching one to one. Children: Child matches to check.

Step 4. Teacher: Play the same or different game. Children: Child says same or different.

### Practical activity: Make it the same
1. Look at a group.
2. Make a group the same.
3. Check by matching.

**Safety:** Use large counters and supervise closely. Keep the floor clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Make groups of three or four with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make two groups with the same number and a third with one more.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Is it the same?
  **Expected response:** Yes or no.
- **Ask:** How do you check?
  **Expected response:** Match one to one.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes a group the same as another
- Says the word same

### Conclusion
Put the counters away and say same.

### Home connection

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 1 more and fewer check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W01-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Which group has more

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child compares groups.

### Success criteria
- Says more or fewer
- Checks by matching

**Previous knowledge:** Children have counted to 10, named numerals 1 to 10, sorted, compared size and length, and named three shapes.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, same, group, match, less

**Instructional materials:** Counters; Plates; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the counting song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: more, fewer and matching. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "Which has more?" and watch. Children: Child answers and matches.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Compare stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and use large counters. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use clear differences.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to make a group with two more.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: says more; says fewer; matches one to one; makes a group the same.

- **Ask:** Which has more?
  **Expected response:** The larger group.
- **Ask:** Which has fewer?
  **Expected response:** The smaller group.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 1
- Compares groups with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep comparing groups at home.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
