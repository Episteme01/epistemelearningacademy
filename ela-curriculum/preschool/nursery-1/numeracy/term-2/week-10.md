# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 2, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising comparing and counting; Revising joining and taking away; Revising shapes and patterns

## Learning objectives
- Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations.
- Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, join, take away, pattern, rectangle

**Content:** Revising comparing and counting, joining and taking away, and shapes and patterns, with end-of-term records.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly during play; record how far each child counts and how well they join and take away; share records with parents in plain language with a strength, a next step and a home idea.

**Learner activities:** Show learning from the term through play stations and a celebration.

**Resources:** Counters; Number lines; Toy animals; Shapes; Beads and cubes; Stickers; Class record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s Term 2 numeracy learning.

## Expected outcomes
- Completes familiar tasks with growing independence.
- Each child has an observation record for Term 2.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and rhymes; Social Habits: celebration; Creative Development: patterns

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising comparing and counting

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising comparing and counting

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit more and fewer, one more and less and counting beyond ten through games.

### Success criteria
- Says more and fewer
- Counts beyond ten

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt comparing, one more and less, counting to 15, number order, making five, joining and taking away, shapes and patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, join, take away, pattern, rectangle

**Instructional materials:** Counters; Number lines; Ten frames; Plates

### Starter
Sing the counting song and say "Today we play our favourite counting games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play more or fewer with two plates. Children: Child says more or fewer.

Step 2. Teacher: Play one more and one less on the number line. Children: Child steps forward and back.

Step 3. Teacher: Count groups beyond ten with a ten frame. Children: Child counts ten and more.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Counting games
1. Set up three game areas.
2. Children play each for five minutes.
3. The teacher notes counting.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and use large counters. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child with numbers up to five.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which has more?
  **Expected response:** The larger group.
- **Ask:** What is one more than six?
  **Expected response:** Seven.

### Expected outcomes
- Compares groups and counts beyond ten
- Finds one more and one less

### Conclusion
Count to fifteen and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep playing counting games at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit more and fewer, one more and less and counting beyond ten through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Maths stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising comparing and counting

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s Term 2 numeracy at play stations.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows counting, joining, shape and pattern knowledge

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt comparing, one more and less, counting to 15, number order, making five, joining and taking away, shapes and patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, join, take away, pattern, rectangle

**Instructional materials:** Six maths stations; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Say "Today we visit six maths stations and show what we know."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: comparing, counting, joining, taking away, shapes and patterns. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Maths stations
1. Set up six stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep floors clear and use large safe materials only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: compares groups; counts beyond ten; one more and less; joins; takes away; names five shapes; continues patterns.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite game.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a game.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a Term 2 observation record
- Shows familiar maths skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite maths game at home during the holiday.

## Day 5 (Friday): Term celebration and records

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising shapes and patterns

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate Term 2 with songs and games, and teachers will complete each child’s record.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows something they enjoyed

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt comparing, one more and less, counting to 15, number order, making five, joining and taking away, shapes and patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** more, fewer, join, take away, pattern, rectangle

**Instructional materials:** Favourite number songs; Stickers; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate our term. Let us sing our favourite number songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite number rhymes with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Give each child a sticker and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

Step 3. Teacher: Complete each child’s record and next step. Children: Children play quietly.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle.
2. Sing and clap.
3. Give out stickers.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Keep the floor clear and supervise all play.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and sing together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s Term 2 numeracy record from the term’s observations. Note the highest number counted accurately, a strength, a next step and a home idea for parents.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite number game?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** What can you count to?
  **Expected response:** The highest number.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed Term 2 record
- Enjoys the celebration

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you next term."

### Home connection
