# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 2, Week 6: Adding

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W06 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Putting two groups together; Counting all; Joining stories

## Learning objectives
- Children will join two small groups and count all.
- Children will tell a simple joining story.

**Key vocabulary:** join, together, altogether, add, plus, total

**Content:** Putting two groups together, counting all, and joining stories.

**Teaching activities:** Use real objects and stories first; model "two and three make five" in words; let children push two groups together and count all; do not introduce the plus sign until children are secure with the idea; keep totals to 5 at first and to 10 for confident children.

**Learner activities:** Join two small groups and count all, and tell a simple joining story.

**Resources:** Counters; Toy animals and a farm; Two baskets; Fruit pictures; A puppet

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child joins two groups and counts all correctly to 5.

## Expected outcomes
- Joins two groups and counts all to 5.
- Tells a joining story with objects.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: story words; Social Habits: working with a partner; Science: counting animals and plants

## Day 1 (Monday): Putting groups together

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W06-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Putting two groups together

### Learning objectives
- Children will put two small groups together and count all.

### Success criteria
- Joins two groups
- Counts all the objects

**Previous knowledge:** Children can make five in different ways, count beyond ten and find one more.

**Key vocabulary:** join, together, altogether, add, plus, total

**Instructional materials:** Counters in two colours; Two plates; A basket

### Starter
Show two on one plate and one on another and say "Let us put them together and count all."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Push two counters and one counter together and count all. Children: Children count all the counters.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "Two and one make three." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

Step 3. Teacher: Give each child two small groups to join. Children: Child joins and counts all.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "How many altogether?" Children: Child says the number.

### Practical activity: Join and count
1. Take two groups.
2. Push them together.
3. Count all the counters.

**Safety:** Use large counters and supervise closely. Keep the floor clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Join groups of one and two and count together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to join groups to make eight.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many altogether?
  **Expected response:** The total.
- **Ask:** What did we do?
  **Expected response:** We joined the groups.

### Expected outcomes
- Joins two groups
- Counts all the objects

### Conclusion
Put the counters away and say altogether.

### Home connection
Join two small groups of fruit and count all.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Counting all

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W06-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Counting all

### Learning objectives
- Children will count all the objects after two groups have been joined.

### Success criteria
- Counts all after joining
- Says the total

**Previous knowledge:** Children can make five in different ways, count beyond ten and find one more.

**Key vocabulary:** join, together, altogether, add, plus, total

**Instructional materials:** Toy animals; A farm picture; Number cards

### Starter
Put two cows and three sheep in a field and say "How many animals altogether?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show two groups of animals and count each group. Children: Children count each group.

Step 2. Teacher: Put the groups together and count all. Children: Children count all the animals.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Two and three make five altogether." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

Step 4. Teacher: Let children make their own groups to join. Children: Child joins and counts.

### Practical activity: Farm joining
1. Put animals in two groups.
2. Join the groups in a field.
3. Count all.

**Safety:** Use large toy animals and supervise closely so animals are not thrown. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Join groups of one and one then one and two.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find three groups to join.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many animals altogether?
  **Expected response:** The total.
- **Ask:** How many cows?
  **Expected response:** The number of cows.

### Expected outcomes
- Counts all after joining
- Says the total

### Conclusion
Put the animals away and say altogether.

### Home connection

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Joining stories

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W06-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Joining stories

### Learning objectives
- Children will act out and tell short joining stories.

### Success criteria
- Acts out a joining story
- Says how many now

**Previous knowledge:** Children can make five in different ways, count beyond ten and find one more.

**Key vocabulary:** join, together, altogether, add, plus, total

**Instructional materials:** A puppet; Counters; A bus made of chairs

### Starter
Tell a story: "Two children are at the gate. Two more come. How many children now?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Act out the story with toys or children. Children: Children watch and count.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "How many now?" Children: Child says the number.

Step 3. Teacher: Tell a new story and let children act it. Children: Child acts out the story.

Step 4. Teacher: Let a child tell a story for a friend to solve. Children: Child tells a story.

### Practical activity: Story acting
1. Listen to a story.
2. Act it out with objects.
3. Say how many now.

**Safety:** Use large safe objects and keep children seated on chairs for bus stories. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Act the story with one to three objects.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to tell a story with totals to ten.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many now?
  **Expected response:** The total.
- **Ask:** Did we join or take away?
  **Expected response:** Join.

### Expected outcomes
- Acts out a joining story
- Says how many now

### Conclusion
Tidy the props and say well done.

### Home connection
Tell a story about adding at bedtime.

3. Tell it to a friend.

**Safety:** Use large toys only and supervise sharing. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation

- **Ask:** What is your story?
  **Expected response:** A joining story.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 6 joining check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W06-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Counting all

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child joins groups and counts all.

### Success criteria
- Joins two groups and counts all
- Tells a joining story

**Previous knowledge:** Children can make five in different ways, count beyond ten and find one more.

**Key vocabulary:** join, together, altogether, add, plus, total

**Instructional materials:** Counters; Toy animals; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the counting song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: counters, farm and story. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "How many altogether?" Children: Child joins and answers.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Joining stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and use large objects. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use totals to three.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to join groups to make ten.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: joins two groups; counts all; acts a joining story; says the total.

- **Ask:** What is two and three?
  **Expected response:** Five.
- **Ask:** How many altogether?
  **Expected response:** The total.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 6
- Joins groups with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep joining groups at home.
