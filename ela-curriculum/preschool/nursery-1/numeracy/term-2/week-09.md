# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 2, Week 9: Patterns

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W09 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** AB patterns; AAB and ABB patterns; Making my own pattern

## Learning objectives
- Children will continue AB and AAB patterns.
- Children will make their own pattern.

**Key vocabulary:** pattern, repeat, next, colour, shape, continue

**Content:** AB patterns, AAB and ABB patterns, and making my own pattern.

**Teaching activities:** Start with body patterns (clap, stamp) and sound patterns before objects; say the pattern aloud as you build it; use colour, shape and size; ask "What comes next?"; let children make their own and explain them.

**Learner activities:** Continue AB and AAB patterns and make their own pattern.

**Resources:** Coloured cubes and bottle tops; Beads and string; Shapes; A drum; Leaves and stones

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child continues an AB pattern and makes a pattern of their own.

## Expected outcomes
- Continues an AB pattern.
- Makes an AB or AAB pattern.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Creative Development: beads and printing; Literacy: rhythm and rhyme; Science: patterns in nature

## Day 1 (Monday): Body and sound patterns

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W09-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** AB patterns

### Learning objectives
- Children will copy and continue simple AB patterns with their bodies and sounds.

### Success criteria
- Copies an AB pattern
- Continues the pattern

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name five shapes, sort and order things.

**Key vocabulary:** pattern, repeat, next, colour, shape, continue

**Instructional materials:** A drum; Clapping and stamping; A puppet

### Starter
Clap, stamp, clap, stamp and say "This is a pattern. It repeats."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Do a clap-stamp pattern and ask children to join. Children: Children copy the pattern.

Step 2. Teacher: Stop and ask "What comes next?" Children: Child says or does the next action.

Step 3. Teacher: Use the drum for a loud-soft pattern. Children: Children copy the sounds.

Step 4. Teacher: Let a child lead a pattern. Children: Child leads and friends copy.

### Practical activity: Clap and stamp
1. Listen to the pattern.
2. Copy the pattern.
3. Say what comes next.

**Safety:** Keep the floor clear and the volume gentle. Make space for stamping.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Do the pattern slowly and say each action.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a three-part pattern.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What comes next?
  **Expected response:** The next action.
- **Ask:** Does it repeat?
  **Expected response:** Yes.

### Expected outcomes
- Copies an AB pattern
- Continues the pattern

### Conclusion
Do a last pattern and wave.

### Home connection
Make clapping patterns with your child.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "copy and continue simple AB patterns with their bodies and sounds" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Colour patterns

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W09-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** AB patterns

### Learning objectives
- Children will make and continue AB patterns with coloured objects.

### Success criteria
- Makes an AB pattern
- Says the colours in order

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name five shapes, sort and order things.

**Key vocabulary:** pattern, repeat, next, colour, shape, continue

**Instructional materials:** Red and blue cubes; Bottle tops; A mat

### Starter
Put red, blue, red, blue in a row and say "What comes next?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Make an AB pattern and say it aloud. Children: Children say red, blue, red, blue.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "What comes next?" Children: Child places the next cube.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children make their own AB pattern. Children: Child makes a pattern.

Step 4. Teacher: Check by saying the pattern. Children: Child says the pattern.

### Practical activity: Cube patterns
1. Take two colours.
2. Make a repeating pattern.
3. Say it aloud.

**Safety:** Use cubes larger than a fist or too big to swallow and supervise closely. Keep the floor clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Continue a pattern that is already started.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a pattern with three colours.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What comes next?
  **Expected response:** The next colour.
- **Ask:** What is your pattern?
  **Expected response:** Colours that repeat.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes an AB pattern
- Says the colours in order

### Conclusion
Put the cubes away and say the pattern.

### Home connection
Make a pattern with socks or spoons at home.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Longer patterns

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W09-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** AAB and ABB patterns

### Learning objectives
- Children will continue AAB and ABB patterns.

### Success criteria
- Continues an AAB pattern
- Says the pattern in order

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name five shapes, sort and order things.

**Key vocabulary:** pattern, repeat, next, colour, shape, continue

**Instructional materials:** Beads or cubes in two colours; A string; A pattern card

### Starter
Put red, red, blue, red, red, blue and say "This pattern has two reds and one blue."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Make an AAB pattern and say it aloud. Children: Children say red, red, blue.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "What comes next?" Children: Child says the next colour.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children continue patterns started by the teacher. Children: Child continues a pattern.

Step 4. Teacher: Introduce ABB with a new pattern. Children: Child says the pattern.

### Practical activity: Bead patterns
1. Look at the pattern.
2. Thread beads to continue it.
3. Say it aloud.

**Safety:** Use large wooden beads and supervise stringing so beads are not put in mouths. Keep strings short.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Continue an AB pattern before trying AAB.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make an ABC pattern.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What comes next?
  **Expected response:** The next colour.
- **Ask:** How many reds in a row?
  **Expected response:** Two.

### Expected outcomes
- Continues an AAB pattern
- Says the pattern in order

### Conclusion
Put the beads away and say the pattern.

### Home connection
Make a bead pattern at home with safe large beads.

## Day 4 (Thursday): My own pattern

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W09-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Making my own pattern

### Learning objectives
- Children will make their own pattern and explain it to a friend.

### Success criteria
- Makes their own pattern
- Explains the pattern

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name five shapes, sort and order things.

**Key vocabulary:** pattern, repeat, next, colour, shape, continue

**Instructional materials:** Shapes, leaves and stones; Paper; Crayons

### Starter
Say "Today you are pattern makers. You can use shapes, leaves or colours."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show examples of patterns made with different objects. Children: Children look and talk.

Step 2. Teacher: Let each child choose objects and make a pattern. Children: Child makes a pattern.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask each child to explain their pattern. Children: Child says their pattern.

Step 4. Teacher: Draw or stick the pattern on paper. Children: Child draws the pattern.

### Practical activity: Pattern makers
1. Choose objects.
2. Make a repeating pattern.
3. Tell a friend.

**Safety:** Use clean leaves and smooth stones and wash hands afterwards. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Make the pattern together and name each object.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a pattern with two changes.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is your pattern?
  **Expected response:** The repeating objects.
- **Ask:** What comes next?
  **Expected response:** The next object.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes their own pattern
- Explains the pattern

### Conclusion
Display the patterns and say well done.

### Home connection
Look for patterns at home such as on cloth or tiles.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "make their own pattern and explain it to a friend" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 9 patterns check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T2-W09-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Making my own pattern

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child makes and continues patterns.

### Success criteria
- Continues an AB pattern
- Makes their own pattern

**Previous knowledge:** Children can name five shapes, sort and order things.

**Key vocabulary:** pattern, repeat, next, colour, shape, continue

**Instructional materials:** Cubes; Beads; Shapes; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the pattern song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: cubes, beads and shapes. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "What comes next?" Children: Child continues a pattern.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Pattern stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and use large beads and cubes. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use AB patterns.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to make an ABC pattern.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: copies a pattern; continues an AB pattern; continues an AAB pattern; makes their own pattern.

- **Ask:** What comes next?
  **Expected response:** The next item.
- **Ask:** What is your pattern?
  **Expected response:** The repeating items.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 9
- Makes and continues patterns with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep spotting patterns at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child makes and continues patterns" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
