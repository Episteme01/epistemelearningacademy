# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 3, Week 4: Ordinal numbers

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W04 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** First and last; Second and third; Race and queue games

## Learning objectives
- Children will say first, second, third and last.
- Children will describe the order of a line.

**Key vocabulary:** first, second, third, last, next, order

**Content:** First and last, second and third, and race and queue games.

**Teaching activities:** Use real lines and races where children can see who is first; say "first, second, third" aloud; distinguish counting numbers (one, two) from ordinal numbers (first, second); make sure every child gets a turn to be first.

**Learner activities:** Say first, second, third and last and describe the order of a line.

**Resources:** Toy cars for a race; A line of children; Ordinal cards: 1st, 2nd, 3rd; Ribbons or medals made of card; A start and finish line

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child says first and last and second and third with support.

## Expected outcomes
- Says first and last in a line.
- Says second and third with support.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Physical Development: running and queueing; Social Habits: taking turns; Literacy: ordinal words

## Day 1 (Monday): First and last

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W04-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** First and last

### Learning objectives
- Children will say who is first and who is last in a line.

### Success criteria
- Says first and last
- Points to the first and last child

**Previous knowledge:** Children can use time words and order numbers.

**Key vocabulary:** first, second, third, last, next, order

**Instructional materials:** A line of children; Cards: first and last; A puppet

### Starter
Make a line of children and say "Who is first? Who is last?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the first and last child in the line. Children: Children look and say first and last.

Step 2. Teacher: Change the order and ask again. Children: Child points to first and last.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child be first once. Children: Child stands at the front.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "Who is last?" Children: Child points to the last child.

### Practical activity: Line up
1. Make a line.
2. Name the first.
3. Name the last.

**Safety:** Keep the line safe and orderly and supervise movement. Make sure every child has a turn at the front.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Make a line of three children.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to organise a line of five.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is first?
  **Expected response:** The child at the front.
- **Ask:** Who is last?
  **Expected response:** The child at the end.

### Expected outcomes
- Says first and last
- Points to the first and last child

### Conclusion
Walk in a line and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Talk about who is first and last in family queues.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say who is first and who is last in a line" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Second and third

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W04-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Second and third

### Learning objectives
- Children will say second and third and describe where someone is in a line.

### Success criteria
- Says second and third
- Places an object in the correct position

**Previous knowledge:** Children can use time words and order numbers.

**Key vocabulary:** first, second, third, last, next, order

**Instructional materials:** Ordinal cards; Toy cars; A line on the mat

### Starter
Put three toy cars in a row and say "The red car is first. The blue car is second. The green car is third."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Name the position of each car in the row. Children: Children say first, second, third.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "Which car is second?" Children: Child points to the second car.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children place cars in order. Children: Child places cars.

Step 4. Teacher: Match ordinal cards to cars. Children: Child matches a card.

### Practical activity: Car order
1. Put cars in a row.
2. Say first, second, third.
3. Match the cards.

**Safety:** Use large toy cars and keep them off the floor after use. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use two cars first.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to put five cars in order and name fourth and fifth.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which car is second?
  **Expected response:** The middle car.
- **Ask:** Which car is third?
  **Expected response:** The last car in the row.

### Expected outcomes
- Says second and third
- Places objects in the correct position

### Conclusion
Put the cars away and say first, second, third.

### Home connection
Say first, second and third when queueing at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say second and third and describe where someone is in a line" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Race game

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W04-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Race and queue games

### Learning objectives
- Children will take part in a race and say the finishing order.

### Success criteria
- Takes part in a race
- Says who was first, second and third

**Previous knowledge:** Children can use time words and order numbers.

**Key vocabulary:** first, second, third, last, next, order

**Instructional materials:** A start and finish line; Card medals 1st, 2nd and 3rd; A bell

### Starter
Say "Today we have a walking race. We will see who is first, second and third."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Explain the rules: walk, do not push. Children: Children repeat walk.

Step 2. Teacher: Ring the bell and let small groups race. Children: Children walk to the finish line.

Step 3. Teacher: Name the first, second and third. Children: Children say the order.

Step 4. Teacher: Give each racer a medal or sticker. Children: Child receives a medal.

### Practical activity: Walking race
1. Stand at the start line.
2. Walk to the finish line.
3. Say who was first.

**Safety:** Make it a walking race with a clear track and no pushing. Supervise closely and make sure every child is celebrated.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Walk beside the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to be the race judge.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who was first?
  **Expected response:** The child who finished first.
- **Ask:** Who was second?
  **Expected response:** The next child.

### Expected outcomes
- Takes part in a race
- Says the finishing order

### Conclusion
Cheer for everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Have a walking race at home and say who is first.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "take part in a race and say the finishing order" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 4 (Thursday): Queue and order

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W04-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Race and queue games

### Learning objectives
- Children will describe the order of a queue and take turns in it.

### Success criteria
- Describes position in a queue
- Waits in order

**Previous knowledge:** Children can use time words and order numbers.

**Key vocabulary:** first, second, third, last, next, order

**Instructional materials:** A pretend shop or bus stop; Ordinal cards; A bell

### Starter
Say "We are waiting at the shop. Who is first in the queue?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Make a queue at the pretend shop. Children: Children line up in order.

Step 2. Teacher: Say who is first, second and third. Children: Children say the order.

Step 3. Teacher: Serve the first customer. Children: Child buys and moves away.

Step 4. Teacher: Say who is first now. Children: Child says the new first.

### Practical activity: Shop queue
1. Make a queue.
2. Say the order.
3. Serve the first.

**Safety:** Supervise the queue closely and keep the area clear. Ensure every child gets a turn.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use a queue of three.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say where they are in the queue.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Who is first?
  **Expected response:** The child at the front.
- **Ask:** Who is third?
  **Expected response:** The third child.

### Expected outcomes
- Describes position in a queue
- Waits in order

### Conclusion
Tidy the shop and say well done.

### Home connection
Talk about queues when you go out.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "describe the order of a queue and take turns in it" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 4 order check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W04-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** First and last

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child uses first, second, third and last.

### Success criteria
- Says first and last
- Says second and third with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children can use time words and order numbers.

**Key vocabulary:** first, second, third, last, next, order

**Instructional materials:** Toy cars; Ordinal cards; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the first second third song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: cars, queue and race. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "Who is second?" Children: Child answers and points.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Order stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Use large cars only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use first and last only.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to use fourth and fifth.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: says first and last; says second and third; describes a queue; takes turns in order.

- **Ask:** Who is first?
  **Expected response:** The first child.
- **Ask:** Which car is third?
  **Expected response:** The third car.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 4
- Uses ordinal words with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep using first, second and third at home.

- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child uses first, second, third and last" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
