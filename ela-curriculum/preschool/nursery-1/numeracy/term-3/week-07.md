# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 3, Week 7: Money

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W07 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Coins and notes; Buying with play money; Giving change with objects

## Learning objectives
- Children will recognise Nigerian coins and notes in play.
- Children will buy items with play money.

**Key vocabulary:** naira, coin, note, buy, sell, pay

**Content:** Coins and notes, buying with play money, and giving change with objects.

**Teaching activities:** Use play coins and notes or school-made tokens that look like Nigerian naira; never use real money with children at this age; keep prices to small numbers (1 to 10 play naira); link to market and tuck-shop experiences; respect that families have different incomes and never ask about money at home.

**Learner activities:** Name coins and notes used in play and buy one item with play money.

**Resources:** Play coins and notes; A play market stall with toy goods; Price labels; Baskets; A till or box

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child names play coins and notes and buys one item with play money.

## Expected outcomes
- Names coins and notes used in play.
- Buys one item with play money.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Social Habits: manners at the market; Literacy: market words; Understanding the World: shops and markets

## Day 1 (Monday): Coins and notes

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W07-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Coins and notes

### Learning objectives
- Children will look at play coins and notes and say that we use money to buy things.

### Success criteria
- Names a coin and a note
- Says money buys things

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 20, add and take away to 5 and read a simple graph.

**Key vocabulary:** naira, coin, note, buy, sell, pay

**Instructional materials:** Play coins and notes; A purse; Pictures of a market

### Starter
Show a play coin and say "This is a coin. We use money to buy things at the market."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show a coin and a note and say their names. Children: Children look and repeat the names.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children hold and feel the play money. Children: Children touch coins and notes.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Coins are round and notes are flat." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

Step 4. Teacher: Sort the play money into coins and notes. Children: Child sorts coins and notes.

### Practical activity: Sort the money
1. Look at the play money.
2. Feel each piece.
3. Put coins and notes in separate piles.

**Safety:** Use large play coins that cannot be swallowed and never use real money. Supervise closely and keep coins out of mouths.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Sort two kinds of play money.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what the money can buy.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we use to buy things?
  **Expected response:** Money.
- **Ask:** Is this a coin or a note?
  **Expected response:** A coin or a note.

### Expected outcomes
- Names a coin and a note
- Says money buys things

### Conclusion
Put the money away and say coin and note.

### Home connection
Talk about why we use money when you shop.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Buying with play money

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W07-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Buying with play money

### Learning objectives
- Children will buy one item at a play market using play money.

### Success criteria
- Chooses an item and pays
- Says please and thank you

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 20, add and take away to 5 and read a simple graph.

**Key vocabulary:** naira, coin, note, buy, sell, pay

**Instructional materials:** A play market stall; Toy fruit with price labels of 1 to 5; Play coins

### Starter
Set up a market stall and say "We are going shopping. Each thing costs some money."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to choose an item and read its price. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Show how to count out the coins to pay. Children: Children count along.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each child buy an item. Children: Child pays and takes the item.

Step 4. Teacher: Remind children to say please and thank you. Children: Child says please and thank you.

### Practical activity: Play market
1. Choose an item.
2. Count out the coins.
3. Pay the seller.

**Safety:** Use only play money and toy goods and supervise closely. Keep the stall area clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Buy items that cost one or two.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to buy two items and count the cost.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How much does it cost?
  **Expected response:** The price.
- **Ask:** What do you say?
  **Expected response:** Please and thank you.

### Expected outcomes
- Buys an item with play money
- Uses manners when buying

### Conclusion
Tidy the stall and say thank you.

### Home connection
Play a market game with play money at home.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Being the seller

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W07-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** practise · **Subtopic:** Buying with play money

### Learning objectives
- Children will act as the seller and take payment and give an item.

### Success criteria
- Takes payment as seller
- Counts the coins received

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 20, add and take away to 5 and read a simple graph.

**Key vocabulary:** naira, coin, note, buy, sell, pay

**Instructional materials:** A market stall; Toy goods and price labels; A till box

### Starter
Say "Now some of you are sellers. The seller says the price and takes the money."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how the seller says the price. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let seller children count the coins they receive. Children: Seller counts the coins.

Step 3. Teacher: Give the item to the buyer. Children: Seller hands over the item.

Step 4. Teacher: Swap the buyer and seller roles. Children: Child swaps and buys.

### Practical activity: Market sellers
1. Stand at the stall.
2. Say the price.
3. Count the coins.

**Safety:** Use only play money and keep the area orderly. Supervise swapping of roles.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Sell items that cost one.
- **Extension:** Ask the seller to give change with objects.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How much did you get?
  **Expected response:** The number of coins.
- **Ask:** What did you give?
  **Expected response:** The item.

### Expected outcomes
- Acts as a seller
- Counts the coins received

### Conclusion
Tidy the stall and say thank you.

### Home connection
Play shopkeeper at home with a friend.

## Day 4 (Thursday): Giving change

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W07-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Giving change with objects

### Learning objectives
- Children will use counters to show how change is given.

### Success criteria
- Shows change with counters
- Says how many are left

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 20, add and take away to 5 and read a simple graph.

**Key vocabulary:** naira, coin, note, buy, sell, pay

**Instructional materials:** Play coins as counters; A stall; Items costing 2, 3 and 4

### Starter
Say "I have five coins. The apple costs three. How many do I have left?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Pay three coins from five and count what is left. Children: Children count the change.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "That is the change." Children: Children repeat change.

Step 3. Teacher: Let children pay with more coins and find the change. Children: Child pays and counts change.

Step 4. Teacher: Check by counting all. Children: Child counts all the coins.

### Practical activity: Change with counters
1. Count your coins.
2. Pay for the item.
3. Count the change.

**Safety:** Use large play coins only and keep totals small. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use totals to three and pay with two coins.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to pay with five and get change from items costing up to four.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many coins are left?
  **Expected response:** The change.
- **Ask:** What is change?
  **Expected response:** What is left after paying.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows change with counters
- Says how many are left

### Conclusion
Put the coins away and say well done.

### Home connection
Play a pretend shop with coins and change.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 7 money check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W07-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Buying with play money

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child uses play money.

### Success criteria
- Names coins and notes
- Buys an item with play money

**Previous knowledge:** Children can count to 20, add and take away to 5 and read a simple graph.

**Key vocabulary:** naira, coin, note, buy, sell, pay

**Instructional materials:** Play money; Market stall; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the market song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: sorting money, buying and selling. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "How much?" and watch. Children: Child answers and counts.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Money stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and use play money only. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use one coin items.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to buy two items and find the change.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names coins and notes; pays for an item; acts as seller; finds change with counters.

- **Ask:** What do we use to buy?
  **Expected response:** Money.
- **Ask:** How much is it?
  **Expected response:** The price.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 7
- Uses play money with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep playing shop at home.
