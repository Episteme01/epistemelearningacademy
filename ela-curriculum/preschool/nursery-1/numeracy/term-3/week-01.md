# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 3, Week 1: Weight

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W01 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Heavy and light; Heavier and lighter; Using a balance

## Learning objectives
- Children will compare two objects by weight.
- Children will use a balance to check.

**Key vocabulary:** heavy, light, heavier, lighter, balance, weigh

**Content:** Heavy and light, heavier and lighter, and using a balance.

**Teaching activities:** Let children lift real objects and feel the difference before using a balance; teach that size does not always tell weight (a big balloon is light); use a simple pan or seesaw balance; say "the heavier side goes down".

**Learner activities:** Compare two objects by weight and use a balance to check.

**Resources:** A balance or seesaw; Stones, sponges, balloons and books; Bags of sand and cotton wool; Baskets; Pictures of heavy and light animals

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child says which of two objects is heavier and uses a balance with support.

## Expected outcomes
- Says which of two is heavier.
- Uses a balance with support.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Science: forces and materials; Physical Development: lifting and carrying; Literacy: comparing words

## Day 1 (Monday): Heavy and light

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W01-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Heavy and light

### Learning objectives
- Children will say heavy and light for different objects.

### Success criteria
- Says heavy or light correctly
- Lifts objects to feel the weight

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare size and length, count to 15, join and take away to 5 and make patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** heavy, light, heavier, lighter, balance, weigh

**Instructional materials:** A stone and a sponge; A bag of sand and a bag of cotton wool; Pictures of an elephant and a feather

### Starter
Hold a stone in one hand and a sponge in the other and say "The stone feels heavy. The sponge feels light."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Let children hold two objects and say which feels heavy. Children: Children lift and say heavy or light.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "Heavy things are hard to lift. Light things are easy." Children: Children repeat heavy and light.

Step 3. Teacher: Show pictures of an elephant and a feather. Children: Child says heavy or light.

Step 4. Teacher: Sort objects into heavy and light baskets. Children: Child sorts the objects.

### Practical activity: Heavy and light baskets
1. Pick up an object.
2. Feel its weight.
3. Put it in the heavy or light basket.

**Safety:** Use objects that children can lift easily and nothing with sharp edges. Supervise lifting and keep feet clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Compare two objects that are very different in weight.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find three objects that are heavier than a book.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Is the stone heavy or light?
  **Expected response:** Heavy.
- **Ask:** Is the feather heavy or light?
  **Expected response:** Light.

### Expected outcomes
- Says heavy and light correctly
- Sorts objects by weight

### Conclusion
Put the objects away and say heavy and light.

### Home connection
Talk about heavy and light things at home.

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Heavier and lighter

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W01-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Heavier and lighter

### Learning objectives
- Children will compare two objects and say which is heavier or lighter.

### Success criteria
- Says heavier or lighter
- Compares by lifting

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare size and length, count to 15, join and take away to 5 and make patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** heavy, light, heavier, lighter, balance, weigh

**Instructional materials:** Pairs of objects of similar size; Books and shoes; Bags of sand

### Starter
Hold two bags of sand and say "Which one is heavier? Let us lift them and feel."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show two objects and let children lift one in each hand. Children: Children lift and compare.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "Which is heavier?" Children: Child points to the heavier one.

Step 3. Teacher: Ask "Which is lighter?" Children: Child points to the lighter one.

Step 4. Teacher: Compare size and weight: a big balloon and a small stone. Children: Child says the balloon is light.

### Practical activity: Lift and compare
1. Pick two objects.
2. Lift one in each hand.
3. Say which is heavier.

**Safety:** Use objects that are safe to lift and keep them away from toes. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Compare objects with a very clear difference.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to guess before lifting.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which is heavier?
  **Expected response:** The heavier object.
- **Ask:** Which is lighter?
  **Expected response:** The lighter object.

### Expected outcomes
- Compares two objects by weight
- Says heavier and lighter

### Conclusion
Put the objects away and say heavier and lighter.

### Home connection
Compare two bags or fruits and say which is heavier.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Using a balance

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W01-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Using a balance

### Learning objectives
- Children will use a balance to check which object is heavier.

### Success criteria
- Places objects on a balance
- Says which side goes down

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare size and length, count to 15, join and take away to 5 and make patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** heavy, light, heavier, lighter, balance, weigh

**Instructional materials:** A pan balance or seesaw; Small objects such as blocks, stones and sponges; A tray

### Starter
Show a balance and say "The heavier side goes down. The lighter side goes up."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Put a stone on one side and a sponge on the other. Children: Children watch the balance move.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "The stone side goes down. The stone is heavier." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

Step 3. Teacher: Let each pair test two objects. Children: Pairs place objects and watch.

Step 4. Teacher: Say which is heavier. Children: Child says heavier or lighter.

### Practical activity: Balance test
1. Choose two objects.
2. Put one on each side.
3. See which side goes down.

**Safety:** Use a stable balance on a low table and light objects only. Supervise closely and keep fingers clear of the moving arm.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Place the objects together and watch the balance.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to find two objects that balance.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Which side went down?
  **Expected response:** The heavier side.
- **Ask:** What does that tell us?
  **Expected response:** That object is heavier.

### Expected outcomes
- Uses a balance with support
- Says which is heavier

### Conclusion
Put the balance away and say heavier.

### Home connection
Make a simple balance with a hanger and bags at home.

## Day 4 (Thursday): Heavy or light hunt

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W01-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Heavy and light

### Learning objectives
- Children will find heavy and light things and record them.

### Success criteria
- Finds a heavy and a light object
- Says which is which

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare size and length, count to 15, join and take away to 5 and make patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** heavy, light, heavier, lighter, balance, weigh

**Instructional materials:** Two baskets; Objects around the room; A clipboard

### Starter
Say "Let us find heavy things and light things around the room."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to lift a safe object and say heavy or light. Children: Children watch.

Step 2. Teacher: Let children search for objects. Children: Child finds an object and lifts it.

Step 3. Teacher: Put each object in the correct basket. Children: Child sorts the object.

Step 4. Teacher: Check the baskets by comparing a heavy and a light object. Children: Child compares two objects.

### Practical activity: Weight hunt
1. Search for objects.
2. Lift each one.
3. Put it in the heavy or light basket.

**Safety:** Only lift objects that are safe and small and never heavy furniture. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hunt in a small area with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to order three objects from lightest to heaviest.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What did you find that is heavy?
  **Expected response:** A heavy object.
- **Ask:** What did you find that is light?
  **Expected response:** A light object.

### Expected outcomes
- Finds heavy and light objects
- Sorts by weight

### Conclusion
Put the objects back and say well done.

### Home connection
Find heavy and light things at home.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 1 weight check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W01-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Using a balance

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child compares weights.

### Success criteria
- Says which is heavier
- Uses a balance with support

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare size and length, count to 15, join and take away to 5 and make patterns.

**Key vocabulary:** heavy, light, heavier, lighter, balance, weigh

**Instructional materials:** Balance; Objects; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the heavy and light song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: lifting, balance and hunt. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask "Which is heavier?" Children: Child lifts and answers.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Weight stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep the balance on a low table. Use light objects only.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and compare two objects.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to order four objects by weight.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: says heavy and light; says heavier and lighter; uses a balance; sorts by weight.

- **Ask:** Which is heavier?
  **Expected response:** The heavier object.
- **Ask:** What does the balance show?
  **Expected response:** The heavier side goes down.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 1
- Compares weights with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep comparing weights at home.
