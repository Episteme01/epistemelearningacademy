# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 3, Week 9: Consolidation

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W09 · **Week type:** consolidation · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Adding story problems; Taking away story problems; Making up our own story

## Learning objectives
- Children will solve simple number stories with objects.
- Children will make up a number story.

**Key vocabulary:** story, join, left, altogether, how many, problem

**Content:** Adding story problems, taking away story problems, and making up our own story.

**Teaching activities:** Use stories with real objects and children’s own lives (market, school, farm); read each story twice; let children act it out before solving; ask "Do we join or take away?"; keep totals to 5 and extend to 10 for confident children; celebrate different ways of solving.

**Learner activities:** Solve simple number stories with objects and make up a number story.

**Resources:** Counters and toy animals; Story cards with pictures; A puppet; A farm, market and school set; A number line

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child solves a story problem to 5 with objects and tells a simple story.

## Expected outcomes
- Solves a story problem to 5 with objects.
- Tells a simple number story.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: story language; Social Habits: working with a partner; Science: animals and plants

## Day 1 (Monday): Adding stories

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W09-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Adding story problems

### Learning objectives
- Children will solve stories where two groups join together.

### Success criteria
- Solves an adding story with objects
- Says how many altogether

**Previous knowledge:** Children can join and take away to 5, count to 20 and use money and position words.

**Key vocabulary:** story, join, left, altogether, how many, problem

**Instructional materials:** Toy animals; A farm picture; Counters

### Starter
Tell a story: "Three goats are in the field. Two more goats come. How many goats now?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Act out the story with toy goats. Children: Children watch and count.

Step 2. Teacher: Join the goats and count all. Children: Children count all the goats.

Step 3. Teacher: Say "Three and two make five." Children: Children repeat the sentence.

Step 4. Teacher: Tell a new story for children to solve. Children: Child solves with objects.

### Practical activity: Farm adding stories
1. Listen to the story.
2. Act it with toys.
3. Count all.

**Safety:** Use large toy animals and supervise sharing. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use groups of one, two and three.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to solve a story with a total of ten.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** How many goats now?
  **Expected response:** The total.
- **Ask:** Did we join or take away?
  **Expected response:** Join.

### Expected outcomes
- Solves an adding story
- Says how many altogether

### Conclusion
Put the animals away and say altogether.

### Home connection
Tell an adding story with toys at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "solve stories where two groups join together" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Join or take away?

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W09-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Adding story problems

### Learning objectives
- Children will decide whether a story is about joining or taking away.

### Success criteria
- Says join or take away
- Solves the story

**Previous knowledge:** Children can join and take away to 5, count to 20 and use money and position words.

**Key vocabulary:** story, join, left, altogether, how many, problem

**Instructional materials:** Story cards; Counters; Two baskets: join and take away

### Starter
Read two stories and ask "Which story is about joining? Which is about taking away?"

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Read a story and ask whether we join or take away. Children: Children say join or take away.

Step 2. Teacher: Solve the story with counters. Children: Child solves the story.

Step 3. Teacher: Sort story cards into the two baskets. Children: Child sorts a story card.

Step 4. Teacher: Check the answers together. Children: Children check by counting.

### Practical activity: Join or take away sort
1. Read a story card.
2. Decide join or take away.
3. Solve it with counters.

**Safety:** Use large counters and supervise closely. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use stories with picture clues.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a story card for a friend.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** Is this a joining story?
  **Expected response:** Yes or no.
- **Ask:** What is the answer?
  **Expected response:** The number.

### Expected outcomes
- Decides join or take away
- Solves the story with counters

### Conclusion
Put the cards away and say well done.

### Home connection
Make up join and take away stories at home.

## Day 4 (Thursday): Our own number stories

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W09-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** consolidate · **Subtopic:** Making up our own story

### Learning objectives
- Children will make up their own number story and solve a friend’s story.

### Success criteria
- Makes up a number story
- Solves a friend’s story

**Previous knowledge:** Children can join and take away to 5, count to 20 and use money and position words.

**Key vocabulary:** story, join, left, altogether, how many, problem

**Instructional materials:** Toys and counters; A puppet; Paper and crayons

### Starter
Say "Today you are story makers. Make a number story for your friend."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show how to make up a story with toys. Children: Children watch and listen.

Step 2. Teacher: Let pairs make a story together. Children: Pairs make a story.

Step 3. Teacher: Let pairs tell their story and ask friends to solve it. Children: Class solves the story.

Step 4. Teacher: Draw a picture of the story. Children: Child draws the story.

### Practical activity: Story makers
1. Choose toys.
2. Make a number story.
3. Ask a friend to solve it.

**Safety:** Use large toys only and supervise sharing. Keep crayons away from mouths.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Make a story together with two toys.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to write the story with numerals.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What is your story?
  **Expected response:** A number story.
- **Ask:** What is the answer?
  **Expected response:** The number.

### Expected outcomes
- Makes up a number story
- Solves a friend’s story

### Conclusion
Display the pictures and say well done.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 9 number stories check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W09-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Making up our own story

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child solves and tells number stories.

### Success criteria
- Solves a story problem to 5
- Tells a simple number story

**Previous knowledge:** Children can join and take away to 5, count to 20 and use money and position words.

**Key vocabulary:** story, join, left, altogether, how many, problem

**Instructional materials:** Toys; Counters; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the counting song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: adding, taking away and making a story. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station tell a story and watch. Children: Child solves with objects.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Story stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and use large toys. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use totals to three.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to solve a story with totals to ten.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: solves adding stories; solves taking away stories; says join or take away; makes up a story.

- **Ask:** What is three and two?
  **Expected response:** Five.
- **Ask:** What is five take away one?
  **Expected response:** Four.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 9
- Solves number stories with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep telling number stories at home.

- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?
