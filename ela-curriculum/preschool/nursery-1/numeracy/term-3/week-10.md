# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 3, Week 10: Revision and assessment

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W10 · **Week type:** revision-assessment · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Revising number and counting; Revising shape and measures; Celebration and end-of-year record

## Learning objectives
- Children will show year learning through games and a maths fair.
- Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note.

**Key vocabulary:** count, shape, pattern, measure, add, money

**Content:** Revising number and counting, shape and measures, and celebrating the year with end-of-year records and a transition note.

**Teaching activities:** Keep every activity familiar and joyful; observe quietly; record how far each child counts and how well they solve small problems; invite parents to the celebration; complete the transition note honestly for the Nursery 2 teacher.

**Learner activities:** Show learning from the year through games and a small maths fair.

**Resources:** Counters and number lines; Shapes, patterns and beads; Balance and cups; Play market; Graph; Certificates or stickers; End-of-year record sheets

**Assessment:** Observe and record each child’s end-of-year numeracy learning and complete the transition note.

## Expected outcomes
- Takes part confidently in familiar maths games.
- Each child has an end-of-year observation record.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: songs and stories; Social Habits: celebration and thanks; Creative Development: shapes and patterns

## Day 1 (Monday): Revising number and counting

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W10-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising number and counting

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit counting, numerals, more and fewer, joining and taking away through games.

### Success criteria
- Counts and names numerals
- Joins and takes away to 5

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt number, shape, measure, time, data, money, position and number stories this year.

**Key vocabulary:** count, shape, pattern, measure, add, money

**Instructional materials:** Counters; Numeral cards; Number lines; Dice

### Starter
Sing the counting song and say "Today we play our favourite number games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the dice and counters game. Children: Child throws and counts.

Step 2. Teacher: Play one more and one less on the number line. Children: Child steps forward and back.

Step 3. Teacher: Play join and take away stories. Children: Child solves with objects.

Step 4. Teacher: Let each child choose a favourite game. Children: Child chooses and plays.

### Practical activity: Number games
1. Set up three game areas.
2. Children play each for five minutes.
3. The teacher notes counting.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and use large counters. Keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child with numbers up to five.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to lead a game.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What comes after nine?
  **Expected response:** Ten.
- **Ask:** What is two and three?
  **Expected response:** Five.

### Expected outcomes
- Shows counting and number knowledge
- Joins and takes away with confidence

### Conclusion
Count to twenty and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep playing number games over the holiday.

- Which children could show "revisit counting, numerals, more and fewer, joining and taking away through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Revising shape and measures

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W10-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** revision · **Subtopic:** Revising shape and measures

### Learning objectives
- Children will revisit shapes, patterns, size, weight, capacity and time through games.

### Success criteria
- Names five shapes and makes a pattern
- Compares size, weight or capacity

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt number, shape, measure, time, data, money, position and number stories this year.

**Key vocabulary:** count, shape, pattern, measure, add, money

**Instructional materials:** Shapes; Beads; A balance; Cups and water tray; A daily timetable

### Starter
Say "Today we play shape and measuring games."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Play the feely bag with five shapes. Children: Child feels and names a shape.

Step 2. Teacher: Make a bead pattern. Children: Child threads a pattern.

Step 3. Teacher: Use the balance to compare two objects. Children: Child says which is heavier.

Step 4. Teacher: Pour water and say full or empty. Children: Child pours and says full or empty.

### Practical activity: Shape and measure games
1. Set up four areas.
2. Children play each for four minutes.
3. The teacher notes responses.

**Safety:** Supervise each area and wipe spills at once. Use large smooth shapes and beads and shallow water.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Play beside the child with two objects.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to make a pattern and compare weights.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What shape is this?
  **Expected response:** The shape name.
- **Ask:** Which is heavier?
  **Expected response:** The heavier object.

### Expected outcomes
- Names shapes and makes patterns
- Compares measures with confidence

### Conclusion
Put the materials away and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Keep looking for shapes and measuring at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "revisit shapes, patterns, size, weight, capacity and time through games" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Maths fair stations

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W10-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Revising number and counting

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will gather evidence of each child’s end-of-year numeracy at a small maths fair.

### Success criteria
- Completes familiar tasks with confidence
- Shows number, shape, measure, time and money knowledge

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt number, shape, measure, time, data, money, position and number stories this year.

**Key vocabulary:** count, shape, pattern, measure, add, money

**Instructional materials:** Six maths stations; Class record sheets; A camera or notebook

### Starter
Say "Today is our maths fair. We visit six stations and show what we know."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up six stations: counting, numerals, shapes, measures, money and stories. Children: Children rotate every four minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: Observe each child at two stations and record what they do. Children: Children play at stations.

Step 3. Teacher: Take photographs and make notes. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Maths fair
1. Set up six stations with familiar materials.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station with an adult or helper. Keep water shallow, play money large and floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and stay at familiar stations.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to show friends how a station works.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: counts to 20 and counts objects; names numerals; names five shapes; compares measures; uses play money; solves number stories.

- **Ask:** What do you like?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** Show me your favourite station.
  **Expected response:** Child chooses a station.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an end-of-year observation record
- Shows familiar skills at stations

### Conclusion
Say well done to everyone and wave goodbye.

### Home connection
Play a favourite maths game at home during the holiday.

## Day 4 (Thursday): Maths celebration

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W10-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Celebration and end-of-year record

### Learning objectives
- Children will celebrate the year with number songs and games and show their work to visitors.

### Success criteria
- Joins in songs and games
- Shows something they enjoyed to an adult

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt number, shape, measure, time, data, money, position and number stories this year.

**Key vocabulary:** count, shape, pattern, measure, add, money

**Instructional materials:** Favourite number songs; Children’s graphs, shape pictures and patterns; Certificates or stickers; Class record sheets

### Starter
Say "Today we celebrate everything we counted, measured and made. Let us sing our favourite number songs."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Sing favourite number rhymes with actions. Children: Children sing and do actions.

Step 2. Teacher: Invite children to show their work to a visitor or parent. Children: Child shows their work.

Step 3. Teacher: Give each child a sticker or certificate and say their name. Children: Child waves and says thank you.

### Practical activity: Celebration circle
1. Sit in a circle with visitors.
2. Sing and share work.
3. Hand out certificates or stickers.

**Safety:** Check for allergies before any snack and use non-toxic stickers. Supervise visitors and keep floors clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Hold the child’s hand and share their work together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to choose the last song.

### Assessment
Complete each child’s end-of-year numeracy record from the year’s observations. Note the highest number counted accurately, a strength and a next step for Nursery 2.

- **Ask:** What is your favourite thing?
  **Expected response:** Child points or names.
- **Ask:** What can you count to?
  **Expected response:** The highest number.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a completed end-of-year record
- Enjoys the celebration

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say "See you in Nursery 2."

## Day 5 (Friday): Transition note and records

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W10-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Celebration and end-of-year record

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will complete a transition note for each child for the Nursery 2 teacher.

### Success criteria
- Records what each child can do
- Records a next step

**Previous knowledge:** Children have learnt number, shape, measure, time, data, money, position and number stories this year.

**Key vocabulary:** count, shape, pattern, measure, add, money

**Instructional materials:** Transition note template; Term records; Children’s portfolios; A quiet table

### Starter
Today the children enjoy free play with familiar materials while the teacher completes the transition notes.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set out free play with blocks, shapes, a play market and number games. Children: Children play freely and happily.

Step 2. Teacher: Review each child’s three term records and portfolio. Children: Children play while the teacher reviews.

Step 3. Teacher: Complete the note: strengths, counting range, shapes, measures, problem solving and next steps. Children: Children play and talk with the helper.

### Practical activity: Free play and records
1. Set out four play areas with a helper.
2. The teacher reviews records at a quiet table.
3. The teacher completes each transition note.

**Safety:** A second adult must supervise play while the teacher completes the notes. Keep all areas safe and tidy.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Offer familiar toys and a helper nearby.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to help a friend tidy up.

### Assessment
Write for each child: how far they count; numeral recognition; use of more, fewer, one more and one less; joining and taking away; shapes and patterns; measures; interests; any support needed; and a next step. Use the four-level scale and facts you have seen.

- **Ask:** What do you like to play?
  **Expected response:** A favourite game or toy.
- **Ask:** What did you enjoy this year?
  **Expected response:** A game, number or song.

### Expected outcomes
- Has a transition note for each child
- Plays happily in familiar activities

### Conclusion
Tidy up together and say goodbye to the year.

### Home connection
Share the end-of-year report with your child and keep counting together.
