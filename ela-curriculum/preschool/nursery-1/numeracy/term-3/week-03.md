# Nursery 1 Numeracy — Term 3, Week 3: Time

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W03 · **Week type:** new-learning · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Status:** draft

**Subtopics:** Morning, afternoon and night; Days of the week; Yesterday, today and tomorrow

## Learning objectives
- Children will name parts of the day and days of the week.
- Children will use yesterday, today and tomorrow.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, today, yesterday, tomorrow

**Content:** Morning, afternoon and night, days of the week, and yesterday, today and tomorrow.

**Teaching activities:** Link time words to the school day and family routines; use a daily timetable with pictures; sing the days of the week song; use a calendar and turn a page each day; keep each idea concrete (what we did yesterday).

**Learner activities:** Name parts of the day and days of the week and say yesterday, today and tomorrow.

**Resources:** A daily picture timetable; Days of the week cards; A calendar; A sun and moon picture; Pictures of morning, afternoon and night routines

**Assessment:** Observe whether each child names parts of the day and says today and tomorrow correctly with support.

## Expected outcomes
- Names parts of the day.
- Says today and tomorrow correctly with support.

**Cross-curricular connections:** Literacy: the days song; Science: day and night; Social Habits: routines

## Day 1 (Monday): Morning, afternoon and night

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W03-D01 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** introduce · **Subtopic:** Morning, afternoon and night

### Learning objectives
- Children will name morning, afternoon and night and say what we do in each.

### Success criteria
- Names parts of the day
- Matches activities to the time of day

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare weight and capacity.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, today, yesterday, tomorrow

**Instructional materials:** Pictures: waking up, school, lunch, sleeping; A sun and a moon; A timetable

### Starter
Show a sun picture and say "In the morning we wake up. In the afternoon we play. At night we sleep."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show each picture and say the time of day. Children: Children repeat morning, afternoon or night.

Step 2. Teacher: Ask "What do we do in the morning?" Children: Child says wake up or go to school.

Step 3. Teacher: Put activity pictures under the right time. Children: Child places a picture.

Step 4. Teacher: Sing a song about the day. Children: Children sing with actions.

### Practical activity: Day pictures
1. Look at the picture.
2. Say the time of day.
3. Put it in the right place.

**Safety:** Use gentle pictures and keep the floor clear. Accept different home routines.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Sort two times of day only.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what they do in each part of the day.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do at night?
  **Expected response:** We sleep.
- **Ask:** When do we go to school?
  **Expected response:** In the morning.

### Expected outcomes
- Names parts of the day
- Matches activities to the time of day

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say good morning.

### Home connection
Talk about your family routine through the day.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "name morning, afternoon and night and say what we do in each" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

**Board or slide content:**

- NIGHT

**Picture suggestions:**

- A moon

## Day 2 (Tuesday): Days of the week

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W03-D02 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Days of the week

### Learning objectives
- Children will say the days of the week in order.

### Success criteria
- Says the days of the week with support
- Names today

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare weight and capacity.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, today, yesterday, tomorrow

**Instructional materials:** Days of the week cards; A calendar; A days song

### Starter
Sing the days of the week song with a clap for each day.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Teach the song and show each day card. Children: Children sing and point.

Step 2. Teacher: Say "Seven days make a week." Children: Children count seven days.

Step 3. Teacher: Put the day cards in order. Children: Child places a card.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "What day is it today?" Children: Child says the day.

### Practical activity: Days in order
1. Look at the day cards.
2. Put them in order.
3. Say the days.

**Safety:** Keep cards on a mat and supervise sharing. Keep the floor clear.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Use three days and say them together.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say the days of a whole week without cards.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What day is it today?
  **Expected response:** Today’s day.
- **Ask:** How many days in a week?
  **Expected response:** Seven.

### Expected outcomes
- Says the days of the week with support
- Names today

### Conclusion
Sing the song once more and wave.

### Home connection
Say the days of the week at the start of each day.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say the days of the week in order" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- MONDAY
- FRIDAY
- SUNDAY

**Picture suggestions:**
- A calendar

**Song or rhyme:** Seven days in a week, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday.

## Day 3 (Wednesday): Yesterday, today and tomorrow

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W03-D03 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** explore · **Subtopic:** Yesterday, today and tomorrow

### Learning objectives
- Children will say what happened yesterday, what happens today and what will happen tomorrow.

### Success criteria
- Says yesterday, today and tomorrow
- Matches an activity to the right word

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare weight and capacity.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, today, yesterday, tomorrow

**Instructional materials:** A calendar; Pictures of class activities; Three baskets: yesterday, today, tomorrow

### Starter
Say "Yesterday we sang. Today we count. Tomorrow we play."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Show the calendar and point to yesterday, today and tomorrow. Children: Children look and say the words.

Step 2. Teacher: Talk about what we did yesterday. Children: Child says something from yesterday.

Step 3. Teacher: Talk about what we will do tomorrow. Children: Child says something for tomorrow.

Step 4. Teacher: Sort pictures into the three baskets. Children: Child sorts a picture.

### Practical activity: Three days
1. Look at the calendar.
2. Say what we did yesterday.
3. Say what we will do tomorrow.

**Safety:** Keep the sorting seated and gentle. Accept every child’s idea.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Talk about today and tomorrow only.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what day it was yesterday.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What day is tomorrow?
  **Expected response:** The next day.
- **Ask:** What did we do yesterday?
  **Expected response:** An activity from yesterday.

### Expected outcomes
- Says yesterday, today and tomorrow
- Matches activities to the right word

### Conclusion
Say "See you tomorrow" and wave.

### Home connection
Talk about yesterday, today and tomorrow at bedtime.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "say what happened yesterday, what happens today and what will happen tomorrow" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- YESTERDAY
- TODAY
- TOMORROW

**Picture suggestions:**

## Day 4 (Thursday): Our week

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W03-D04 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** apply · **Subtopic:** Days of the week

### Learning objectives
- Children will make a picture week showing what happens on each day.

### Success criteria
- Places activities on days
- Says what happens on a day

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare weight and capacity.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, today, yesterday, tomorrow

**Instructional materials:** A large week chart with seven boxes; Activity pictures; Glue or sticky tack

### Starter
Show a blank week chart and say "Let us fill in what we do each day."

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Name each day and talk about what we do. Children: Children say an activity.

Step 2. Teacher: Place picture cards in the day boxes. Children: Child places a picture.

Step 3. Teacher: Read the chart together. Children: Children say the days and activities.

Step 4. Teacher: Ask "What do we do on Friday?" Children: Child says the activity.

### Practical activity: Week chart
1. Look at the chart.
2. Choose a picture.
3. Place it on a day.

**Safety:** Use sticky tack that is safe and keep it away from mouths. Supervise closely.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Fill in three days with the child.
- **Extension:** Ask the child to say what comes after Wednesday.

### Assessment
Observe each child during the practical activity and note Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding on the class record. Ask the questions below one to one or in a small group.

- **Ask:** What do we do on Friday?
  **Expected response:** A Friday activity.
- **Ask:** What day comes after Monday?
  **Expected response:** Tuesday.

### Expected outcomes
- Places activities on days
- Says what happens on a day

### Conclusion
Hang the chart and say the days.

### Home connection
Make a picture week at home.

## Day 5 (Friday): Week 3 time check

**ID:** ELA-PS-N1-NUM-T3-W03-D05 · **Duration:** 25 minutes · **Day type:** review-assess · **Subtopic:** Yesterday, today and tomorrow

### Learning objectives
- Teachers will observe how each child uses time words.

### Success criteria
- Names parts of the day
- Says today and tomorrow correctly

**Previous knowledge:** Children can compare weight and capacity.

**Key vocabulary:** morning, afternoon, night, today, yesterday, tomorrow

**Instructional materials:** Timetable; Calendar; Class record sheet

### Starter
Sing the days song and show three stations.

### Step-by-step lesson presentation
Step 1. Teacher: Set up three stations: parts of the day, days and yesterday today tomorrow. Children: Children rotate every five minutes.

Step 2. Teacher: At each station ask a time question and watch. Children: Child answers with a word.

Step 3. Teacher: Record each child’s level. Children: Children tidy at the signal.

### Practical activity: Time stations
1. Set up three stations.
2. Children rotate on the signal.
3. The teacher observes and records.

**Safety:** Supervise each station and keep floors clear. Accept different home routines.

### Differentiation
- **Support:** Provide an adult partner and use morning and night.
- **Extension:** Ask a confident child to say the days in order.

### Assessment
Observe and record Emerging, Developing, Secure or Exceeding for: names parts of the day; says days of the week; says yesterday, today and tomorrow; matches activities to times.

- **Ask:** What day is it today?
  **Expected response:** Today’s day.
- **Ask:** When do we sleep?
  **Expected response:** At night.

### Expected outcomes
- Has an observation record for Week 3
- Uses time words with support

### Conclusion
Wave goodbye and say well done.

### Home connection
Keep using time words at home.

### Teacher reflection (private)
- Which children could show "Teachers will observe how each child uses time words" without help, and who needs another turn tomorrow?
- What did the children say or do that I did not expect, and how will I use it in the next lesson?
- Which resource or instruction worked best, and what would I change before I teach this again?

### Learner-facing content (safe to turn into slides)
**Board or slide content:**
- Time

**Picture suggestions:**
- Photos of stations
