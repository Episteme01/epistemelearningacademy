# Nursery 1 Numeracy — annual scope and sequence

Three terms of ten teaching weeks. Five planned lessons a week (150 in the year). Dates are configured by ELA's academic calendar, not written here.

## Term 1: Term 1 builds counting and numerals to 10, sorting and matching, size and length comparisons, and the shapes circle, square and triangle

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Counting | Counting 1 to 5 with objects; Counting rhymes; Numerals 1 to 5 | Children will count objects to 5 touching each one once. Children will recognise the numerals 1 to 5. | one, two, three, four, five, count | Counts up to 5 objects accurately. Matches numerals 1 to 5 to quantities. |
| 2 | Counting | Saying numbers to 10; Number rhymes to 10; Counting steps and claps | Children will say the number names from 1 to 10 in order. Children will count steps, claps and jumps to 10. | six, seven, eight, nine, ten, number | Says numbers to 10 in order. Counts actions to 10 with support. |
| 3 | Counting | One touch for each object; Counting in a line and a circle; How many are there? | Children will touch each object once as they count to 10. Children will say the last number as how many. | how many, count, touch, all, line, group | Counts up to 10 objects with one-to-one touch. Says how many in a group. |
| 4 | Numerals | Looking at numerals 6 to 10; Tracing numerals in sand and air; Matching numerals and groups | Children will recognise the numerals 6 to 10. Children will trace numerals in sand and in the air. | numeral, trace, sand, match, six, ten | Names numerals 1 to 10 with support. Traces three or more numerals. |
| 5 | Consolidation | Number hop and jump; Number hunt; Counting bags | Children will play games that practise counting and numerals to 10. Children will show how many in a group with fingers or objects. | hop, jump, find, count, number, game | Plays counting games with confidence. Counts out a number of objects when asked. |
| 6 | Sorting | Sorting by colour and size; Matching pairs; Odd one out | Children will sort objects by colour and size. Children will find pairs and the odd one out. | sort, match, pair, same, different, odd one out | Sorts a set of objects by colour or size. Finds the odd one out with support. |
| 7 | Size | Big and small; Bigger and smaller; Ordering three things by size | Children will compare two objects by size. Children will order three objects from small to big. | big, small, bigger, smaller, biggest, smallest | Says which of two is bigger. Orders three objects by size with support. |
| 8 | Length | Long and short; Longer and shorter; Measuring with hands and steps | Children will compare two objects by length. Children will measure with hand spans or steps. | long, short, longer, shorter, longest, measure | Says which of two is longer. Measures a short object with hands or cubes. |
| 9 | Shape | Circles and squares; Triangles; Shapes around us | Children will name circle, square and triangle. Children will find these shapes in the classroom. | circle, square, triangle, round, corner, side | Names circle, square and triangle. Finds each shape in the room. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising counting and numerals; Revising sorting and measures; Revising shapes | Children will show Term 1 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 1 record. | count, numeral, sort, long, circle, ten | Completes familiar tasks with confidence at play stations. Each child has an observation record for Term 1. |

## Term 2: Term 2 builds more and fewer, one more and one less, counting beyond 10, numeral order, making 5, joining and taking away, rectangles and ovals, and patterns

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Comparing | Which group has more; Which group has fewer; Matching one to one | Children will say which of two groups has more or fewer. Children will match objects one to one to check. | more, fewer, same, group, match, less | Says which group has more. Matches groups one to one to check. |
| 2 | Counting | One more; One less; Number lines to 10 | Children will find one more and one less than a number to 5. Children will use a number line to 10. | one more, one less, add one, take one, number line, next | Says one more than a number to 5. Says one less than a number to 5. |
| 3 | Counting | Saying numbers 11 to 15; Counting groups of 11 to 15; Counting in twos | Children will say numbers to 15 in order. Children will count groups of up to 15 objects. | eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, twos | Says numbers to 15 in order. Counts up to 12 objects accurately. |
| 4 | Numerals | Putting numerals 1 to 10 in order; Missing numbers; Before and after | Children will put numeral cards 1 to 10 in order. Children will find a missing number. | order, before, after, missing, next, between | Orders numerals 1 to 10. Finds a missing number from 1 to 5. |
| 5 | Consolidation | Fingers and parts of 5; Two groups that make 5; Number bond games | Children will show 5 in different ways with fingers and objects. Children will find two groups that make 5. | five, parts, whole, make, fingers, together | Shows 5 in two different ways. Says how many more to make 5 with support. |
| 6 | Adding | Putting two groups together; Counting all; Joining stories | Children will join two small groups and count all. Children will tell a simple joining story. | join, together, altogether, add, plus, total | Joins two groups and counts all to 5. Tells a joining story with objects. |
| 7 | Subtracting | Taking some away; How many are left; Taking away stories | Children will take objects away from a group and say how many are left. Children will tell a simple taking away story. | take away, left, fewer, gone, remove, minus | Takes away from a group of up to 5. Says how many are left. |
| 8 | Shape | Rectangles; Ovals; Shape sorting | Children will name rectangle and oval. Children will sort shapes by name. | rectangle, oval, long, round, side, corner | Names rectangle and oval. Sorts five shapes by name. |
| 9 | Patterns | AB patterns; AAB and ABB patterns; Making my own pattern | Children will continue AB and AAB patterns. Children will make their own pattern. | pattern, repeat, next, colour, shape, continue | Continues an AB pattern. Makes an AB or AAB pattern. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising comparing and counting; Revising joining and taking away; Revising shapes and patterns | Children will show Term 2 learning through play stations. Teachers will gather evidence for every child’s Term 2 record. | more, fewer, join, take away, pattern, rectangle | Completes familiar tasks with growing independence. Each child has an observation record for Term 2. |

## Term 3: Term 3 builds weight and capacity, time and ordinal numbers, tallying, picture graphs, money, position, and number stories

| Week | Topic | Subtopics | Learning objectives | Key vocabulary | Expected outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Weight | Heavy and light; Heavier and lighter; Using a balance | Children will compare two objects by weight. Children will use a balance to check. | heavy, light, heavier, lighter, balance, weigh | Says which of two is heavier. Uses a balance with support. |
| 2 | Capacity | Full and empty; Half full; Pouring and filling | Children will say full, half full and empty. Children will fill and pour with cups and jugs. | full, empty, half, pour, fill, cup | Says full or empty for a container. Finds a half-full container. |
| 3 | Time | Morning, afternoon and night; Days of the week; Yesterday, today and tomorrow | Children will name parts of the day and days of the week. Children will use yesterday, today and tomorrow. | morning, afternoon, night, today, yesterday, tomorrow | Names parts of the day. Says today and tomorrow correctly with support. |
| 4 | Ordinal numbers | First and last; Second and third; Race and queue games | Children will say first, second, third and last. Children will describe the order of a line. | first, second, third, last, next, order | Says first and last in a line. Says second and third with support. |
| 5 | Consolidation | Tally marks; Counting in fives and tens; Counting games | Children will make tally marks and count them. Children will count to 20 with support. | tally, mark, five, ten, twenty, count | Makes tally marks for up to 5. Counts to 20 with support. |
| 6 | Data | Collecting information; Making a picture graph; Reading the graph | Children will collect information such as favourite fruit. Children will make and read a simple picture graph. | graph, favourite, most, least, row, picture | Adds a picture to a class graph. Says which column has most. |
| 7 | Money | Coins and notes; Buying with play money; Giving change with objects | Children will recognise Nigerian coins and notes in play. Children will buy items with play money. | naira, coin, note, buy, sell, pay | Names coins and notes used in play. Buys one item with play money. |
| 8 | Position | Over, under and next to; Left and right; Giving directions | Children will use position words. Children will follow and give simple directions. | over, under, next to, left, right, between | Places an object on, under and next to. Follows a two-step direction. |
| 9 | Consolidation | Adding story problems; Taking away story problems; Making up our own story | Children will solve simple number stories with objects. Children will make up a number story. | story, join, left, altogether, how many, problem | Solves a story problem to 5 with objects. Tells a simple number story. |
| 10 | Revision and assessment | Revising number and counting; Revising shape and measures; Celebration and end-of-year record | Children will show year learning through games and a maths fair. Teachers will complete every child’s end-of-year record and transition note. | count, shape, pattern, measure, add, money | Takes part confidently in familiar maths games. Each child has an end-of-year observation record. |
