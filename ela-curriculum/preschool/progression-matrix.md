# Preschool progression matrix: Toddler to Reception

These are ELA's **design targets**, not claims from NERDC or the EYFS. Each row increases in difficulty and independence. A reviewer should check them against the cited sources before approval.

| Competency | Toddler | Nursery 1 | Nursery 2 | Reception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Counting objects | Reliably to 3; to 5 with support | To 10 with one-to-one touch | To 20 and counts on from a number | To 30 or more; counts in 2s and 10s with support |
| Number rhymes and recitation | Rhymes to 10 | Counts aloud to 20 | Counts aloud to 30 | Counts aloud to 100 in groups |
| Numerals | Matches 1 to 5 with support | Recognises 1 to 10 | Recognises and writes 1 to 10; recognises to 20 | Reads and writes 0 to 20; places numbers on a line |
| Quantity and operations | Same, more, one more | More, fewer, one more or less | Number partners to 5; adds and subtracts within 5 with objects | Number composition to 10; adds and subtracts within 10 with objects and talk |
| Shape and space | Circle, square, triangle; in, on, under | Adds rectangle; position words and direction | Properties (sides, corners); patterns with shapes | Describes and combines 2D shapes; meets simple 3D shapes; maps and directions |
| Measures | Big/small, long/short, heavy/light, full/empty | Compares three objects; uses non-standard units | Measures with hands, blocks, cups | Measures and records with non-standard units; days, months and simple time |
| Listening and speaking | Single words and phrases; joins rhymes | Short sentences; retells with pictures | Sequenced retelling; asks questions | Extended talk; explains and reasons |
| Phonics and reading | Environmental sounds; rhymes; picture sharing | Initial sounds; first letter sounds; first blending | Systematic phonics; segments and blends CVC; first decodable books | Secure CVC and early digraphs; reads short decodable sentences |
| Writing | Mark-making and pre-writing patterns | Letter-like shapes; writes own name with help | Forms letters correctly; writes CVC words with help | Writes simple sentences from sounds |
| Science enquiry | Notices, handles, names | Observes and asks "what" | Predicts and checks; records with drawing | Plans a simple test; records with words, tallies and drawings |
| Self-care and independence | Washes hands with help; names feelings | Toilet routines; dresses with help | Dresses and tidies independently | Manages belongings and routines; helps others |
| Social and emotional | Shares with adult help; takes turns briefly | Cooperates in pairs; names own feelings | Resolves small disagreements with words | Works in groups; shows empathy; gives reasons |
| Assessment mode | Observation and play evidence only | Observation, talk and small tasks | Observation plus short practical tasks | Observation plus short oral and practical tasks; no formal exam |

Reception prepares for Primary 1 through routines, stamina for short focused tasks, early reading, writing and number sense. It does not use long periods of seated paper work.
