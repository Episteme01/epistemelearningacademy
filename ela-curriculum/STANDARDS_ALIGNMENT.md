# Standards alignment

## Method

1. **Level of claim.** ELA maps by **subject area and developmental theme**. It does not cite numbered statements from NERDC or the EYFS, because the full documents were not opened when this batch was written (see `SOURCE_REGISTER.md`).
2. **Direct alignment vs enrichment.** "Mapped" means the lesson content fits the area's theme. "ELA enrichment" means content added by ELA's academic ambition (for example earlier numeral recognition or a structured phonics sequence) that is not claimed to come from any framework.
3. **Review.** A named reviewer opens each source, ticks the check column, adds the date and edits any mapping that is wrong. Only then may a programme move from `draft` to `approved`.
4. **No approval claims.** ELA does not claim government approval, accreditation or certification.
